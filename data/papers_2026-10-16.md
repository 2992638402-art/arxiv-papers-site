## 1. Reinforcement Learning-driven Diffusion Models: Language, Vision, and Beyond

**arXiv ID:** [2610.21539v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21539v1)

**作者:** Zhiyuan Ma, Jiabao Wei, Tianyu Wang, Hanqing Wang, Jiadong Pan et al.

**分类:** cs.AI

**摘要:**

Diffusion models (DMs) have steadily demonstrated outstanding performance in high-fidelity, controllable generation across diverse scenarios, following the "pretraining-finetuning" paradigm established in LLMs. Despite these advances, conventional DMs still face critical limitations in sophisticated reasoning, autonomous exploration of generation pathways, and adaptability to complex, human-aligned objectives. Inspired by RLHF's success in LLMs, RL has recently emerged as a pivotal post-training mechanism to iteratively refine DMs through reward-driven trajectory optimization. This paper presents the first comprehensive review of RL-driven DMs and an empirical analysis of how RL reshapes the diffusion process towards experience-centric, goal-directed generation. We propose a novel technical panorama elucidating RL's multifaceted roles in diffusion-based language, multimodal, robotic, and scientific models, with a granular focus on diffusion trajectory optimization and reward engineering. Through extensive experiments on multiple task-specific benchmarks, we systematically evaluate representative methods to intuitively highlight the advantages of different RL post-training strategies. Furthermore, we reveal and dissect three crucial bottlenecks that currently hinder the real-world deployment of RL-driven DMs: reward design fragility, exploration-exploitation trade-off in high-dimensional generation spaces, and training instability under sparse rewards. Based on these perspectives, we provide a principled roadmap for the integration of RL into DMs and outline promising future research directions for robust, interpretable RL-diffusion systems. We also construct a public repository to continuously track the latest developments in this rapidly evolving field: https://github.com/MAIR-Lab-HUST/Awesome-RL-for-Diffusion-Models.

## 2. Functional Subnetwork Localization for Efficient Continual Learning in Large Language Models

**arXiv ID:** [2610.21537v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21537v1)

**作者:** Shuoran Jiang, Wentao Zhu, Yang Xiang, Dongfeng Li, Yunan Zhang et al.

**分类:** cs.AI

**摘要:**

Biological intelligence continually adapts to new information while retaining prior skills through localized plasticity. In contrast, continual learning in large language models (LLMs) remains vulnerable to catastrophic forgetting. A promising direction is to identify functionally specialized subnetworks within LLMs that enable targeted and resource-efficient adaptation. Using gradient-attribution profiling, we localize cognition-associated functional subnetworks exhibiting extremely low mutual overlap. Building on this finding, we propose Function-isolated Memory-Efficient Optimization (CiMEO), a continual learning framework that routes each training example to its corresponding functional subnetwork, restricting parameter updates and optimizer states to selected regions. Across 1.4 million sequential instructions, CiMEO achieves the highest average performance among the compared methods on five generative benchmarks spanning programming, examination-style tasks, and mathematical reasoning, while mitigating catastrophic forgetting. Subspace-overlap and principal-angle analyses further indicate that localized updates help preserve pre-trained representations, highlighting functional subnetwork localization as a practical approach to efficient and stable continual learning in LLMs.

## 3. Modeling and Understanding Neural Weights of Humanoid Policies with Diffusion

**arXiv ID:** [2610.20606v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20606v1)

**作者:** Huixuan Cheng, Bike Zhang, Shankar Sastry, Koushil Sreenath

**分类:** cs.RO, cs.AI, cs.LG

**摘要:**

Recent advances in robot learning have driven rapid progress in humanoid control, enabling increasingly agile behaviors in the real world. However, humanoid control networks are typically obtained through conventional training procedures, while comparatively little attention has been given to directly modeling the distribution of their learned weights and generating functional networks in weight space. In this work, we investigate whether high-performing humanoid control networks can be generatively modeled directly through their weights. We adopt a latent diffusion framework that couples a linear autoencoder with a Diffusion Transformer. The autoencoder compresses actor network parameters into a compact latent space, and the Diffusion Transformer models the distribution of these latents. Trained on networks collected from reinforcement learning for the Unitree G1 robot, our approach generates deployable networks that perform various motions in the real world, including walking, dancing, and spin kick. Beyond generation, we further study the learned weight space to better understand the structure captured by these humanoid control networks. We identify a structural principle in the first-layer weights that consistently reflects the robot's kinematic prior. Notably, this kinematic prior emerges under both Reinforcement Learning (RL) and Imitation Learning (IL) training, suggesting that it is not specific to a particular optimization process but may instead reflect common knowledge associated with humanoid embodiment. Together, these results demonstrate the feasibility of generative modeling in humanoid network weight space, while providing initial insights into the information encoded by their learned weights. Additional results are available on our project website: https://humanoid-weight-diffusion.github.io/

## 4. Time Window Optimization for Motor Imagery Classification via Temporal Structure Embedded Reinforcement Learning

**arXiv ID:** [2610.16921v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16921v1)

**作者:** Huan Luo, Na Lu, Xiaopeng Wang, Ziyang Shang

**分类:** cs.NE, cs.LG

**摘要:**

Online motor imagery-based brain-computer interfaces (MI-BCIs) typically decode continuous EEG using fixed-length sliding windows that may be misaligned with neural events. Existing window optimization methods often evaluate windows independently, overlooking temporal dependencies and potentially producing fragmented or physiologically implausible selections. We propose Temporal Structure Embedded Reinforcement Learning (TSE-RL), a framework that incorporates prior knowledge of MI event structure into a constrained Markov decision process for adaptive window optimization. First, TSE-RL assigns each window an explicit semantic role through Discard, Onset, Sustain, or Offset actions, extending binary retain/discard decisions to event-level alignment. Second, a finite-state machine masks invalid actions to enforce the temporal order of MI phases and promote physiologically plausible window sequences. Third, a Transformer-based history encoder captures dependencies across windows, improving training stability and computational efficiency compared with recurrent architectures. TSE-RL is plug-and-play and can be integrated with diverse MI classifiers. Experiments on multiple benchmark datasets demonstrate consistent improvements in decoding performance in both cross-session and cross-subject settings. Visualization analyses further suggest that the learned window selection policy aligns with neurophysiological principles.

## 5. Can Visual Generation Expand What Unified Multimodal Models Know?

**arXiv ID:** [2610.21459v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21459v1)

**作者:** Wenkai Li, Wenyi Hong, Zhuoyi Yang, Jie Tang

**分类:** cs.CV, cs.AI

**摘要:**

Unified multimodal models (UMMs) receive image-generation supervision that exposes them to visual information not fully documented in text. Can learning to generate images expand what pretrained models know? We introduce Knowledge Transfer Probing (KTP), a controlled protocol that supplies new knowledge through image-generation training and tests its accessibility through text-only question answering. Across seven knowledge categories and three UMMs, we observe direct but uneven transfer from visual supervision to language. Motivated by these observations, we successively introduce Textual Alignment and Interleaved Image--Text Alignment, which connect generation and answering through shared knowledge and interleaved responses, respectively. Guidance on selected entities can improve knowledge access on other entities without QA supervision, with additional gains from interleaved alignment. Further analysis shows that knowledge correctly reproduced in images is often more accessible through text, although substantial gaps remain between the two forms of access. These findings support visual generation as a source of knowledge beyond textual supervision, and motivate joint investigation of training data design and model architecture to make this knowledge more broadly accessible.

## 6. CIPHEUR: LLM-Guided Structural Search Control for Conflict-Graph Optimization

**分类:** cs.NE, cs.MA, eess.SY

**摘要:**

Conflict-graph search can stagnate despite effective operators when interventions fail to address the conflicts blocking progress. A disruptive intervention may also require further search before its value emerges. We propose CIPHEUR, a large language model (LLM)-guided framework that connects conflict evidence to coordinated intervention and recovery through Structured Intervention Units (SIUs). The shared control interface supports offline synthesis of executable policies and online contextual decisions. Together, these results demonstrate how structural search control translates LLM design knowledge and contextual reasoning into improved solution quality and sustained search progress.

## 7. Hindsight-Privileged RLVR: Single-Rollout Token Credit Without Group Sampling

**arXiv ID:** [2610.20497v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20497v1)

**作者:** Wei Chen, Yubing Wu, Zhou Xu, Haoyue Zhang, Delu Zeng et al.

**分类:** cs.LG, cs.AI

**摘要:**

Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) trains language models from checked task outcomes, but assigning a terminal reward to individual reasoning tokens or actions remains difficult. Group-relative methods such as Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) sample several attempts per prompt, but lose their reward-based learning signal when all attempts receive the same outcome. We propose Hindsight-Privileged RLVR (HP-RLVR), a single-rollout method that turns a completed trajectory's own future into token-level update weights. The generating policy snapshot scores each eligible token under its causal history and under a hindsight context containing the realized future and verifier feedback. A normalized Jensen--Shannon divergence between the two views weights the reward relative to a historical baseline, yielding bounded token credit for both successful and failed attempts, with no reference solution or learned critic. In paired Qwen3-8B runs over a shared $100$-step schedule, HP-RLVR trains $1.21\times$, $1.46\times$, and $2.30\times$ faster than GRPO on mathematics, code generation, and ALFWorld, using one eighth the rollouts per prompt. Accuracy is comparable overall, with HP-RLVR ahead on mathematics at all three sizes and GRPO ahead on code and ALFWorld. In ALFWorld, $43.2\%$ (4B) and $49.0\%$ (8B) of GRPO's gradient-norm records are zero, versus none for HP-RLVR.

## 8. Perception or Reasoning? Diagnosing and Tackling Reinforcement Learning Bottlenecks in VLMs

**arXiv ID:** [2610.21380v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21380v1)

**作者:** Zeyuan Yang, Zhuohao Chen, Jiaben Chen, Yao Zhang, Maohao Shen et al.

**分类:** cs.CV

**摘要:**

Reinforcement learning has brought strong gains to language model reasoning, but its improvements on visual tasks remain less consistent. One difficulty is that visual task failures can arise from different sources: the same benchmark may be limited by perception for one model and by reasoning for another. We introduce Perception and Reasoning Attribution (PARA), which measures the remaining perceptual and reasoning headroom of each model--benchmark pair by comparing how many current errors are recovered with more accurate visual information versus a stronger reasoner. Across 4 vision-language models and 17 multimodal benchmarks, PARA broadly agrees with the expected characteristics of existing benchmarks while revealing substantial model-specific variation. More importantly, it shows that RL gains become smaller as perceptual headroom increases, but larger when reasoning explains more of the recoverable errors, suggesting that current RL exploits reasoning headroom more effectively than perceptual headroom. Motivated by this gap, we analyze how existing RL objectives affect visual perception and find that reward- and token-level signals influence visual attention only indirectly. We therefore propose GRAD, a group-relative attention distillation objective that uses the best rollout in each group to directly guide the visual attention of weaker rollouts. GRAD consistently improves performance across two model scales and seven benchmarks, and further complements existing RL methods.

## 10. MYOPIA: Understanding the Limits of Tree-Based Reasoning in Vision-Language Models

**arXiv ID:** [2610.21573v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21573v1)

**作者:** Islam Mesabah, Sary Elmansoury, Gerrit Großmann, Peter Neigel, Sebastian J. Vollmer

**分类:** cs.CV

**摘要:**

Decision trees can make vision-language model (VLM) classification interpretable by replacing a single prediction with a sequence of root-to-leaf questions. However, the accuracy cost of this greedy, non-backtracking procedure remains unclear. We introduce \textbf{MYOPIA}, a benchmark comprising four LLM-generated, human-refined decision trees for GTSRB, MNIST, CIFAR-10, and MSCOCO, together with matched prompts, diagnostic protocols, and an evaluation harness. Across GPT-4o, Qwen-VL Max, and LLaMA-3.2 11B Vision, tree traversal underperforms zero-shot prompting in all 12 dataset--model pairs; paired bootstrap analyses consistently favor zero-shot. Our diagnostics characterize interacting failure modes associated with this gap. Models often recall tree-encoded class attributes more accurately than they ground the same attributes in images: the mean label--image accuracy gaps are 9.5, 13.8, and 12.6 points for GPT-4o, Qwen-VL Max, and LLaMA, respectively. Incorrect local decisions then irreversibly exclude the correct class, while answer extraction introduces additional model-dependent loss. A node-level audit of the four trees finds that split entropy is weakly but consistently associated with lower node accuracy, whereas depth, branching factor, and within-node grounding differences are less stable. \textbf{MYOPIA} therefore provides a framework for diagnosing hierarchical visual classification and motivates testing tree designs that prioritize perceptual answerability and recovery from early errors. Code is available at https://github.com/islammesabah/VLM_Classification.

## 11. PrismaCoder: Pushing the Boundaries of Open Multimodal Code Intelligence through Decomposed Rubric Synthesis

**分类:** cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.CV, cs.LG, cs.SE

**摘要:**

Multimodal code intelligence connects the symbolic world of programs with the visual world their execution creates. Interest in this capability is burgeoning, as models are increasingly asked to produce charts, webpages, animations, and semi-structured documents. Recently, proprietary models have pushed far ahead, producing interactive artifacts even from a text description or a screenshot alone. Yet their data and training remain closed, and open progress is held back by the quantity and quality of available data and by the pipelines and methods for producing it. On the model side, we train PrismaCoder-9B and PrismaCoder-35B on this data and release both. They lead the open state of the art by a wide margin and rival leading proprietary systems. Our analyses further show that the proposed rubric-based pipeline improves multimodal coding performance across model backbones. We release our code and data at https://qiushisun.github.io/PrismaCoder-Home/.

## 13. CarryOn: A Dual-Loop Harness for Personalized and Reliable Robot Assistance

**arXiv ID:** [2610.21453v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21453v1)

**作者:** Zixuan Wang, Yuqi Liu, Yuxin Chen, Sengqiao Yang, Jinhui Ye et al.

**分类:** cs.RO, cs.CV

**摘要:**

While generalist vision-language-action (VLA) models have enabled diverse open-world manipulation, real-world robotic assistance requires two critical capabilities they currently lack: personalization and reliable execution. First, executing an instruction like "make me a sandwich" requires grounding subtask decomposition in persistent user preferences rather than relying on a single generic solution. Second, reliable deployment demands explainable safety assurance that proactively prevents hazards and gracefully recovers from occasional physical failures. ## 15. LTM: Latent Transition Based In-Context Memory for Vision-Language-Action Models

## 18. ManiFusion: Unlocking the High-Throughput Generation via Superposition in Manifold Space

**arXiv ID:** [2610.21433v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21433v1)

**作者:** Minkyu Kim, Baekseung Kim, Junhoo Lee, Jangho Kim, Nojun Kwak

**分类:** cs.CV, cs.LG

**摘要:**

Diffusion models achieve high-quality image generation but remain computationally expensive due to iterative denoising across many timesteps. Existing acceleration methods mainly reduce per-sample latency, but gains along this axis are becoming saturated. We instead explore an orthogonal throughput-oriented direction by processing multiple samples within a single denoising evaluation. To this end, we propose ManiFusion, a backbone-agnostic framework for multi-sample generation through fused-space computation. ManiFusion jointly processes multiple samples by fusing them before the denoising backbone and separating them afterward through unfusion. ManiFusion generalizes across diffusion families, including both U-Net and transformer backbones in pixel and latent spaces. We further show that ManiFusion composes with existing acceleration methods and reaches throughput regimes unattainable by prior approaches with minimal quality degradation.

## 19. Compute-Optimal Scaling Laws for Spiking Language Models

**arXiv ID:** [2610.15146v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.15146v1)

**作者:** Dan Saunders, Benjamin Ren, Catherine He

**分类:** cs.LG, cs.CL, cs.NE

**摘要:**

Spiking neural networks target energy-efficient language modeling through sparse, binary activations, but the efficiency-accuracy tradeoff of spiking language models is uncharacterized at scale. We present the first Chinchilla-style IsoFLOP scaling study of a spiking language model, sweeping SpikeGPT-family models (RWKV backbone) from width 384 to 1536 across compute budgets from $3\times10^{17}$ to $10^{19}$ FLOPs on FineWeb-EDU. We report two findings. First, the compute-optimal frontier is $N_{opt} \propto C^{0.56}$, close to the 0.50 that Hoffmann et al. report for dense Transformers, so spiking does not change how parameters and data should be traded off under a compute budget. Second, against non-spiking RWKV and Transformer baselines trained the same way, the loss gap splits into a backbone cost of about 0.16 nats that does not change with compute and a spiking cost that falls from 0.34 to about 0.23 nats and then levels off. These results give the first compute-optimal recipe for spiking language models and quantify the accuracy cost of their sparsity.
