## 1. T2-z: Test-Time Optimization of Environment Latents for Robotic World Models

**arXiv ID:** [2610.22364v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22364v1)

**作者:** Chenyuan Zhou, Yining Hong, Huang Huang, Yunfan Jiang, Wenlong Huang et al.

**分类:** cs.RO, cs.AI, cs.CV

**摘要:**

Predicting how the world responds to an action requires more than what can be seen: robots must account for environmental factors that are neither directly observable nor explicitly specified, from physical properties such as friction and mass to human-defined environmental rules. We introduce T2-Z for test-time optimization of environment latents, which augments a robotic world model with a learnable environment latent to condition world predictions. This compact latent captures underlying environmental variation without supervision of what environmental factors exist or what values they take. During training, T2-Z alternates between two stages: a world-model optimization stage that fixes the latents and learns general action-conditioned dynamics across environments; and an environment-latent optimization stage that freezes the world model and optimizes the conditioning latents, allowing latent structure to emerge purely through world prediction. At test time, the world model remains frozen and a newly initialized environment latent is optimized from one or more test-time interactions, adapting the model to previously unseen environmental configurations. Across 7 simulated settings and 4 real-world robotic tasks, T2-Z reduces object prediction error metric from 15.20 to 7.00 and raises action success from 33.73% to 72.96%. Its emergent latents capture structured representations of individual and compounded factors across diverse visual scenes. Ablations further show that T2-Z can efficiently infer environment latents from as little as a single test-time interaction trajectory.

## 2. Routing Policy Optimization for Online Diffusion Reinforcement Learning

**arXiv ID:** [2610.22282v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22282v1)

**作者:** Qiuhong Shen, Yiding Yang, Yuxin Zhang, Xinwei Huang, Minxuan Lin et al.

**分类:** cs.CV

**摘要:**

Reinforcement learning can align visual generators with reward signals beyond their pretraining objectives, but existing score-function methods typically introduce stochasticity by perturbing the reverse denoising trajectory, leading to costly rollout-based optimization. We instead ask whether stochastic exploration can be introduced inside the generator while preserving its original sampler. Routing Policy Optimization (RPO) answers this question by turning attention routing into a stochastic exploration action. At a few selected denoising levels, each query block keeps its own block and samples further key blocks in proportion to the generator's native attention mass, or keeps dense attention. The sampled route perturbs the velocity of that transition, and a Gaussian likelihood around the dense velocity scores the perturbation with a score-function objective. RPO further uses a controlled two-rollout estimator: two rollouts share the same prompt, initial latent, routed denoising levels, and dense anchor layers, differing only in their sampled attention routes. Their reward difference provides an advantage signal for the routed transitions. Because the likelihood differentiates the dense velocity itself, the learned updates remain effective after routing is removed. At inference, RPO restores dense attention and retains the original deterministic sampler. Experiments on text-to-image and text-to-video generation show that RPO improves generation quality while achieving nearly 4x higher training efficiency than Flow-GRPO-style methods. ## 3. Which Tokens to Merge? Diffusion Dynamics for Efficient Image Generation

**arXiv ID:** [2610.22370v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22370v1)

**作者:** SeungJu Cha, Ye-Chan Kim, HyunGee Kim, Sungho Koh, Dong-Jin Kim

**分类:** cs.CV

**摘要:**

Token merging has emerged as an effective training-free strategy for accelerating diffusion models by reducing redundant token computation while minimizing generation degradation. A key challenge is determining which tokens can be safely merged and which should be preserved for subsequent computation. Existing method addresses this by preserving prompt-aligned foreground regions, thereby protecting semantically relevant structures. However, this text-relevance criterion overlooks background objects, local details that are not explicitly mentioned in the prompt, leading to degraded structural coherence. In this work, we propose Dynamics-aware Token Merging (DaTMe), a training-free framework that grounds token merging in the underlying diffusion dynamics. Our key insight is that token importance should reflect whether a token remains unresolved during denoising and whether it carries structural information. To this end, DaTMe constructs a dynamics- aware importance map from the Tweedie denoised estimate. The map combines a temporal signal, measuring inter-step changes in the predicted clean image, with a structural signal, capturing local high-frequency content such as boundaries and fine details. Using this map, DaTMe adaptively assigns tokens into merging roles under a given compression ratio: important tokens are preserved as anchors, while tokens that are both temporally stabilized and structurally homogeneous are selected as safe merge candidates. Experiments on PixArt-a and FLUX demonstrate that DaTMe maintains visual fidelity across the entire image, highlighting diffusion dynamics as an effective token-level merging decision in efficient generation. Our code is available at https://github.com/SeungJuCha/DaTMe.

## 4. Designing Transformers for Continuous Diffusion Language Models

**作者:** Weijian Luo, Yujian Chen, Hao Jiang, Guang Cheng, Tianyang Hu

**分类:** cs.AI

**摘要:**

Continuous diffusion language models (CDLMs) offer an alternative approach to parallel language generation; however, most existing studies directly use transformer architectures from either causal large language models (LLMs) or Diffusion Transformers (DiTs) for image generation, which were not optimized for CDLMs. In this paper, we conduct a systematic study on Transformer architectures for CDLMs and introduce Language Diffusion Transformer (LangDiT), combining feature and query-key normalization, wider noise conditioning, likelihood-shortcut gradient routing and calibration, and confidence-gated training self-conditioning. LangDiT significantly reduces generation perplexity in matched LangFlow comparisons, establishing a new state of the art in continuous language generation across different model formulations. When adapted to Plaid-style CDLM formulations, LangDiT results in a new leading GenPPL of 11.33 using 1024 denoising NFEs, and 27.51 GenPPL using 128 denoising NFEs. These gains are achieved while retaining each model's underlying representation, training objective, and inference sampler, isolating transformer architecture as a fundamental source of improvement. Our study identifies architectural design as an important source of improvement in continuous diffusion language models.

## 5. SCOPE: Semantic Bounded Credit Optimization for Prompt Engineering in Text-to-image Generation

**作者:** Yufei Jiao, Jinfa Huang, Zeyue Xue, Tianyu Xie, Yuexiao Ma et al.

**分类:** cs.CV

**摘要:**

Reinforcement learning (RL) enables language models to improve text-to-image (T2I) generation by rewriting prompts for a frozen image generator. Existing methods typically apply a single signal derived from the image to individual subword tokens, although objects, counts, attributes, and relations form coherent semantic spans. The rewriter jointly generates an enhanced prompt and its semantic partition. Ablation studies show that both semantic boundaries and pooling within spans contribute to these gains, supporting semantic structure as a practical inductive bias for prompt engineering with RL.

## 8. Grid-Bench: Can LLMs Predict How Discrete Worlds Change?

**arXiv ID:** [2610.22312v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22312v1)

**作者:** Zixiang Xu, Zirui Song, Wanrong Zheng, Chiyu Zhang, Yue Huang et al.

**分类:** cs.CL

**摘要:**

Spatial instructions describe how to move objects, transform regions, or modify their surroundings. When several operations are applied in sequence, each acts on a configuration shaped by earlier changes. How accurately can large language models (LLMs) predict the resulting spatial state? We introduce Grid-Bench, an executable benchmark in which eight families of operations transform discrete worlds represented as two-, three-, and four-dimensional grids. Given an initial grid, explicit rules, and an event history expressed as text, a model predicts the complete final grid. Models with similar exact-match accuracy can nevertheless differ substantially in the number of incorrect cells on shared instances. Even over long histories, some models recover many correct changes beyond what copying the initial state would yield. Grid-Bench makes both complete recovery and these differences in returned states directly measurable across a range of spatial conditions.

## 10. Provably Efficient Risk-Sensitive Offline Reinforcement Learning via Optimized Certainty Equivalents

**arXiv ID:** [2610.21870v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21870v1)

**作者:** Hanzhen Wang, Boxiang Lyu, Zhongxiang Dai, Siyang Gao, Dake Zhang et al.

**分类:** cs.LG, cs.AI, math.OC, stat.ML

**摘要:**

Risk-sensitive reinforcement learning (RL) addresses decision-making under uncertainty and adverse outcomes. Learning risk-sensitive policies from fixed datasets is important when additional interaction with the environment is costly. Existing work on risk-sensitive RL focuses on online settings or specific risk measures, leaving a general theoretical understanding of its offline setting underdeveloped. In this work, we investigate efficient offline risk-sensitive RL using the optimized certainty equivalent (OCE), a general class of risk measures. We consider two formulations: dynamic-OCE RL, which applies OCE recursively at each step, and static-OCE RL, which applies a single OCE to the cumulative rewards. To handle large state spaces, we further develop pessimistic least-squares value-iteration algorithms that incorporate the general OCE risk criterion into linear function approximation. We establish utility-dependent sublinear guarantees with explicit dependence on the feature dimension $d$. To our knowledge, we provide the first offline algorithms along with the first upper and lower suboptimality bounds for both dynamic- and static-OCE RL.

## 12. Query-Efficient Rejection Sampling for Parallel Masked Diffusion

**arXiv ID:** [2610.22238v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22238v1)

**作者:** Shiyuan Zhang, Xuheng Li, Qiwei Di, Quanquan Gu

**分类:** cs.LG, math.PR, stat.ML

**摘要:**

Masked diffusion models (MDMs) accelerate generation by unmasking multiple tokens in parallel, but sampling these tokens independently ignores their correlations and biases the output. Anari et al. (2026) corrected this bias with rejection sampling, decomposing the log acceptance probability into a sum of token-level terms, but the evaluation requires one score query per unmasked token. We propose the Single-Increment Rejection Sampler (SIRS), which provides an estimate of the log acceptance probability from a single randomly sampled increment at the cost of one query. SIRS then converts a Poisson number of clipped estimates into accept/reject decisions through a Bernoulli factory adapted from Chen et al. (2026). Since each query returns the conditionals of all remaining tokens in the block, we further Rao-Blackwellize each increment by averaging over these tokens at no extra cost, thereby reducing clipping. We show that the KL-divergence between the data distribution and the SIRS output is bounded by a clipping term plus the model's training error. With exact scores, parallel decoding pays the full within-block dependence, whereas SIRS pays only the part beyond the clipping range.

## 18. SoftCache: Adaptive Step Size Caching for Diffusion Transformers

**arXiv ID:** [2610.22369v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22369v1)

**作者:** Shimon Malnick, Nadav Orenstein, Ohad Fried, Shai Avidan

**分类:** cs.CV

**摘要:**

Diffusion and flow models generate a sample over iterative steps, each a full pass through a large network, and that pass dominates generation cost. Training-free caching accelerates them by reusing activations from recent evaluations instead of recomputing them. Dynamic caching methods decide when to refresh by accumulating a cheap probe of the network input into a staleness signal and spending a fresh evaluation once it crosses a threshold. They perform hard caching: the signal is a binary switch between refresh and reuse, and every step, fresh or cached, advances by the prescribed step size. We observe that the signal carries much more than this switch. How far it sits below the threshold, its headroom, predicts the error induced by reuse. We propose SoftCache, which performs soft caching: instead of a binary switch, the headroom sets how far each cached step goes. Where the headroom is large, the sampler jumps past several scheduled nodes, and as a refresh nears, its steps shrink back toward the original step. These adaptive jumps may land between the scheduled nodes, taking the sampler off its fixed grid. SoftCache requires no training and adds no per-backbone settings: on FLUX for images and on HunyuanVideo and Wan2.1 for video, at speedups from $3.5\times$ to $6.9\times$, it is ahead of every baseline on all four fidelity metrics.

## 19. SUPRA-TEX: 3D Generation with Super-Aligned Texture

**作者:** Chen Yang, Jiemin Fang, Fanpeng Meng, Zihao Huang, Jichen Pang et al.

**分类:** cs.CV, cs.GR

**摘要:**

Single image 3D generation aims to dress a given 3D mesh with relightable materials while faithfully preserving the visual details of a reference image. Existing methods either introduce artifacts when fusing inconsistent multi-view predictions or struggle to preserve fine details through implicit image-feature conditioning. To resolve these challenges, we introduce SUPRA-Tex, built on the principle of decoupling visible-material estimation, surface alignment, and observation-guided 3D completion. This division of labor allows the 3D generator, trained on limited 3D data, to focus its capacity on preserving visible evidence and completing unobserved surfaces.
