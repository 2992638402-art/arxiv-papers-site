## 1. Frontier LLMs Enter the World of Robotics: A Cross-Model Evaluation on RoboColiseum

**arXiv ID:** [2610.27865v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27865v1)

**作者:** Da Huang, Jichao Wang, Junhui Wu, Tong Ran, Linting Guo et al.

**分类:** cs.RO

**摘要:**

Recent studies have explored the potential of multimodal large language models for robot control, yet the models' embodied capabilities remain insufficiently characterized across models, tasks, and execution settings. We conduct a multidimensional comparative evaluation on RoboColiseum across four tracks: Instruction Following, Spatial Reasoning, General Manipulation, and Robustness. We implement Agent2Act, a control harness that integrates GPT-6 Astra, GPT-6.1 Sol, Claude Opus 5.5, and Gemini 4 Argon into the evaluation environment through shared observation, action execution, and feedback interfaces. Under common task settings and scoring criteria, all four models exhibit a consistent capability profile: high instruction-following scores, with three approaching perfect performance; strong spatial reasoning; and general manipulation scores below those of the other three tracks. Within the evaluated configurations, Astra leads instruction following and robustness, while Opus leads spatial reasoning, general manipulation, and overall performance. These results reveal complementary strengths across models and a persistent gap between task understanding and reliable physical execution. Component comparisons on selected tasks further show that harness design affects task success, planning cost, and execution time. Additionally, case studies with Astra examine whole-body coordination, manipulation across robot embodiments, precision manipulation, dexterous-hand manipulation, and real-robot tasks, providing further insight into the model's capabilities and limitations across embodied tasks.

## 2. RoboJudge: Multimodal Language Models as Judges for Embodied Video Generation

**arXiv ID:** [2610.27782v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27782v1)

**作者:** Siyuan Ma, Yu Qi, Cheng Ye, Ruotian Peng, Xinyi Xu et al.

**分类:** cs.CV, cs.RO

**摘要:**

Video generation models are promising foundations for embodied world models, but only if their generated rollouts are physically valid and faithful to task instructions. Multimodal large language models (MLLMs) are increasingly used to judge these properties, yet their agreement with human judgments on embodied videos has not been systematically evaluated. To address this gap, we introduce RoboJudge-Bench, a benchmark for evaluating MLLMs as judges of embodied video generation. The benchmark measures two fundamental dimensions, Physical Adherence and Instruction Alignment, across 800 human-annotated videos from 8 source corpora and 10 video generators, spanning grippers, human hands, and dexterous hands. Experiments reveal uneven alignment between existing judges and human ratings: general-purpose MLLMs show lower agreement on physical adherence, while specialized video judges designed for general-domain physical reasoning do not consistently transfer to embodied scenarios. Motivated by these findings, we construct RoboJudge-Train, comprising 12K videos annotated with 25K human-verified scores and model-assisted rationales for Physical Adherence and Instruction Alignment. We fine-tune a 9B MLLM on this dataset with supervised learning followed by reinforcement learning, yielding RoboJudge-9B. Among the model judges evaluated on RoboJudge-Bench, RoboJudge-9B achieves the highest overall agreement, reaching a Pearson correlation of 0.719 with a statistically significant 0.089 gain over Gemini-3.7-Flash, the best-performing proprietary baseline in our evaluation. Training on RoboJudge-Train also yields gains on external benchmarks, including VideoPhy2 and Physion-Eval. We further use RoboJudge-9B as a downstream evaluator to benchmark video generation models according to Physical Adherence and Instruction Alignment.

## 3. Learning Who Talks to Whom: Role-Aware Communication Topology Scheduling for Efficient LLM Multi-Agent Collaboration

**arXiv ID:** [2610.25851v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25851v1)

**作者:** Haibin Li, Wenya Yu, Xuefeng Jiang, Jia Li, Xiao Han et al.

**分类:** cs.MA

**摘要:**

Multi-agent systems driven by large language models (LLMs) rely on structured communication topologies to facilitate information exchange and collaborative reasoning. However, effectively scheduling these topologies to dynamically determine who talks to whom and when to communicate remains a fundamental challenge in balancing collective reasoning performance against exponential token overhead. Existing topology scheduling approaches either enforce static communication graphs that cannot adapt to dynamic problem-solving states, or overlook agent role complementarity while failing to explicitly incorporate the synthesized graph structure into team-level decision evaluation. To tackle these limitations, we propose ACTS, a role-aware adaptive communication topology scheduling framework for efficient LLM multi-agent collaboration. Orchestrated within a Centralized Training with Decentralized Execution (CTDE) paradigm, ACTS introduces a novel dual-attention mechanism. Specifically, a Role-conditioned Relational Attention module dynamically selects variable-cardinality interaction partners based on agent roles and evolving execution states, while a Topological Attention Mixer explicitly embeds the directed communication topology into team value decomposition for precise credit assignment. Comprehensive evaluations across 9 challenging benchmarks covering code generation, multi-step reasoning, general knowledge, and competitive mathematics demonstrate that ACTS consistently outperforms state-of-the-art baselines, boosting average accuracy by up to 6.19 percentage points while reducing token consumption by over 15%. The project website is available at https://acts-mas.github.io/ACTS/.

## 4. LaLa-World: Scaling Latent World Models with Natural Language Actions

**arXiv ID:** [2610.27851v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27851v1)

**作者:** Junha Song, Geonmo Gu, Jeongeun Park, Heejeong Nam, Lucas Maes et al.

**分类:** cs.CV, cs.LG

**摘要:**

This paper shows that JEPA-style latent world models (LWMs) can learn across diverse domains and data sources by adopting the language-conditioned interface of video generation models. We establish this approach as an effective and efficient basis for planning with imagination and downstream policy fine-tuning. To this end, we introduce LaLa-World, a Language-conditioned Latent World model that learns from simulation and robot trajectories together with broader image and video data. Two designs make this work. First, we describe both numerical actions and visual changes in language, enabling joint training of a single future predictor across heterogeneous datasets. Adding image and video data further improves average planning performance and robustness to action phrasing. In a matched comparison with an RAEv2-based video generation model baseline, LaLa-World reaches comparable planning performance with $6\times$ less training cost and $13.9\times$ faster planning. Beyond planning, the pretrained LaLa-World serves as an initialization for LaLa-Policy, which jointly predicts actions and future visual representations. LaLa-Policy performs competitively with Cosmos Policy on LIBERO and RoboCasa, with up to $5.5\times$ faster inference.

## 5. Testing Associative Creativity in Humans and Multimodal LLMs with Difficult-to-Name Visual Stimuli

**作者:** Manar Ali, Lisa Gottschalk, Simeon Junker, Lisa Hudalla, Alon Fishman et al.

**分类:** cs.CL, cs.CV

**摘要:**

We investigate associative creativity in descriptions of abstract visual stimuli by humans and multimodal large language models (MLLMs). While conceptual association is central to human communication, it remains unclear how humans and models adapt when visual stimuli become increasingly difficult to map onto familiar semantic concepts. We evaluate several state-of-the-art MLLMs and human participants along two dimensions of creativity, originality and effectiveness, using two datasets with controlled manipulations of difficulty. ## 6. World2HOSI: A Multi-Expert Harness for Long-Horizon Human-Object-Scene Interaction Generation

**作者:** Jiushu Li, Haoran Wang, Yumeng Liu, Zhiyang Dou, Yuan Liu et al.

**分类:** cs.CV, cs.AI

**摘要:**

Long-horizon human-object-scene interaction (HOSI) is central to embodied AI, animation, and game development. Existing human-object interaction (HOI) and human-scene interaction (HSI) models excel in their respective domains, but none alone covers tasks requiring multiple interaction skills. Training unified HOSI models is hindered by scarce and costly data. We introduce World2HOSI, a multi-expert orchestration harness that generates continuous HOSI data from 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) scenes and natural language instructions. ## 7. Efficient Edge-aware Attention Network for Graph Generation

## 8. AWLS: Advantage-Weighted Latent Steering for One-Step Generative Robotic Manipulation Policies

**作者:** Jiahong Tang, Jiaju He, Zhiyuan Yang, Ruyang Han, Haiyue Zhu

**分类:** cs.RO

**摘要:**

Human-collected real-world robotic manipulation demonstrations often contain mixed-quality trajectories, including hesitant, indirect, or inefficient action segments. While diffusion- and flow-based policies can model such multimodal data, standard behavior cloning (BC) assigns equal likelihood to all demonstrated actions and may therefore reproduce suboptimal modes. Offline RL value estimates offer a natural signal for biasing policies toward higher-value behavioral modes, but directly fine-tuning the generative policy or using critic gradients for policy steering can amplify critic errors and induce prior drift. We propose Advantage-Weighted Latent Steering (AWLS), an offline post-training method for improving frozen one-step generative manipulation policies without updating their decoders. We further introduce a rollout-free decoder-sensitivity criterion for selecting pretrained checkpoints that remain responsive to latent-noise perturbations. Experiments on simulated and real-world robotic manipulation tasks with clean and mixed-quality demonstrations show that AWLS improves over the frozen base policy while limiting drift from the pretrained behavior prior. ## 14. Ask to Learn: Query-Based Collaborative Reinforcement Learning

**arXiv ID:** [2610.27606v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27606v1)

**作者:** Kaicheng Zhang, Hongqian Niu, Laixi Shi, Didong Li, Guannan Qu et al.

**分类:** stat.ML, cs.LG

**摘要:**

Offline reinforcement learning is fundamentally limited by the coverage of the available data, while collecting additional trajectories through online exploration may be costly, unsafe, or infeasible. We study whether this limitation can be overcome without directly interacting with the underlying environment. Motivated by practical settings such as collaborator-mediated simulation, where a collaborator can provide information complementary to the offline data, we introduce query-based collaborative reinforcement learning to study how to acquire this information efficiently. The learner starts with a fixed offline dataset and adaptively queries state-time pairs; the collaborator samples an action according to a fixed, possibly suboptimal policy, and the simulator returns the corresponding reward and next state. Experiments on multiple synthetic and semi-synthetic environments demonstrate the empirical efficiency of the proposed method.

## 15. ACE-Compression: An Ability-Centric Evaluation of Compressed Large Language Models

**arXiv ID:** [2610.27913v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27913v1)

**作者:** Jiahui Ning, Liang Chen, Yuchen Liang, Yi Zhang, Fenglei Fan

**分类:** cs.AI, cs.CL

**摘要:**

Model compression makes large language models deployable, but today's evaluation tells an incomplete story. Standard practice measures retained task performance and stops there. This misses a critical fact: compression perturbs the distributed knowledge embedded across billions of weights, and task scores alone cannot reveal which underlying abilities survive and which quietly collapse. To solve this question, we introduce ACE-Compression, an ability-centric evaluation framework. Instead of asking only "does the compressed model still answer correctly?", we ask "which of its core abilities remain intact?" Our framework probes four deployment-facing dimensions: procedural reasoning and constraint execution, knowledge grounding and truthfulness, social cognition and affective understanding, and persona fidelity and stylistic consistency. Across more than 35 compressed checkpoints from six open-weight LLMs-covering quantization and pruning-we find that compression redistributes ability profiles. Coarse, local, and surface-level signals often persist, while fine-grained, long-tail, and context-dependent abilities become fragile. Quantization consistently outperforms pruning in retention, but the practical meaning of that gap depends entirely on which ability an application requires. Based on our findings, deployers can choose compression methods by the ability profile their application needs, not just by a leaderboard average. And algorithm designers gain diagnostic signals to build compression methods that preserve what matters. We believe that ACE-Compression turns compression from a black-box accuracy drop into an interpretable intervention-one we can measure, understand, and ultimately improve.

## 17. Why Do LLMs Accept Generics? Explaining Acceptability using Psycholinguistic and Model-Derived Factors

**arXiv ID:** [2610.27928v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27928v1)

**作者:** Mervat Abassy, Zhaoxin Feng, Tatsuki Kuribayashi, Kentaro Inui, Emmanuele Chersoni

**分类:** cs.CL

**摘要:**

Psycholinguistic studies explore why humans accept some generic statements but reject others by showing that these judgments depend on more than how common a property is. People consider several other factors such as how well it sets the kind apart from others or whether it is characteristic of the kind. Inspired by the linguistic and cognitive literature, we explore to what extent factors from these studies explain how large language models (LLMs) judge generics. Our results with twelve models on a psycholinguistic dataset and on naturally occurring generics show that models weigh how common a property is against how well it distinguishes the kind, and combine the two much as people do, though less clearly. Like people, they favor characteristic properties, but unlike the accounts of human judgment, they ignore striking ones. What matters most to LLMs is whether a statement is factually true, and much of the influence of human factors reaches their ratings through that judgment. In six 7-9B models, shifting the hidden states along each factor's direction moves the judgment as the factor predicts.

## 19. Tracing Recurrent and Emergent Hallucination Patterns in Decomposed Text-to-SQL Generation

**arXiv ID:** [2610.27720v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27720v1)

**作者:** Hayden Moore, Abhinav Verma, Anshuman Dutt

**分类:** cs.CL

**摘要:**

Large language models can achieve strong Text-to-SQL benchmark performance while still producing schema and query-logic errors. Most evaluations characterize such failures only in the final generated query, making it difficult to determine whether a related error was also observed in an earlier, simpler step or appears only at the final stage. We present a drill-down evaluation framework that decomposes Text-to-SQL queries into progressively more complex executable sub-queries and corresponding natural-language questions, allowing rule-based hallucination categories to be examined across a generation path. We distinguish recurrent hallucinations, in which the same rule-based category is observed in an earlier step and again in the final query, from emergent hallucinations, in which the category is observed at the final step without an earlier detection in the same path. Across five LLMs evaluated on an expanded BIRD-mini benchmark, several categories show appreciable recurrence probabilities, while clause abuse shows comparatively higher conditional emergence. Under the conversational-history condition, recurrence probabilities are generally higher and emergence probabilities are generally lower. These observations indicate that the two conditional measures do not move uniformly in the same direction under additional context and motivate trajectory-level analysis in addition to final-output evaluation. ## 20. Prescribed Performance Guided Safe Reinforcement Learning for Roundabout Navigation of Autonomous Vehicles

**作者:** Wenhan Li, Yangrui Zhang, Hang Zhou, Peiran Liu, Yiding Ji

**分类:** cs.RO

**摘要:**

Connected and Autonomous Vehicles (CAVs) face significant challenges in roundabout navigation due to dense traffic interactions and unpredictable agent behaviors, necessitating high levels of adaptability and safety.
