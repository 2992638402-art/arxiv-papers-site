## 1. Harness Scaling for Generalizable Agentic Reinforcement Learning

**arXiv ID:** [2610.29458v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29458v1)

**作者:** Fei Bai, Huatong Song, Chuan Hao, Ran Tao, Jian Yang et al.

**分类:** cs.LG, cs.AI, cs.CL

**摘要:**

Agent harnesses have become a central component of modern agentic systems, shaping how models use tools, manage context, and coordinate long-horizon interaction. Realizing the benefits of these harnesses depends on how effectively the underlying model can make use of them, motivating reinforcement learning directly through the harness. However, existing approaches typically train through a single fixed harness, which can lead to specialization and limited transfer across harness configurations. To address this, we introduce \textbf{Harness Scaling}, a new scaling dimension for agentic RL that extends training from a single harness to diverse execution configurations. Concretely, we construct a factorized harness space, select a compact training subset with balanced factorial coverage, and organize cross-harness training to combine global coverage with controlled variation for the same task. Across multiple benchmarks, model scales, and RL algorithms, Harness Scaling consistently improves generalization to unseen white-box configurations. Moreover, models trained through structured white-box variation transfer effectively to unseen external black-box harnesses, outperforming models trained through a single complex black-box harness. These results establish harness diversity as a meaningful scaling dimension for agentic RL and highlight the importance of how this diversity is organized for cross-harness generalization.

## 2. Sparsity Should Be ALLOTted by Output: Training-Free Allocation of Block-Sparse Attention for Video Diffusion Transformers

**arXiv ID:** [2610.29441v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29441v1)

**作者:** Tianze Xia, Xiaoling Zhou, Ruya Lan, Yiqiao Liao, Chengru Song et al.

**分类:** cs.CV

**摘要:**

Full 3D attention is costly in video Diffusion Transformers. Block-sparse attention reduces this cost by computing only selected attention blocks. However, existing methods either overlook the potential to optimize sparsity allocation or rely on heuristic rules or local metrics to guide it. In this work, we observe that sparsifying attention in different layers and at different denoising steps affects final-video fidelity to substantially different degrees. Yet the identities of the most sensitive layer-step pairs remain nearly unchanged across inputs. Building on these findings, we propose Allocating Layer-step Latency by Output Tests (ALLOT), a training-free sparsity allocation method. Treating each layer-step pair as a cell, ALLOT estimates each cell's contribution to final-video error through (i) cell probing and (ii) trajectory calibration, then jointly optimizes cell densities under a measured-latency budget. It preserves existing selection strategies and kernels, replacing only the allocation component. At equal or shorter wall-clock time, ALLOT outperforms every compared allocation strategy with XAttention, with an 11.8 dB PSNR gain on HunyuanVideo. Extensive experiments across four additional published pipelines on Wan2.1, Wan2.2, and HunyuanVideo show that ALLOT outperforms or ties the default in all nine settings.

## 3. AdvFidelity: Unifying Preference Alignment and Few-Step Image Generation within Reinforcement Learning

**arXiv ID:** [2610.29380v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29380v1)

**作者:** Sicong Kang, Fangao Zeng, Yuhan Li, Hao Zhou, Mengfei Xu et al.

**分类:** cs.CV

**摘要:**

Practical image generation requires both human preference alignment and few-step efficiency, yet combining reinforcement learning (RL) with distillation is often multi-stage and may erode RL gains We observe that few-step RL progressively improves structural coherence, even with specialized rewards, but leaves low-level artifacts that these rewards may fail to penalize. To address this gap, we propose AdvFidelity, which integrates artifact-sensitive fidelity supervision into RL through an online adversarial reward. This enables joint reward alignment and high-fidelity few-step generation within a single RL stage, without distribution matching distillation (DMD) or other explicit distillation objectives. We train a discriminator to rank high-quality references (positives) above content-matched reconstructions (negatives) produced by the current generator. Reconstructing moderately noised references largely preserves style and scene layout while exposing low-level degradations. This encourages the discriminator to assess artifacts rather than content differences. With negatives reconstructed online, alternating updates adapt fidelity supervision to the generator's evolving failure modes. References can come from existing target-domain data or full-step model samples. Built on LeapAlign, AdvFidelity backpropagates gradients from both rewards through the few-step sampling trajectories used at inference. Across two model backbones and three task settings, AdvFidelity matches full-step LeapAlign in alignment, task performance, and overall generation quality and remains highly competitive with other evaluated full-step RL methods, using only 4 or 8 steps without CFG.

## 5. JaxMARL-BC: Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning for Macroeconomic Modelling, in Seconds

**arXiv ID:** [2610.28907v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28907v1)

**作者:** Daniele Montagnani, Andrea Coletta, Antonietta Mira, Aldo Glielmo

**分类:** cs.MA, cs.AI, cs.LG, econ.GN

**摘要:**

Multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL) offers a principled way of populating simulated economies with many heterogeneous decision makers. Its adoption, pioneered in finance, has nonetheless been slow in macroeconomics because of two main limitations: the lack of a shared frame of reference connecting MARL systems to dynamic stochastic general equilibrium (DSGE) models the profession already trusts, and the very long training times. The first limitation was recently addressed by the Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning Business Cycle (MARL-BC), which places deep RL households in a real business cycle economy and reduces to classic DSGE models in specific limits. We verify its correctness against the known DSGE limits, characterise its scaling through a simple cost law, and demonstrate its value by a proof-of-concept calibration of an extended model with heterogeneous returns on capital, in a two-hour experiment that would previously have taken over a month. We release JaxMARL-BC as open source.

## 6. Efficient Edge-aware Attention Network for Graph Generation

**arXiv ID:** [2610.28618v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28618v1)

**作者:** Vincent Jung, Alba Carballo-Castro, Yiming Qin, Lonneke van der Plas, Pascal Frossard

**分类:** cs.LG

**摘要:**

Graph generative models commonly use graph-specific Transformer architectures to jointly update node and edge features. However, these architectures do not directly benefit from recent hardware-aware attention implementations, limiting their scalability. The adaptation is, however, non-trivial: edge and graph-level features must be injected into pairwise attention scores, graph masks must be respected, and dynamic edge updates remain a major source of quadratic memory usage. We propose an edge-aware attention layer for graph generation that incorporates structural information through head-wise score modulation, while updating edge representations with a lightweight residual mechanism that retains attention-score dependent edge updates. This layer can be effectively implemented using PyTorch's FlexAttention. We achieve similar generation quality to our baseline while reducing peak GPU memory by around 65% and substantially improving training and sampling throughput. These results suggest that the proposed architecture is a practical step toward scalable graph foundation models.

## 7. OptionWorld: A Benchmark for Diagnosing Failures of LLMs as Long-Term Personal Assistants

**arXiv ID:** [2610.28491v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28491v1)

**作者:** Ruoyu Zhang, Zhaocen Liu, Weiyi Tian, Melissa Feng, Shilong Liu

**分类:** cs.CL

**摘要:**

Long-term assistants must follow unfinished tasks across days as information and circumstances change. Finding information or starting an action, however, does not ensure that the task gets completed. We introduce OptionWorld, a benchmark that traces information acquisition, judgments, actions, and outcomes in simulated daily-life tasks to identify where current LLMs fall short as long-term personal assistants. In runs with archived decision inputs, the key facts remain in the model's last input before the deadline in 35.5% of failed instances. Failure analysis identifies the three most frequent problems: needed information is not obtained or combined, available opportunities to act go unused, and agents impose restrictions that the task does not require. Three follow-up experiments show that supplying missing facts restores task success in some cases; action reminders can restart work while subsequent steps still miss the deadline; and correcting a supplied note that wrongly forbids contact restores permitted inquiry. These findings locate shortcomings in maintaining task state: connecting facts to unfinished work, following through before deadlines, and separating actual restrictions from the agent's assumptions.

## 8. CCG Diffusion: Combinatory Categorial Grammar-Driven Compositional and Syntactically Disambiguated Text-to-Image Generation

**arXiv ID:** [2610.29415v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29415v1)

**作者:** Ryosuke Yamaki, Daichi Mochihashi, Nobutaka Shimada, Seitaro Shinagawa, Tadahiro Taniguchi

**分类:** cs.CV, cs.CL

**摘要:**

Text-to-image generation models do not explicitly receive the syntactic structure of the prompt, i.e., which word modifies which and where each phrase attaches. Consequently, they often struggle to bind attributes to the correct objects, to depict spatial relations and counts correctly, and to render syntactically ambiguous prompts in the intended interpretation. Existing syntax-based control methods mainly address attribute binding and have limited ability to control spatial relations and counts or to specify the interpretation of syntactically ambiguous prompts. They rely on phrase-structure or dependency grammars and require additional rules or constraints on the tree structure to extract modification relations between words or to specify a syntactic interpretation. We propose CCG Diffusion, a training-free method that controls image generation using Combinatory Categorial Grammar (CCG). In CCG, the category of each word carries rich syntactic information: it specifies what the word combines with and what phrase it forms. Modification relations between words can therefore be read directly from a CCG parse. Moreover, prepositional phrase (PP) attachment ambiguity, a representative syntactic ambiguity, can be resolved simply by fixing the preposition's category. CCG Diffusion applies the same two CCG-based mechanisms, prompt rewriting and attention guidance, both to controlling attribute binding, spatial relations, and counts and to resolving PP ambiguity. On two architecturally different backbones, Stable Diffusion and Sana, CCG Diffusion achieves the highest scores among the compared methods in attribute binding and 2D spatial relations and significantly outperforms all of them in PP ambiguity resolution.

## 9. Parallel Thinking for Large Language Model with Path Diversity

**分类:** cs.AI

**摘要:**

Parallel thinking has emerged as a promising paradigm for enhancing the reasoning capabilities of Large Language Models (LLMs) by exploring multiple reasoning paths simultaneously. However, we identify that parallel thinking via Reinforcement Learning (RL) typically suffers from the mode collapse of reasoning paths, where parallel thinking trajectories degenerate into similar ones. This homogeneity undermines the fundamental motivation of parallel thinking, that is, maintaining multiple hypotheses concurrently to prevent premature loss into a suboptimal solution. In this paper, we propose Diverse Parallel Thinking (DPT), a simple yet effective RL approach for parallel thinking designed to encourage path diversity. DPT rewards the model to develop distinct reasoning strategies by penalizing the prefix similarity of parallel thinking paths, thereby enhancing a more comprehensive exploration of the reasoning space. We conduct various experiments and analyses across multiple reasoning benchmarks to demonstrate the effectiveness of DPT. Results show that DPT successfully outperforms previous parallel thinking methods, achieving state-of-the-art performance over baselines. We validate path diversity as a key element to improve parallel thinking. Our code is available at https://github.com/JiaruQian/DPT.

## 11. Generalized CycleGRPO for Fine-Grained Multimodal Understanding and Grounding

**作者:** Xin Zhang, Haochen Wang, Beibei Lin, Zhaoxiang Zhang, Robby T. Tan et al.

**分类:** cs.CV

**摘要:**

Fine-grained multimodal intelligence requires models to both understand a designated region and localize it from language. Existing methods learn these directions with separate objectives and task-specific annotations. We present Generalized CycleGRPO, the first unified reinforcement learning framework coupling region understanding and localization across spatial, temporal, and spatio-temporal domains. Given a region, a multimodal large language model generates candidate descriptions and then localizes each description to reconstruct the input region. Reconstruction overlap provides a verifiable reward for captioning, while individual localization rewards improve the inverse mapping. For temporal tasks, it achieves 51.9% tIoU on Charades and 33.3% Temporal Evidence Coverage on DOVE, compared with 45.6% and 14.8% for the Qwen3-VL initialization. These results establish region-text cycle consistency as a general self-evaluation principle for multimodal understanding and localization. ## 12. LiveAct: Towards Real-Time Multi-Agent World Models with Heterogeneous Action Interfaces

**作者:** Xinyu Wang, Huafeng Shi, Yue Ma, Zijie Meng, Teng Xi et al.

**分类:** cs.CV

**摘要:**

Interactive video world models predict how an environment evolves under user actions, but most existing systems assume one controlled agent and one task-specific action interface. We study multi-agent video world modeling, where one model generates a joint visual future for multiple controlled agents while interpreting heterogeneous controls ranging from binary game buttons to continuous robot commands. This task presents two complementary challenges. First, every agent-indexed action stream must control its intended visual entity independently. Second, the model must adapt the meaning of each control variable across different tasks, where the same tensor dimension may denote an attack, steering command, or robot command. We introduce LiveAct, centered on a Heterogeneous Action Interface (HAI) whose two components mirror this decomposition. We further transfer the complete interface to a blockwise causal generator through teacher forcing and rollout-aware distribution matching, enabling efficient streaming generation while retaining agent-specific control and action semantics. We instantiate the formulation on six multi-agent sources spanning games and multi-robot manipulation, and evaluate visual quality, action following, agent attribution, and latency. Across games and robot manipulation, LiveAct reaches 0.898 and 0.942 agent-attribution accuracy without agent locations or identity crops, and the causal generator attains 17.70 FPS at $512\times 448$.

## 15. Plan First, Act Later: Streaming Reasoning for Executable Memory in Robot Control

**arXiv ID:** [2610.29329v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29329v1)

**作者:** Qinghang Liu, Conglin Wang, Zongsheng Li, Boyong He, Han Gao et al.

**分类:** cs.RO

**摘要:**

Long-horizon robot manipulation requires a robot to remember task-relevant information beyond its short observation window. Existing memory-augmented policies typically retrieve relevant information from history and reason over it at execution time, placing a heavy reasoning burden on the high-level model. To address this challenge, we propose PAL (\textbf{P}lan first, \textbf{A}ct \textbf{L}ater), a hierarchical memory framework for vision-language robot manipulation. It maintains a short-term visual buffer, a semantic-event memory, and an executable \texttt{subtask\_list}. On RoboMME, PAL achieves a \textbf{56.05\%} success rate, outperforming the strongest baseline, FrameSamp-Modul, at 44.51\%. In real-world experiments across four manipulation tasks, \pal{} reaches \textbf{60.83\%} overall success, surpassing MemER (45.00\%) and FrameSamp-Modul (28.33\%). These results show that proactively writing future-relevant subtasks into hierarchical memory improves long-horizon visual reasoning and robot control. Code and videos can be found on our website: https://qinghangliu.github.io/pal-webpage/

## 16. One Act, Many Masks: Effect-Marginal Policy Optimization for Long-Horizon Agents

**arXiv ID:** [2610.29427v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29427v1)

**作者:** Shaojin Ma, Ruitao Wang, Pengyi Li, Jie Yang, Liangyu Chen et al.

**分类:** cs.LG, stat.ML

**摘要:**

Recent advances in multi-turn reinforcement learning (RL) have substantially improved the performance of LLM agents on complex, long-horizon interactive tasks. However, despite progress in fine-grained credit assignment, policy optimization still applies these learning signals through response-specific probability ratios. Different textual responses can induce the same executable effect, yet credit differences arising from subsequent outcomes remain tied to their individual textual realizations. We identify this mismatch as credit-update misalignment. Our analysis reveals that credit-text pairing directly shapes policy-update directions, even when the policy context and executable effect are held fixed. Motivated by this finding, we propose Effect-Marginal Policy Optimization (EMPO), which aligns policy optimization with execution-level equivalence through an effect-level surrogate that couples updates across alternative textual realizations. EMPO preserves the original credit signals, integrates with both trajectory-level and step-level credit estimators, and requires no additional critics or rollouts. Experiments on ALFWorld, WebShop, and Sokoban show that EMPO outperforms strong baselines in both task performance and learning efficiency.

## 17. BitMTP: When Multi-Token Prediction Meets Low-Bit Large Language Models

**arXiv ID:** [2610.28500v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28500v1)

**作者:** Ning Zhang, Shihao Wang, Jinrui Zhang, Chaodong Xiao, Lei Zhang

**分类:** cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.DC, cs.LG

**摘要:**

For efficient deployment of large language models (LLMs), methods of ultra-low-bit backbones reduce weight storage, memory traffic, and linear-layer execution cost, while multi-token prediction (MTP) methods reduce sequential decoding steps by predicting multiple future tokens in parallel. Although complementary, the direct combination of these methods cannot accelerate decoding at comparable quality: masked drafting lacks intermediate token input supplied in next-token training, while wider computation and causal key-value (KV) updates can offset the savings from fewer steps. To address these challenges, we propose BitMTP, an algorithm-system co-design framework for efficient MTP on ultra-low-bit LLM backbones. A joint next-token prediction and MTP objective equips a single model with both autoregressive (AR) generation and block-causal drafting. Confidence-only decoding, speculative-only decoding, and hybrid decoding provide different quality-throughput operating points without using separate draft and verifier models. Accepted-prefix alignment keeps the committed prefix, materialized causal KV boundary, and CUDA Graph replay state consistent for continued generation. BitMTP uses this contract to select width-specialized low-bit execution and reuse captured graphs over shared KV storage. On NVIDIA H20, BitMTP with hybrid decoding achieves five-task mean speedups of 2.2x on BitNet b1.58-2B and 2.1-2.4x on 1.7B-4B Bonsai models at comparable task quality. On RTX 3090 and Jetson Orin, it further achieves up to 2.0x in five-task mean throughput and 3.3x on MBPP+, respectively. These results demonstrate that BitMTP accelerates ultra-low-bit LLM generation across backbones, model sizes, and GPU types. The source code is released at https://github.com/PolyU-VCLab/BitMTP

## 18. RubricOpt: Self-Revision of Semantic Rubrics for Preference Optimization

**arXiv ID:** [2610.29491v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29491v1)

**作者:** Wei Chen, Yubing Wu, Shigui Li, Delu Zeng, Jingfeng Liu et al.

**分类:** cs.AI

**摘要:**

A model's failures can reveal gaps in the behavioral criteria guiding its training. We introduce RubricOpt, a framework that lets a policy revise these criteria within preference optimization. The policy diagnoses its own rubric-free outputs, retains requirements already covered by the rubric, and edits clauses that leave recurring failures unaddressed. A shared semantic representation connects task demands, preference-data contrasts, and rubric clauses, while a deterministic gate checks evidence references and fixed semantic anchors. The rubric then guides Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) during training, with preference pairs, labels, and the objective held fixed, and is removed at evaluation. Our analysis relates the local effect on rubric-free success to semantic alignment and a residual beyond the measured factors. Experiments on capability and open-ended generation benchmarks show that rubric conditioning can improve outcomes over ordinary DPO, with gains and regressions varying across models and datasets. A matched continuation isolates the contribution of revision from additional training and finds mixed incremental effects for the tested edit, whose feedback overlaps the evaluation set. RubricOpt thus brings the behavioral specification itself into the policy's feedback loop.
