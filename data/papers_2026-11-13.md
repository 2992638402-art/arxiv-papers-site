## 1. Is the Importance Ratio Necessary for Stable Reinforcement Learning in LLMs?

**arXiv ID:** [2611.15404v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15404v1)

**作者:** Shuibai Zhang, Junhyuck Kim, Gyeongman Kim, Jaewoong Cho

**分类:** cs.LG, cs.AI, cs.CL

**摘要:**

Reinforcement learning (RL) has become central to post-training large language models (LLMs). However, popular RL methods like GRPO incur non-negligible overhead by computing both old-policy and current-policy likelihoods to form importance sampling ratios. In this work, we propose Likelihood-Gated Policy Optimization (LGPO), which enforces a soft trust region constraint via likelihood-based gating, eliminating the need to compute old-policy likelihoods. Empirically, we show that removing the importance sampling correction term does not harm training stability, whereas removing the trust region mechanism can lead to collapse. Moreover, ratio-based clipping can fail in fully on-policy training: the importance ratio stays at 1, so the ratio-based trust region constraint never activates. Under standard training settings where GRPO is stable, LGPO achieves comparable training stability and peak performance while reducing training time by ~18% on average relative to a standard implementation that recomputes old-policy likelihoods. In fully on-policy training, where GRPO fails, LGPO remains stable, enabling more efficient and robust LLM RL post-training across training regimes.

## 2. CaseScope: A Multi-Field Benchmark for Case-Level Pathology Diagnosis with Multimodal LLMs

**arXiv ID:** [2611.15325v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15325v1)

**作者:** Wenchuan Zhang, Chengyang Zhang, Fengling Li, Ting Yin, Chen Shu et al.

**分类:** cs.CV

**摘要:**

Pathologists rarely diagnose from a single image: they scan a section at low power for architecture, zoom into selected fields for cytology, and integrate the evidence across views and scales. Pathology benchmarks for multimodal large language models (MLLMs), however, mostly ask about one image or require whole-slide pipelines, and the few that show several fields draw nested regions from cancer slides of one public cohort. We introduce CaseScope, a benchmark of diagnosis from several curated fields of one lesion: CaseScope-Teaching, 436 cases from a residency-training image library, reconstructed, de-identified, harmonised against a WHO-based ontology and re-examined by a pathologist wherever an automated pre-review raised a doubt, and CaseScope-Atlas, 800 cases from 88 published atlases (387 of them figure series of one disease), labelled from the captions by two LLMs that must agree. They cover 843 entities across 18 organ systems and pose 4,679 items in six tasks, from open-ended and ten-way diagnosis with ontology-guided hard negatives under circular evaluation to the verification and location of morphological findings. Across twenty-eight open and four proprietary MLLMs, diagnoses are unstable -- the best open and proprietary models are correct under all ten option rotations on 17.9% and 61.7% of the teaching cases -- and wrong answers favour the classic look-alike; open models make little use of the additional fields on teaching cases, where the one-line specimen site matters more, although on atlas cases with three or more fields the fields count about as much as the site; and most open models verify findings barely above chance (median 62.0% vs. 50%, best 81.5%), gaining a median +3.4 points from images; proprietary models reach 83.2-93.2%. Rankings broadly replicate between the sources. The evaluation code is available at https://github.com/Wenchuan-Zhang/CaseScope.

## 3. FIBER: Bayesian Fusion of Fast-DetectGPT and IRT-Informed Detector Evidence for LLM-Generated Text Detection

**arXiv ID:** [2611.15058v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15058v1)

**作者:** Yan Li, Guangsheng Bao, Zhipeng Chen, Guandong Zhang, Yanbin Zhao

**分类:** cs.CL

**摘要:**

Detecting large language model (LLM)-generated text remains challenging under domain and generator shifts. Supervised detectors may generalize poorly, while zero-shot methods often rely on individual statistical signals without leveraging historical detector behavior. We propose FIBER (Fast-DetectGPT with IRT-informed Bayesian Evidence Recalibration), which integrates continuous Fast-DetectGPT scores with auxiliary detector outputs through Bayesian evidence fusion. Item response theory (IRT) models historical detector-correctness patterns, providing class-conditional detector-output likelihoods through marginalization over latent text representations. These likelihoods are combined with Fast-DetectGPT score densities and a historical class prior for prediction. When historical Fast-DetectGPT scores are unavailable, a variant called FIBER-Stream estimates class-specific score distributions from an unlabeled batch. On a 32,000-text multi-domain benchmark, FIBER achieves the highest accuracy (0.620) and improves mean AUC over Fast-DetectGPT (0.664 versus 0.600) under five-fold cross-validation. FIBER also consistently improves AUC across four individual domains. Under out-of-distribution transfer, FIBER-Stream achieved an AUC of 0.911, compared with 0.889 for FIBER and 0.922 for Fast-DetectGPT alone. These results demonstrate the potential of integrating complementary detector evidence and historical information to improve detection performance across heterogeneous settings.

## 4. Thompson Sampling using Prior-fitted Diffusion Transformers

**arXiv ID:** [2611.14656v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14656v1)

**作者:** Sihwa Park, Jingsen Zhu, Vinamr Jain, Sheng-Yen Chou, Alexander Terenin

**分类:** cs.LG, cs.AI, stat.ML

**摘要:**

Thompson sampling is natural randomized strategy for optimizing unknown functions in a black-box manner. In continuous global optimization, Thompson sampling has largely been restricted to Gaussian process priors, as these are essentially the only class for which computing the posterior distribution has been tractable in practice. In this work, we introduce \emph{prior-fitted diffusion transformers}, which are diffusion models that perform Thompson sampling in-context at inference time, after being pre-trained using the prior-fitted network paradigm. To apply these models, we develop a set of practical non-Gaussian priors for general black-box functions---including those that have characteristics that cannot be achieved under Gaussianity, such as priors over unimodal functions. We also quantify the tradeoff between model size and the number of pre-training iterations, and show that these models follow scaling laws akin to those observed in transformers defined over other modalities. Across a set of global optimization benchmarks, we show that prior-fitted diffusion transformers with non-Gaussian priors can achieve improved performance compared to traditional pipelines built on Gaussian processes.

## 5. PARLEY: Evaluating LLM Collaboration Through Cooperative Asymmetric Puzzles

**arXiv ID:** [2611.14242v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14242v1)

**作者:** Javier Hernandez, Andrew D. Wilson

**分类:** cs.MA

**摘要:**

As large language models power autonomous agents, understanding how they collaborate and where they fail becomes critical. We introduce PARLEY, an extensible platform for studying LLM agent collaboration through four asymmetric puzzle tasks grounded in cognitive psychology (referential communication, joint coordination, perspective-taking, and spatial reasoning). GPT-4o agent pairs succeeded in 60.5% of conversations (N=400, 100 per task), with task-level variation (34-80%) reflecting distinct collaborative demands. From these conversations, we derived the data-driven PAC taxonomy: six failure types organized into Perception (visual interpretation and spatial reasoning), Adaptation (progress tracking and feedback integration), and Coordination (verification and grounding). Failure to incorporate external feedback was a critical indicator of unsuccessful collaboration, with agents repeatedly applying unsuccessful strategies. We also show that a taxonomy-guided intervention (N=200) improved average task success to 70.5%. These results demonstrate how PARLEY can be used to derive meaningful, data-driven insights that can in turn lead to improved agentic collaboration.

## 6. Semantic Consistency of Vision Tokens: A Vision-Centric Perspective on Multimodal Large Language Models

**arXiv ID:** [2611.15448v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15448v1)

**作者:** Nikola Đukić, Tim Lebailly, Tinne Tuytelaars

**分类:** cs.CV

**摘要:**

In computer vision, vision-language and self-supervised encoders are two unique paradigms characterized by global language alignment and high-quality vision-only patch representations, respectively. Multimodal large language models (MLLMs) often use the former because of language alignment, despite the recent rise in vision-centric tasks resembling traditional dense vision tasks that would likely benefit from the high-quality patch representations, raising the question whether self-supervised models could aid MLLMs in vision-centric tasks. To investigate this, we define semantic (in)consistency of patch tokens from language-aligned encoders and identify two sets of tokens, semantically consistent (SCTs) and semantically inconsistent (SITs). Through systematic probing via the removal of SCTs or SITs from the input of modern MLLMs, we show higher reliance on SCTs than SITs, as removal of the former induces larger performance drops. This demonstrates that MLLMs benefit from semantic consistency of vision tokens. With this in mind, we devise a simple yet effective extension of the MLLM training objective that combines traditional language modeling with semantic guidance via minimization of semantic inconsistency. This strategy, named Semantically-Guided Training (SGT), combines the signals of both loss terms to push the vision encoder towards providing semantically consistent tokens, while preserving language alignment. Extensive evaluation shows that SGT outperforms all baselines and MLLMs with specialized vision encoders on vision-centric benchmarks while keeping strong scores on text-centric benchmarks, thus setting state of the art on average across all tasks. Our results highlight the importance of combining language alignment and semantic consistency through the lens of MLLMs rather than independently from them and pave the way towards the design of better MLLM vision encoders.

## 7. The Illusion of Diversity: Aligning LLM Exploration via Effective Entropy

**arXiv ID:** [2611.15493v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15493v1)

**作者:** Xiaoliang Fu, Jiaye Lin, Yangyi Fang, Cong Qin, Binbin Zheng et al.

**分类:** cs.LG

**摘要:**

Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards has shifted the alignment paradigm of large language models from subjective preference to objective correctness, yet a fundamental disconnect persists between exploration diversity and reasoning validity. Existing strategies predominantly rely on "blind" entropy metrics, e.g., token-level or global sequence-level entropy, which structurally fail to distinguish between effective reasoning and potential hallucinations. This misalignment creates an illusion of diversity, where high entropy tends to signal chaotic degeneration rather than valuable exploration. To bridge this gap, we introduce Effective Entropy, a validity-aware entropy concept that quantifies diversity exclusively within the valid subspace and systematically filters out the noise of incorrect trajectories. Built on this concept, we instantiate VEPO, a simple Validity-aware Entropy Policy Optimization method that directly incorporates effective entropy into RLVR training. VEPO induces an adaptive attraction dynamic that drives up the probability of under-explored valid trajectories, a mechanism that matches the practical regime of large-scale autoregressive models where sequence probabilities remain extremely small. Experiments on six mathematical reasoning benchmarks with 1.5B and 7B DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen models show consistent aggregate accuracy gains over GRPO and competitive repeated-sampling success. A direct evaluation with matched correct counts finds more distinct strategies on commonly solved MATH500 problems, supported by paired statistical tests and three-seed training comparisons.

## 8. RE-PRESENT: Evidence Triangulation across Alternative Candidate-Space Views for Efficient LLM Ensemble Inference

**arXiv ID:** [2611.14780v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14780v1)

**作者:** Juming Xiong, Weixin Liu, Kevin Guo, Congning Ni, Chongyu Qu et al.

**分类:** cs.MA

**摘要:**

Heterogeneous large language models (LLMs) exhibit complementary strengths, motivating routing and multi-model inference methods that seek to identify reliable predictions for each input. However, exploiting this complementarity remains challenging even when a correct prediction is available within the ensemble. We investigate this challenge across heterogeneous LLM ensembles and find that a simple single-round aggregation rule is highly competitive with learned routing methods, yet substantial oracle headroom remains. Further analysis shows that the evaluated single-round response signals provide limited discriminative power for identifying reliable predictions. In contrast, controlled re-presentations of the same question elicit complementary evidence about prediction reliability. Motivated by these findings, we introduce RE-PRESENT, a training-free framework that selectively re-presents disputed questions and directly aggregates candidate-level evidence across alternative views. Across seven multiple-choice benchmarks and two heterogeneous model ensembles, RE-PRESENT improves over strong single-round aggregation while offering a favorable accuracy--cost trade-off compared with self-consistency and multi-model debate.

## 10. Decide Before You Move: Diffusion-Generated Search Reference for Zero-Shot Open-Vocabulary Object Navigation in Semi-Static Environments

**arXiv ID:** [2611.15093v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15093v1)

**作者:** Yuxuan Feng, Fujing Xie, Sören Schwertfeger, Hermann Blum

**分类:** cs.RO

**摘要:**

Open-world navigation requires a robot to locate a user-specified target in an indoor scene under open-vocabulary queries. Realistic indoor environments are semi-static: walls and furniture largely persist while smaller objects move, relocate, or vanish between visits. Prior-map systems answer queries from object poses recorded at reconstruction time, which expire as soon as objects move, whereas online methods re-derive where to look from current observations every few steps, often through expensive calls to a vision--language model. Assuming that walls and large furniture do not move, we keep their map to plan paths, but use a target--furniture co-occurrence prior to decide where to search. We present PreNav, an ObjectNav framework on semi-static environments. Experiments on a Gibson ObjectNav benchmark show that PreNav outperforms a strong online baseline under matched structural priors, using a generator trained only on room-level ScanNet++ scenes and applied to floor-level Gibson without fine-tuning. Real-world experiments under object relocation and open-vocabulary queries further validate the approach.

## 11. BiPOP: Partial-Order Planning for Long-Horizon Bimanual Robotic Manipulation

**作者:** Kun Chu, Xufeng Zhao, Jan-Gerrit Habekost, Cornelius Weber, Stefan Wermter

**分类:** cs.RO

**摘要:**

Bimanual robotic manipulation is versatile, yet its central challenge lies not only in executing dexterous motion with each hand, but also in coordinating the two arms for long-horizon manipulation under coupled spatial and temporal constraints. Prior work has largely focused on endowing robots with human-level manipulation skills, paying less attention to the structure of bimanual behaviour itself: many practical tasks are a coordinated composition of reusable unimanual skills and a small set of synchronous bimanual skills. ## 13. GHA-SmellFix: Taking Action with Agentic LLMs for Detecting and Refactoring GitHub Actions Workflow Smells

Continuous integration and continuous delivery workflows are key artifacts in software projects. In GitHub Actions, they encode build, test, release, deployment, and security automation in YAML. ## 14. A Theory of Policy Optimization in Post-Training: Coverage Collapse and Reference Resets

**arXiv ID:** [2611.14467v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14467v1)

**作者:** Audrey Huang, Nan Jiang, Sham M. Kakade, Sadhika Malladi, Akshay Krishnamurthy et al.

**分类:** cs.LG, stat.ML

**摘要:**

Policy optimization is central to language model post-training, yet principled guidance remains limited. We revisit its theoretical foundations to identify algorithms that learn near-optimal policies using total samples and gradient steps governed by the base model's response coverage, a measure of how readily it generates optimal responses. We call such algorithms coverage-preserving. This guarantee captures the goal of amplifying capabilities accessible through sampling from the base model. (1) Coverage collapse. REINFORCE, the basis for PPO and GRPO, can drive the probability of optimal responses to near zero, even as reward improves. This coverage collapse stalls optimization, and convergence can require exponentially many steps despite good initial coverage. The failure arises even with exact gradients and persists under KL regularization to the base model. (2) Reference resets. We show that repeatedly resetting the KL reference to the most recent policy mitigates coverage collapse. We formalize this intervention in Approximate Policy Mirror Descent (APMD), a meta-algorithm that approximately solves a sequence of KL-regularized RL subproblems. APMD enables stronger regularization, making each subproblem easier to solve, while reference resets allow convergence to global optimality. (3) Coverage-preserving algorithms. For softmax linear policies over pretrained features, we use APMD to establish the first global convergence guarantees showing that widely used families of post-training algorithms are coverage-preserving. These include stylized PPO and GRPO variants with explicit KL regularization and reference resets, and squared-loss algorithms that implement them implicitly, including those used to train the Kimi models. These results clarify how regularization, clipping, and objective choice affect the sampling and optimization costs of post-training.

**摘要:**

LLM applications increasingly rely on translators and compatibility layers to move conversations across APIs.
