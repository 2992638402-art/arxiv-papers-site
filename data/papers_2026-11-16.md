## 1. Personalization of Basic and Moral Values Guiding LLM-Controlled Assistant Robots

**arXiv ID:** [2611.18230v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18230v1)

**作者:** Mateusz Wozniak, Qi Gao, Agnieszka Wykowska

**分类:** cs.HC, cs.AI

**摘要:**

Personal assistant robots powered by large language models (LLMs) are likely to enter everyday life within the next decade, but the values that should guide their behaviour remain largely unspecified. Current alignment practice steers a single model toward an aggregate of human values, ignoring that individual users may prefer a robot whose value profile differs from their own. We administer parallel self- and robot-targeted versions of three established psycho-metric instruments - the simplified Schwartz Values Survey (SVS), the Portrait Values Questionnaire (PVQ-21), and the Moral Foundations Questionnaire (MFQ) - to potential future users (N=22), capturing both their personal values and the values they would want an assistant robot to embody. We compare the three instruments as personalization tools using the UEQ-S and benchmark fifteen contemporary LLMs against the resulting profiles. Participants wanted their robot to score markedly lower than themselves on personal-focus values and higher on social-focus values; moral profiles were nearly identical. LLMs prompted to act as robots shifted only weakly toward the desired profile, indicating that default model behaviour and prompt engineering are no substitute for explicit value personalization.

## 2. AutoRL: A Multi-Agent System towards Autonomous Reinforcement Learning

**arXiv ID:** [2611.17790v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17790v1)

**作者:** Simon Tang, Wenjie Shi, Yongjing Yin, Keheng Wang, Jialin Liu et al.

**分类:** cs.AI, cs.LG

**摘要:**

Recursive self-improvement (RSI) is one of the most promising paths toward an AI singularity for superintelligence. Achieving this vision requires AI systems to undertake the full training pipeline for iterative improvement. Reinforcement learning (RL) plays an essential role in this cycle to improve model capabilities and generalization, yet automating RL remains difficult due to unstable training dynamics and the need for sustained supervision. We introduce AutoRL, a multi-agent system for automating RL training, as a cornerstone for future RSI. Different from conducting research by end-to-end complete experiments, AutoRL monitors the evolving training state and integrates process-level supervision and research into an ongoing RL training, following the practical workflow of an RL researcher. Specifically, AutoRL consists of three RL-specific agentic roles. Perceptors track training dynamics, rollouts, evaluation, and efficiency, triggering deliberation when they detect anomalies, degradation, or stagnation. Three complementary Proposers diagnose problems and refine candidate interventions through shared evidence and checks. The Orchestrator reviews their recommendation and applies approved changes through online hot updates or checkpoint-based resumption. Experiments on Qwen3 and LongCat-Flash-Lite demonstrate the superiority of AutoRL over static RL recipes and a general agent. The research records show that existing agents can handle unattended RL supervision and dynamic adjustment. The difficulty for future RSI lies in developing fundamental methods that are valuable for resource-consuming validation.

## 3. MineralExp: Benchmarking LLM Agents for Experimental Design in Real-World Mineral Exploration

**arXiv ID:** [2611.17931v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17931v1)

**作者:** Bing Wang, Mingyang Wang, Yuanhang Mi, Yue Xu, Kaibo Su et al.

**分类:** cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.CE

**摘要:**

In mineral exploration, verifying geological hypotheses relies on designing a variety of geochemical experiments. While large language model (LLM) agents increasingly automate downstream geoscientific data analysis, a crucial upstream capability remains largely unexplored: automated geochemical experimental design, i.e., deciding which experiments to conduct before committing to costly laboratory work. Evaluating this capability of LLM agents is difficult for two reasons. First, geochemical experiments exhibit high methodological diversity and subjectivity: to test the same hypothesis, different geologists may choose different minerals and instruments, so no single protocol can serve as the reference answer. Second, open-ended experimental plans resist representation as structured labels, which makes objective scoring difficult. To address these challenges, we introduce MineralExp, a benchmark that reformulates geochemical experimental design as a hypothesis-based experiment selection problem, i.e., a multi-label classification problem. We abstract concrete instrument names and procedural details into a shared taxonomy of 19 experimental types across five mineral-system processes: Source, Transport, Deposition, Modification, and Preservation. This taxonomy yields deterministic multi-label targets while accommodating diverse experimental protocols. Constructed from published ore-deposit literature and verified by domain experts, MineralExp comprises 277 cases and 1,572 reference experimental plans. Given a geological hypothesis and initial context, an LLM agent selects the relevant tasks and generates concrete experimental plans, which are scored by multi-label classification metrics and an LLM-as-a-Judge metric. The experimental results show that current agents generally achieve high recall but low precision, tending to propose more experiments than are necessary to test the hypothesis.

## 4. RE-PRESENT: Evidence Triangulation across Alternative Candidate-Space Views for Efficient LLM Ensemble Inference

**arXiv ID:** [2611.14780v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14780v1)

**作者:** Juming Xiong, Weixin Liu, Kevin Guo, Congning Ni, Chongyu Qu et al.

**分类:** cs.MA

**摘要:**

Heterogeneous large language models (LLMs) exhibit complementary strengths, motivating routing and multi-model inference methods that seek to identify reliable predictions for each input. However, exploiting this complementarity remains challenging even when a correct prediction is available within the ensemble. We investigate this challenge across heterogeneous LLM ensembles and find that a simple single-round aggregation rule is highly competitive with learned routing methods, yet substantial oracle headroom remains. Further analysis shows that the evaluated single-round response signals provide limited discriminative power for identifying reliable predictions. In contrast, controlled re-presentations of the same question elicit complementary evidence about prediction reliability. Motivated by these findings, we introduce RE-PRESENT, a training-free framework that selectively re-presents disputed questions and directly aggregates candidate-level evidence across alternative views. Across seven multiple-choice benchmarks and two heterogeneous model ensembles, RE-PRESENT improves over strong single-round aggregation while offering a favorable accuracy--cost trade-off compared with self-consistency and multi-model debate.

## 5. Black-Box Uncertainty Quantification for Large Language Models via Ensemble-of-Ensembles

**arXiv ID:** [2611.18453v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18453v1)

**作者:** Wang Ma, Debarun Bhattacharjya, Junkyu Lee, Nhan H Pham, Harsha Kokel et al.

**分类:** cs.LG, cs.AI

**摘要:**

Reliable use of large language models (LLMs) requires uncertainty estimates that separate ambiguity intrinsic to a prompt from genuine knowledge gaps. Bayesian and deep-ensemble methods deliver this aleatoric/epistemic decomposition in principle, but are computationally prohibitive at LLM scale and require white-box access. Existing black-box methods, in contrast, summarize to a single scalar that cannot make the distinction. We introduce a two-level ensemble that bridges this gap. Stochastic decoding of a fixed prompt forms the inner ensemble; meaning-preserving semantic perturbations of the prompt form the outer ensemble. Variability is measured in a continuous embedding space, and the law of total covariance gives an exact within-/between-perturbation decomposition to estimate aleatoric and epistemic uncertainty (AU/EU); it requires only black-box sampling access. On top of this estimator, we contribute three theoretical results that ground the framework: (i) a finite-sample bias identity for nested Monte Carlo with a closed-form bias-corrected epistemic estimator; (ii) a Bayesian bridge bounding the gap between perturbation-population AU/EU and Bayesian targets; and (iii) a separation result identifying a class of failure modes that any consistency-only black-box method provably misses while perturbation-based EU detects. Across five short QA benchmarks and four instruction-tuned LLMs spanning two families and two scales, our estimator is competitive with strong white-box baselines, and at a matched generation budget it matches leading black-box methods on error ranking while decomposing AU and EU; controlled paired interventions show that AU and EU respond selectively to distinct, actionable sources of failure: prompt ambiguity and model knowledge gaps; and a long-form pilot on the NQ long-answer split shows the framework extends to claim-level factuality without retraining.

## 7. Vision-as-a-Tool: Visual Harness for Multimodal Agentic Coding

**arXiv ID:** [2611.17797v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17797v1)

**作者:** Haolin Yang, Wenshan Wu, Jin Jiang, Yu Cheng, Yan Xia et al.

**分类:** cs.AI, cs.CV

**摘要:**

Multimodal agentic coding requires robust visual reasoning to analyze observations and evaluate generated artifacts. Current frameworks largely rely on a Thinking-with-Images paradigm, where agents depend on their intrinsic vision to execute visually grounded tasks. However, this architectural coupling introduces a fundamental bottleneck. Agent performance remains strictly bounded by internal perceptual limitations. Furthermore, text-only agents lack native perception. Without specialized tools, they cannot perform visual reasoning. To overcome these constraints, we introduce the Vision-as-a-Tool (VaaT) paradigm. Prior visual tool-use merely manipulates images for an agent's intrinsic vision to read. In contrast, VaaT reframes general-purpose multimodal large language models (MLLMs) as callable external utilities. As a result, text-only agents can completely offload visual reasoning during agentic execution. Meanwhile, vision-capable agents can dynamically augment their native perception on demand. We instantiate this paradigm in VHarness, a unified framework driven by five atomic actions. We comprehensively evaluate VHarness across Image-to-HTML, Image-to-SVG, and Image-to-3D generation tasks. Experimental results demonstrate that VHarness consistently improves visual fidelity and geometric accuracy across both text-only and multimodal coding agents. These findings highlight that modularizing visual reasoning into callable utilities provides a flexible and scalable foundation for future agentic coding systems.

## 8. A Generalizable Approach to Safety-Aware Robotic Behavior Planning with an Explicit Constraint Space

**作者:** Xianxu Wu, Shang-Ching Liu, Zhaocong Liang, Hengxiang Chen, Yujian Ma et al.

**分类:** cs.RO, cs.LG

**摘要:**

Large language and vision-language models have substantially advanced robotic behavior planning from natural-language instructions and visual observations. However, safe manipulation in tabletop environments remains challenging because the safety constraints required for a task depend on the surrounding scene and may not be explicitly specified in the instruction. We propose a safety-aware behavior planning framework based on an explicit Constraint Space that stores reusable textual safety rules. Rather than retrieving rules solely by semantic similarity, we formulate constraint selection as task- and environment-conditioned applicability learning. A lightweight query-conditioned dual-tower model identifies the constraints applicable to the current context, which are then provided together with the original task and image to a multimodal behavior planner. Validated inspection feedback is further used for test-time adaptation of constraint applicability and parameter-efficient adaptation of the Safety Inspector through LoRA, while newly identified constraints are incorporated into the Constraint Space. The resulting framework integrates explicit safety knowledge representation,context-aware constraint selection, corrective safety inspection, and test-time knowledge adaptation for safety-aware robotic behavior planning.

## 10. Dancing Tokens on the Vocabulary Network from LLM Generation

**arXiv ID:** [2611.18248v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18248v1)

**作者:** Yuan-Chao Hu

**分类:** cs.AI, cond-mat.dis-nn

**摘要:**

Large language models (LLMs) generate text one token at a time, yet how a particular generation relates to the geometry learned in pre-training remains unclear. The fundamental explainability of LLMs is elusive. Here, we build a similarity network from the input embeddings of six open LLMs and map generation onto it. The token-embedding network is scale-free while heavy-tailed, as are random embeddings with matched covariance, but far more fragmented. Distillation barely changes this topology because input embeddings are largely inherited. The knowledge space is thus deterministic from pre-training. Consecutive generated tokens are rarely graph neighbours and, in most models, no more often than in shuffled order, so generation is not a walk on the predefined network, although the tokens it visits are concentrated within it. Through the logit lens in a layer- wise manner, tokens settle late in depth in most models, and repetitive models remain confined to small domain-specific sub-graphs. These observables could serve as inference-time monitors of degeneration. We thus provide a fresh perspective on understanding LLM generation in the microscopic way from node or link prediction by graph learning.

## 14. Disentangling Forgetting from Utility: Lightweight LLM Unlearning via Orthogonal Null-Space Projection

**arXiv ID:** [2611.17864v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17864v1)

**作者:** Xiuyuan Wang, Weiming Liu, Zongxin Yang, Fan Wang, Liemiao Jin et al.

**分类:** cs.LG

**摘要:**

Large Language Models (LLMs) pose significant security risks by retaining private or hazardous information, creating an urgent need for unlearning techniques that excise specific knowledge without compromising general utility. However, prevailing fine-tuning-based unlearning methods struggle to achieve this balance, often suffering from computational inefficiency and unintended utility degradation due to the entangled nature of knowledge within model parameters. To disentangle forgetting from utility, we propose UUE, a theoretically grounded framework that reformulates unlearning as controllable model editing. First, UUE projects parameter updates into the orthogonal left null space of retained knowledge representations, strictly ensuring that the editing process causes minimal interference to the model's general capabilities. Second, distinct from targeted mapping, we introduce a convex untargeted objective that derives a closed-form analytical solution to effectively disrupt undesired knowledge by pushing forget-set embeddings toward their antipodal points. We implement UUE via lightweight, pluggable adapters (including a low-rank variant, UUE-L), enabling efficient deployment without modifying the frozen backbone. Extensive experiments on TOFU and WMDP benchmarks demonstrate that UUE and UUE-L achieve superior unlearning efficacy, significantly outperforming existing methods.

## 15. f-Divergence Self-Play for Tabular Anomaly Detection via Large Language Models

**arXiv ID:** [2611.18370v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18370v1)

**作者:** Hoang Tran Vuong, Linh Ngo Van, Dang Nguyen, Thin Nguyen, Phuoc Nguyen et al.

**分类:** cs.LG, cs.CL

**摘要:**

Anomaly detection in tabular data poses significant challenges due to heterogeneous feature types-mixing numerical, categorical, and textual attributes, which complicate learning meaningful representations of normality. Recent work has applied large language models (LLMs) to this problem by serializing table rows as text sequences, yet these approaches rely on one-shot supervised fine-tuning that offers limited signal to tighten the model's description of normality. We propose DiSPaT, a self-play fine-tuning framework that strengthens the model's understanding of normal data. Building on the theoretical foundation of f-divergence minimization, we derive a tight approximation connecting our training objective to reducing the distributional gap between real normal data and model-generated samples. DiSPaT operates through an alternating optimization: at each iteration, the current policy generates synthetic samples that serve as pseudo-anomalies, while a critic discriminator learns to distinguish these from real normal samples; this signal drives policy updates that progressively align the model distribution with the true normal-data distribution. Extensive experiments on diverse benchmarks demonstrate that DiSPaT consistently outperforms prior LLM-based methods, deep learning approaches, and classical unsupervised detectors for tabular anomaly detection.

## 16. VEPO: Mitigating Multilingual Hallucinations in LVLMs via Visual Evidence-guided Preference Optimization

**arXiv ID:** [2611.18096v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18096v1)

**作者:** Chengpeng Fu, Xiaocheng Feng, Wenshuai Huo, Yichong Huang, Baohang Li et al.

**分类:** cs.CL

**摘要:**

Large vision-language models (LVLMs) achieve strong multimodal performance, yet they remain prone to object hallucination---generating answers that are linguistically fluent but visually ungrounded. This issue is especially severe in low-resource languages, where weaker cross-modal alignment causes models to rely more on language priors than on image evidence. Existing remedies either require costly multilingual annotations or external translation/reward models, or intervene at inference time in ways that can distort the output language. We propose VEPO(Visual Evidence-guided Preference Optimization), a training-time method that transfers reliable visual grounding from English to other languages without external models. VEPO first localizes key visual evidence using English queries via mid-layer cross-modal attention, then constructs preference pairs that share the same correct answer but differ in evidence sufficiency (full image vs.\ key-region masked image). Direct preference optimization on these pairs encourages low-resource inputs to prefer answers supported by complete visual evidence. Experiments on multilingual POPE and MME with Chinese, French, and Hindi show consistent hallucination reduction and perception gains, with the largest improvements on the lowest-resource language. Analyses further confirm that VEPO sharpens question-relevant attention and that evidence-guided masking outperforms random masking.

## 18. Before AI Agents Negotiate for Us: A Competitive Tournament of Rule-Based, Search, Optimization, Learned, and Language-Model Negotiators Under Scarcity

**arXiv ID:** [2611.15838v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15838v1)

**作者:** Eduardo de Avila-Armenta, Raúl Alejandro Aquino-Ibarra, Jorge Octavio Nicolás-Díaz, Umberto Leon-Dominguez

**分类:** cs.AI, cs.GT, cs.LG, cs.MA

**摘要:**

Automated bilateral negotiation underlies two-sided matching markets such as freight brokering, where many shippers and carriers negotiate price before a matching mechanism decides who is paired with whom. Prior strategy comparisons typically use isolated one-on-one duels or a single fixed market, so they cannot show how a strategy behaves against several live rivals under a changing balance of supply and demand. We built nine negotiation architectures spanning rule-based, search-based, optimization-based, imitation-learning, and reinforcement-learning families, and ran all nine simultaneously on both sides of a Gale-Shapley matching market under a balanced condition and two scarcity conditions rotated across all 36 exclusion pairs. In the balanced carrier market, an imitation-learned engine (ML2), a reinforcement-learning policy trained only on isolated negotiations (RL), and an expected-utility optimization engine (OPT) formed a statistically tied top group, and no architecture led in every condition and role. RL beat all eight carrier rivals when carriers were scarce, but its match rate collapsed from 62% to 16% when shippers were scarce and it had to compete with the other carrier agents for demand. A second reinforcement-learning policy trained against rival agents (RL2) kept its match rate above 91% in every condition, yet lost on realized payoff to all eight shipper-side rivals in two of the three conditions, traced to a confirmed train/deployment feature mismatch. We also tested nine large language models (LLMs), from 1 to 72 billion parameters, under the same protocol: they lost significantly to the two strongest engines (OPT2 and ML2) in 32 of 36 comparisons and won none. These results show that isolated or single-condition evaluation can hide failure modes that a many-sided, scarcity-varying, statistically corrected tournament exposes directly.

## 150. Electrocardiographic Imaging via Posterior Sampling under Operator Discrepancy

**arXiv ID:** [2611.17851v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17851v1)

**作者:** Ricarda Buttmann, Andreas M. Lehrmann

**分类:** cs.LG

**摘要:**

Electrocardiographic imaging (ECGI) reconstructs heart-surface potentials from body-surface potentials, an ill-posed inverse problem. Diffusion posterior samplers combine a learned prior with an explicit forward operator that they assume to be accurate. In ECGI this operator is only an estimate, because the body changes with respiration and posture, and body composition such as subcutaneous fat is rarely known for an individual patient. The resulting operator discrepancy is structured and depends on the heart signal. We introduce OpDis-PS, a diffusion posterior sampler that models this discrepancy instead of treating it as signal-independent noise. OpDis-PS learns how the discrepancy depends on the signal, folds this into a corrected forward operator, and reconstructs with a fixed prior across all operator conditions. We study two realistic sources of discrepancy, changing lung conductivity and an added subcutaneous-fat layer. Across both, OpDis-PS reconstructs more accurately than classical regularization and learned predictors, with its largest gains on held-out cardiac pathologies.
