## 📝 摘要

Robust motion planning and control for manipulators is the problem of guaranteeing safe execution and collision-free motion under disturbances. The Kinodynamic Motion Planning via Funnel Control (KDF) is a recently proposed planner that comes with robustness guarantees and is particularly attractive for collision-free control as it guarantees closed-loop evolution inside a funnel, i.e., a collision-free region around a nominal trajectory. Despite its attractive theoretical properties, the current approach for computing funnels is computationally demanding, which limits its expressiveness and performance. We address its current limitations by proposing a novel funnel planning and control solution. Our key tool to enable this is a neural Signed Configuration Distance Function (nSCDF) which allows us to estimate collision-free balls and gradients in a computationally efficient way. We use the nSCDF in our planner to compute a nominal trajectory, where path length and clearance are balanced. To track the trajectory with collision-avoidance guarantees, we design a funnel controller that exploits the geometric information from the nSCDF. From numerical experiments, we observe that our planner produces nominal paths $2$ times faster, while at the same time obtaining $20 \%$ shorter path length and $5$ times the clearance. Finally, we also observe improved motion performance and robustness properties when using the nSCDF for funnel control.
