## 📝 摘要

We adapt the diffeomorphic coarse-to-fine LapIRN architecture for longitudinal whole-body PSMA PET/CT registration in the Learn2Reg 2026 PSMAReg challenge. The method combines affine pre-registration, multi-modal deformable registration, and test-time instance optimization. We further introduce PET biomarker preservation losses for metabolic tumor volume (MTV) and total lesion glycolysis (TLG), including Jacobian-based constraints, and a per-structure rigidity regularization for skeletal structures. The submitted configuration achieved 81.0% DSC and 6.17 mm HD95, with MTV and TLG errors of 3.3% and 3.3%, respectively, on our evaluation protocol. Code is available at https://github.com/koegl/psmareg.
