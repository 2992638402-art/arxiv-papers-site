## 📝 摘要

Large Language Models (LLMs) have shown impressive capabilities across many tasks, including processing large amounts of text, following instructions, and generating ideas. This allows the use of such models in various contexts, including games and Tabletop Role-Playing Games (TTRPGs), such as Dungeons &amp; Dragons. However, these games include many complex components, from crafting interesting stories to adhering to rules, while allowing some freedom in players' actions. In this work, we first highlight key components that LLMs should exhibit in the context of TTRPGs. Then, we introduce our framework, CriticaLLM, a fully automated Game Master that can run entire TTRPG games, from creating diverse and interesting stories to managing exploration and combat phases. We evaluate our framework with actual TTRPG players and examine the diversity of generated plots and antagonists across four LLMs of varying sizes. Overall, our framework appears to be a promising option for running entire TTRPG games.
