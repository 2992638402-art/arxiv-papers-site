## 📝 摘要

We provide the first computationally efficient, differentially private algorithms for agnostically learning halfspaces. In particular, we study three different settings of agnostic learning: improper learning under Gaussian and log-concave marginals; proper learning under Gaussian marginals; and semi-agnostic learning under Gaussian marginals. In all three aforementioned settings, the added cost in the sample complexity due to privacy matches the respective non-private sample complexity, implying that we get privacy for free.
