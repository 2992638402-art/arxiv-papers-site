## 📝 摘要

We compute the VC-dimension of metric ball classifiers in the model spaces of Riemannian geometry (Euclidean, spherical, hyperbolic) and present upper and lower bounds on the VC-dimension of metric balls in several other situations, including general normed vector spaces (potentially infinite VC-dimension), vector spaces with $\ell^p$-norms (VC-dimension grows at least linearly in the dimension, faster than Euclidean for $p=1$), flat tori, and general Riemannian manifolds (arbitrarily large VC-dimension after a conformal change). In several situations where the VC-dimension is large or even infinite, we prove generalization bounds under additional assumptions on the data distribution using covering arguments. Both in the VC-dimension and covering setting, we consider data on low-dimensional subsets of high-dimensional Euclidean spaces. As an application, we consider coverage-based algorithms in active learning.
