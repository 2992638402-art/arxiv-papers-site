## 📝 摘要

We study how quickly the normalized Polyak--Ruppert average approaches its Gaussian limit in linear stochastic approximation with bounded i.i.d.\ coefficients. For step sizes decaying as $k^{-γ}$, with $1/2\leγ&lt;1$, we prove an upper bound of order $n^{γ-1}$ on the approximation error over convex sets in fixed dimension. A matching lower bound holds for every fixed model. The fastest rate in this range is $n^{-1/2}$, attained at $γ=1/2$. We also obtain an $O(n^{-1/2})$ bound on the error of the recursive multiplier bootstrap procedure.
