## 📝 摘要

We provide an efficient implementation of combinatorial Bernoulli factories. Such a factory turns coins of unknown biases $x$ into a random vertex of a polytope $P$ whose mean is exactly $x$. For every affine slice of the cube (the class of polytopes that admit a factory), we give a single algorithm whose expected number of coin flips and running time at every interior $x$ are polynomial in the dimension and in $1/{δ_*}$, where ${δ_*}=\min_i\{x_i,1-x_i\}$ is never supplied to the algorithm. For perfect matchings this gives $O(n^{8}{δ_*}^{-2}\log(n/{δ_*}))$ flips, while the factory of Niazadeh, Paes Leme, and Schneider [STOC 2021] spends at least $n^{2n-1}$ flips in expectation at the uniform doubly stochastic matrix. For other polytopes, our method rounds exactly given a point deep inside $P$ and the promise that $x$ is far from the boundary of $P$.
