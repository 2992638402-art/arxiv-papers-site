## 📝 摘要

In this paper, we propose a conditional asset pricing transformer (CAP-T) that captures nonlinear interactions among firms through attention to forecast excess returns. In the first stage, a transformer estimates conditional expected returns, and kernel-weighted principal component analysis of its residuals estimates the local factor loading space. Projecting the conditional mean onto this space separates factor risk compensation from conditional mispricing. Then, in the second stage, separate transformers learn these two components, whose predictions are combined to forecast returns. As a result, the proposed framework decomposes returns into conditional mispricing, factor risk compensation, a centered factor component, and an unpredictable innovation. We establish approximation and generalization bounds and characterize how first-stage prediction errors propagate through local factor estimation and component learning, providing statistical guaranties for conditional mispricing, factor loadings, and factor risk premia. Finally, simulations and empirical studies demonstrate the predictive and interpretive benefits of the proposed method.
