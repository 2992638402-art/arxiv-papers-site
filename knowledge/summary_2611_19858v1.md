## 📝 摘要

As globalization accelerates the demand for seamless cross-border enterprise collaboration, multilingual translation remains bottlenecked by insufficient domain-specific terminology, rigid terminology translation, weak instruction-following capabilities, and high inference latency in long-instruction scenarios. To overcome these barriers, we propose Bridging the Terminology Gap (BTG), a data-centric framework. First, BTG mines cross-lingual term alignments from domain documents and leverages LLMs to synthesize terminology-rich parallel corpora. Second, a hybrid filtering strategy combining heuristics and XCOMET scoring ensures high data fidelity. Third, a two-stage training paradigm employs supervised fine-tuning (SFT) for targeted terminology injection, followed by reinforcement learning (RL) to refine output preferences, effectively balancing domain knowledge integration with model reliability. Extensive offline evaluations (e.g., XCOMET, GEMBA, Acc@T) and online human assessments demonstrate significant improvements in terminology accuracy and instruction-following capabilities, validating the effectiveness of our framework, which is now deployed on our cross-border collaboration platform.
