## 📝 摘要

Exemplar-free continual learning seeks to minimize joint risk over all seen tasks, yet historical data are unavailable for direct optimization. We observe that, for classification, preserving the model's decisions on previously learned classes helps retain old knowledge. This motivates Decision-Preserving Regularization (DPR). Penalizing changes in all pairwise old-class logit margins reduces algebraically to a centered relative-logit penalty. We further apply spectral whitening to remove the classifier's spectral scale bias. Because the global classifier supplies the decision constraints for the next stage, it must remain calibrated across all seen classes despite the drift of stored old-class statistics during representation updates. We therefore fit a closed-form affine map from paired current-task features to transport old-class Gaussian means and covariances into the updated feature space, then calibrate the global classifier using these distributions and current-task features. Across CIFAR-100, Tiny-ImageNet, ImageNet-Subset, and ImageNet-1K under cold-start protocols, comparisons with state-of-the-art methods show that DPR achieves higher accuracy in most settings while remaining highly efficient. Code is available at https://github.com/bnufw/dpr.
