## 📝 摘要

Generating consistent multi-view RGB images and normal maps from a single input image provides complementary appearance and geometric cues for 3D reconstruction. However, maintaining cross-view consistency requires information exchange across viewpoints, and dense cross-view attention becomes computationally expensive as the number of views and spatial resolution increase. In this paper, we propose Global-Local Proxy Attention (GLPA) to reduce cross-view communication costs while preserving fine-grained within-view processing. Specifically, GLPA retains full-resolution interactions within each view and uses compact global proxies for cross-view communication. To further leverage the exchanged information, GLPA incorporates iterative proxy refinement, which rebuilds the proxies from updated features and reuses the same attention parameters for subsequent interactions within a single denoising step. We evaluate GLPA on two structurally different diffusion backbones, FLUX and Stable Diffusion. Experiments on FLUX show that GLPA achieves a $2.65\times$ speedup in attention computation per denoising step over dense attention, while improving reconstruction IoU from 0.6849 to 0.7019 and reducing NVS LPIPS from 0.086 to 0.076. Results on Stable Diffusion further demonstrate the effectiveness of GLPA, with a $1.80\times$ attention speedup and improvements across all five quality metrics.
