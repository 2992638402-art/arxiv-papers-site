## 📝 摘要

We show that efficient and high-precision quantum associative retrieval is possible with an exponential complexity advantage with respect to the classical brute force approach and with a polynomial capacity advantage with respect to classical neural network implementations for any number of patterns scaling polynomially in the number of qubits. Both classical neural and quantum associative memories store the patterns in a Hamiltonian; in the classical case the dynamics is irreversible relaxation toward attractors, in the quantum case it is unitary transformations generating an effective thermal distribution, followed by a measurement.
