## 📝 摘要

The rapidly expanding generative AI landscape brings together methods that differ in architecture, learning objective, mathematical representation and sampling procedure. A concise, selectively organised technical guide can provide an accessible starting point for developing a coherent understanding of this increasingly complex landscape. This article takes a generation-centred approach, connecting how models produce new observations with the modelling and learning principles that make generation possible. We develop a two-level perspective: first, distinguishing three recurring generation routes-direct transformation or decoding, sequential conditional construction, and iterative updating or transport; and second, examining the modelling and learning principles underlying these routes. Representative approaches are introduced through their key components, learning objectives and sampling procedures. We then examine their broader connections, including how different architectures and generation mechanisms can overlap or be combined. By bringing together essential technical explanations within a compact conceptual framework, the article aims to help readers develop a working understanding of unfamiliar approaches, recognise connections among models they have encountered, and navigate different approaches within the broader generative AI landscape.
