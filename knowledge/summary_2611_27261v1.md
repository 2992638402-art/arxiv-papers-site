## 📝 摘要

Can structural information improve estimation of entropic optimal transport (EOT), and when is the gain zero? We study a fixed-dimensional exponential density ratio model (DRM) with a nonparametric baseline, compact support, and fixed positive regularization. At positive limiting sampling fractions, an exact projection identity quantifies the variance gain from the restriction. The pooled empirical-likelihood estimator attains the restricted bound and supports asymptotically valid confidence intervals when the bound is positive. Even under correct specification, the gain can be zero and can shrink as the tilt space expands. The same projection residual determines direction-specific first-order bias under local misspecification. When both samples grow and the target fraction vanishes, a zero first-order tilt derivative can leave source fluctuations and quadratic tilt error as the leading terms. Their balance gives a phase transition in the limiting distribution. A continuous example gives different convergence rates for two correctly specified bases at the same distribution pair. Controlled experiments assess the fixed-proportion theory. Natural-data comparisons distinguish variance reductions from improvements in reference RMSE driven by smaller reference bias.
