## 📝 摘要

Ant Colony Optimization (ACO) is a family of metaheuristics inspired by the pheromone-based navigation of real ants, a decentralized mechanism often associated with shortest-path discovery. Over the past two decades, research has primarily focused on optimization tasks such as vehicle routing within a network. In this paper, we shift this perspective by using swarm intelligence to gather diagnostic information about the underlying network structure itself. Through decentralized exploration, we harness stigmergic dynamics to introduce a novel topological and behavioural metric: the Stigmergic Index. While sharing baseline structural properties with traditional betweenness centrality on simple grids, our index accounts for path polarization effects that can diverge from shortest-path-based assumptions.
