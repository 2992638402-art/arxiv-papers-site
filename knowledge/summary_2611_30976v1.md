## 📝 摘要

We study nonparametric regression with limited labeled data and abundant observations carrying potentially inaccurate pseudo-labels. Our proposed method, weighted prediction-powered regression via calibration (wPPRC), first calibrates pseudo-labels using labeled data, then fits a predictor by weighted regression using observed and calibrated responses. Over Hölder classes, we derive nonasymptotic excess-risk bounds for clipped ReLU estimators and establish a minimax lower bound for the corresponding regression problem. Under suitable conditions, the upper and lower bounds match up to logarithmic factors. Moreover, wPPRC can achieve a worst-case risk of smaller order than the minimax risks under four baseline observation structures on the same joint distribution class, demonstrating the value of additional pseudo-labels. We also construct a validation-selected (VS) estimator using an independent labeled validation sample to choose the weight. The VS estimator satisfies an expected-risk oracle inequality and preserves the improvement over the baseline minimax risks with the same total labeled-data budget. Simulations and an application to predicting homeless counts illustrate the gains of the proposed estimators.
