## 📝 摘要

Clustering longitudinal health trajectories requires handling high-dimensional measurements, irregular visit times, and subject-level heterogeneity while retaining clinically interpretable structure. Existing methods address this only partially: latent-class mixed models yield an interpretable, covariate-driven partition but do not scale past a handful of markers, while deep time-series clustering scales at the cost of that structure and generally requires a common time grid. We introduce the Latent Class Mixed Model Variational Autoencoder (LCMM-VAE), a deep generative model whose latent prior is a mixture of class-specific linear mixed models, so that each subject's latent trajectory decomposes into its class mean trajectory and a subject-specific deviation. We derive a variational EM algorithm whose class responsibilities, random effects and mixed-model parameters are updated in closed form, and provide identifiability guarantees under stated conditions. Experiments on simulated and real longitudinal cohorts show that LCMM-VAE returns a partition, a reconstruction of incomplete trajectories and interpretable class-level dynamics at once, while remaining competitive with classical and deep baselines.
