## 📝 摘要

Generative driving planners may produce trajectories that appear feasible at their reference points but violate environmental constraints over the full vehicle body. We propose FeaXDrive2, an end-to-end generative planning framework that enhances vehicle-embodied spatial feasibility (VESF) through body-scene spatial grounding. The proposed method constructs spatial queries from actual vehicle-body corners and dynamically updates visual sampling locations using evolving trajectory estimates across Flow steps. Retrieved local visual features condition subsequent Action Flow-DiT velocity predictions, allowing trajectory generation to guide spatial information retrieval. Rollout-Consistent Safety Fine-Tuning (RC-F) combines high-risk trajectory supervision with reference behavior preservation, followed by planning-supervised VLM-Action Alignment. Vehicle-geometry counterfactual analysis reveals safety differences under identical trajectories but varying vehicle geometry. On NAVSIM, FeaXDrive2 reduces at-fault collisions by 36.5% relative to the base planner and improves PDMS from 87.0 to 88.8. Under NAVSIM v2 evaluation, EPDMS increases from 86.5 to 88.3. Ablation and body-clearance analyses further demonstrate the safety benefits of body-scene spatial grounding.
