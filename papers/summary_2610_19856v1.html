Lost in the Fallback: How Reliably Do Typed Decision Models Use Fallback Options?

arXiv ID: 2610.19856
分类: cs.CR, cs.AI
发布时间: 2026-10-14

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Yuanbo Xie, Mingli Zhu, Tianyun Liu, Yingjie Zhang, Haixiao Xiong, Tingwen Liu
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Typed decision models (TDMs) are increasingly used in applications such as request routing and model selection, making reliable decisions critical to system performance. Fallback options accommodate categories outside regular choices, enabling deferral to human experts or more capable models. While recent works have examined adversarial vulnerabilities in TDMs, their ability to follow fallback rules in non-adversarial settings remains underexplored. We introduce the Paired Fallback Robustness (PFR) Test to evaluate two aspects of fallback reliability: whether models correctly switch between regular and fallback options, and whether they make consistent decisions under logically equivalent definitions of the same rule. We evaluate open-source decision models on three benchmarks, with controlled comparisons across architectures and parameter counts. We find that correct recognition does not reliably transfer to correct fallback options, and errors persist even when the correct category is explicitly supplied.Moreover, logically equivalent formulations of the same fallback rule can also produce substantial disagreement in model decisions. These findings reveal a crucial reliability gap in current TDMs: fallback mechanisms designed to improve reliability can instead introduce errors. It highlights faithful execution of fallback rules and consistency under equivalent definitions as essential requirements for reliable typed decision-making.
