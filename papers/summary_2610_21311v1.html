First Mathematical Runtime Analyses of Evolutionary Multitask Multi-Objective Optimization

arXiv ID: 2610.21311
分类: cs.NE
发布时间: 2026-10-15

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Danyang Zhang, Weijie Zheng
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

To facilitate the solving of complex problems, evolutionary multitask optimization is regarded as a promising technique due to its capacity for cross-task knowledge transfer. In contrast to empirical evidences, no mathematical runtime support exists for this technique within the domain of multi-objective optimization. In this work, we aim to fill this important gap. We analyze how the representative multiobjective multifactorial evolutionary algorithm (MO-MFEA) covers the Pareto fronts of two bi-objective problems simultaneously. For the two-task problem of maximizing the classic bi-objective OMM and the multimodal OJZJ benchmarks, the MO-MFEA requires only an expected runtime of $O(n^2 \log n)$---the same as optimizing the simple OMM alone---yet achieves a speedup by a factor of $Θ(n^{k-1}/\log n)$ compared to optimizing OJZJ alone (with gap size $k$). Similarly, for the task pair comprising the classic LOTZ and the multimodal DLTB benchmarks, a speedup of $Θ(n)$ is attained over optimizing DLTB alone, while the runtime matches that of optimizing LOTZ alone.
