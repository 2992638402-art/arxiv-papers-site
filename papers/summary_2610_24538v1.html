Accelerating Finite-Sum Bilevel Optimization with Fully First-Order Oracles

arXiv ID: 2610.24538
分类: math.OC, cs.LG, stat.ML
发布时间: 2026-10-17

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Qihao Zhou, Shanshui Niu
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

We study nonconvex--strongly-convex bilevel optimization with finite-sum objectives and fully first-order oracles. We propose Sampled Implicit Corrections for Bilevel Optimization (SICBO), which combines a penalty approximation with variance-reduced corrections for the lower-level variables. Under componentwise lower strong convexity, SICBO finds a point $x$ satisfying $\mathbb{E}|\nablaΦ(x)|^2\le\varepsilon^2$ within $\tilde{O}(N+\sqrt{N}κ^{7/2}\varepsilon^{-2})$ component-gradient calls, where $N$ is the total number of components and $κ$ is the normalized lower-level condition number. The bound achieves square-root finite-sum dependence using only gradients and preserves the best known deterministic fully first-order condition-number exponent for smooth problems. Our improvement follows from a curvature-dependent bound for nonlinear correction errors, which permits epochs of length $\tildeΘ(\sqrt{N/κ})$ with batches of size $\tildeΘ(\sqrt{Nκ})$. A single-level reduction shows that the dependence on $N$ and $\varepsilon$ is optimal up to logarithmic factors for fixed $κ$.
