LIBERO-Pro2.0: A Benchmark for Evaluating the Robustness of Embodied Foundation Models

arXiv ID: 2610.24587
分类: cs.RO
发布时间: 2026-10-17

📝 摘要

LIBERO has emerged as a widely adopted benchmark for evaluating robotic manipulation policies. However, strong performance on LIBERO does not necessarily indicate robust embodied capabilities, as models may exploit benchmark-specific regularities rather than generalize to diverse operating conditions. In this work, we introduce LIBERO-Pro2.0, a comprehensive benchmark for systematically evaluating the robustness of embodied foundation models under distribution shifts. LIBERO-Pro2.0 encompasses 22 static perturbations and 20 runtime interventions across six dimensions: Condition, Environment, Observation, Execution, Robot State Configuration, and Language, yielding 1,648 validated shifted task instances. To support robustness-oriented learning, we further collect 39,390 training trajectories through model-generated and human demonstrations. All shifted task instances undergo six rounds of iterative human review and correction to ensure physical plausibility and task validity. We evaluate 17 representative robot policies spanning vision-language-action models, world-action models, and robustness-oriented policies. Our results reveal substantial robustness gaps across all evaluated paradigms: under runtime distribution shifts, the evaluated models exhibit an average performance drop of 26.5 percentage points, with individual drops reaching 32.5 points. These findings highlight the gap between nominal benchmark performance and robustness under distribution shifts, underscoring the need for systematic robustness evaluation of embodied foundation models.
