When Better Policies Leave Harder Failures: Coverage Shift in VLM Success Monitoring

arXiv ID: 2610.24801
分类: cs.RO
发布时间: 2026-10-17

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Isabel Guan, Dingyuan Zhang, Shipeng Lyu, I-Ming Chen
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Can a better robot policy become harder to monitor? We study a fixed vision-language model (VLM) that judges success while a policy improves. In a controlled 20-seed tray-placement experiment, training selects only simulator-verified successes. Success rises from 66.8% to 97.0%, and accepted hover failures fall from 5.0% to 0.9% of executions. Yet the judge accepts 50.0% of remaining hovers, versus 31.1% under untrained sampling. These residual failures more often lie below the tray rim, which can conceal the gap beneath a grasped cube. A separate boundary probe accepts 148/160 below-rim hovers and 0/160 above-rim hovers. We call this change in conditional monitoring sensitivity coverage shift: learning changes which failures reach a fixed judge. A broader diagnosis covers nine judges and 1,392 stimuli. Judge-filtered experiments across eight settings further show heterogeneous effects of state-dependent acceptance. The results distinguish task improvement from failure detectability and motivate reporting absolute misses, conditional misses, and audit coverage together.
