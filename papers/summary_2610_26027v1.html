A Spiking Ring-Attractor Architecture for Representation and Control of Revolute Robot Joints

arXiv ID: 2610.26027
分类: cs.RO, cs.NE
发布时间: 2026-10-19

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Aung Htet, Alejandro Rodriguez Jimenez, Ruidong Ma, Alessandro Di Nuovo
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Robotic systems with revolute joints evolve on circular state spaces, yet most control architectures represent these states using Euclidean variables that ignore this underlying geometry. We propose a fully spiking neural control architecture in which ring attractors encode joint states, enforcing representations consistent with the topology of revolute systems. Homeostatic regulation computes goal-state mismatch directly over these distributed representations, and opponent gain-modulated populations convert this mismatch into controlled phase shifts of the activity bump, implementing set-point regulation as motion on the ring. We show that the bump dynamics follow an SO(2) geometry, and that gain asymmetries induce a signed phase velocity on the ring. To interface distributed activity with scalar regulation signals, we derive an analytic Fourier phase decoder and implement its signed components using push-pull spiking populations. For multi-joint systems, we demonstrate that local ring-based Fourier features compose into product representations that linearly decode SO(3) orientations through forward kinematics. Experiments in NEST validate the full SO(2) regulation mechanism and the spiking product-feature decoder for lift, pitch, and yaw. Robot experiments on a Tiago arm show that decoded ring dynamics generate joint velocity commands, producing directed motion with joint-dependent accuracy. Together, these results establish a neuromorphic control framework in which representation, regulation, and action are unified within a ring-attractor substrate aligned with the geometry of revolute systems.
