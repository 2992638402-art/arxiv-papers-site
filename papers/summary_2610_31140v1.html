GM-GReFEL: Geometry-Guided Spatiotemporal State-Space Learning for Robust Dynamic Facial Expression Recognition

arXiv ID: 2610.31140
分类: cs.CV, cs.LG
发布时间: 2026-10-22

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Yudhistira Arditya Pratama, Yi-Zeng Hsieh
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Recognizing facial expressions in unconstrained video suffers from two coupled problems: self-attention grows quadratically in cost with clip length, and crowdsourced labels are noisy. We present GM-GReFEL, a spatiotemporal state-space network with a geometry-aware decision stage. Its Modulated GroupMamba backbone replaces a spatiotemporal Vision Transformer. Six channel groups are scanned separately, four over the frame plane and two over time, so the cost grows linearly with sequence length ($\mathcal{O}(N)$); after each layer, a Channel Affinity Modulation gate mixes the groups again. On top of the backbone, a GReFEL reliability module turns the normalized entropy of each prediction into a mixing weight between the classifier output and a vote from trainable geometric anchors, so that facial geometry counts for more when the network is unsure. The model is initialized by static-to-dynamic weight transfer and trained with a Triple Loss. In five-fold cross-validation, the best fold scores 66.57%/76.46% UAR/WAR on DFEW, 0.3 points or less below the strongest compared method, and 45.66%/62.27% on MAFW, ahead of all compared methods; on FERV39k the mean over five splits, 42.30%/52.66%, is competitive. The five-fold means are 64.07%/74.54% on DFEW, on par with recent large pre-trained models, and 38.90%/54.37% on MAFW, below the best of them. A paired $t$-test on DFEW indicates a modest benefit of temporal modeling (+1.61 UAR points, one-sided $p=0.031$). Attention stays on plausible facial regions, and latency is close to a ViT-B/16 baseline (47.4 vs.\ 46.8 ms per clip). Code: https://github.com/ardiytama/GroupMamba-DFER.
