Acquisition for Spherical-Linear Bayesian Optimization on the Cube

arXiv ID: 2610.32628
分类: stat.ML, cs.LG, stat.CO
发布时间: 2026-10-23

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Nathan Wycoff
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Recent work demonstrated that executing Bayesian Optimization (BO) using simply a linear surrogate with a clever transformation is enough to approximate the performance of the standard Gaussian-Process (GP) based version on high-dimensional test functions. When the decision region is a sphere, this strategy admits rapid exact acquisition. However, it is the unit cube we encounter most often in the BO literature, and in this setting a standard approach, namely a multi-start quasi-Newton method, is employed for solving the acquisition subproblem. In this article, we will discuss a series of results, both positive and negative, regarding the complexity of global resolution of various acquisition subproblems over the unit cube. On the basis of this discussion, we will propose a new acquisition function which is globally optimizable using significantly less execution time than the incumbent approach while maintaining or improving performance. The resulting method is competitive with GP-based BO in terms of accuracy per evaluation, and is superior in terms of accuracy per walltime on black-boxes of low to moderate computational intensity.
