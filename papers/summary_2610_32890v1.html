Closing the Modality Gap via Simplex-Constrained Representations

arXiv ID: 2610.32890
分类: cs.AI, cs.CV
发布时间: 2026-10-23

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Shubham Gupta, Siva Reddy, Perouz Taslakian, Valentina Zantedeschi, Cem Subakan
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

While multimodal contrastive models like CLIP and CLAP successfully learn shared representations, their embeddings typically exhibit a persistent \emph{modality gap}. We propose addressing this separation through a purely geometric approach: changing the embedding domain from the unit sphere, $S^{d-1}$, to the probability simplex, $Δ^{d-1}$. Although both manifolds share the same intrinsic dimension, the simplex acts as a structural prior; its nonnegative, unit-mass constraints serve as a cross-modal calibration that forces both modalities to ground themselves in the same coordinate system. By attaching lightweight softmax adapters and using Total Variation similarity, we reduce the centroid $\ell_2$ gap by 97--99% across five multimodal retrieval benchmarks. This confirms that simplicial geometry induces genuine semantic alignment, matching or exceeding the retrieval performance of standard unconstrained models.
