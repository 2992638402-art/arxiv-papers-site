El Agente Quntur: Agentic Active-Space Selection for CASSCF

arXiv ID: 2610.38145
分类: physics.chem-ph, cs.MA
发布时间: 2026-10-28

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Juan B. Pérez-Sánchez, Viviana Roman-Ventura, Olalla Nieto-Faza, Aiwei Yin, Alán Aspuru-Guzik, Varinia Bernales
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Multireference calculations often require substantial human expertise and time to choose and refine an active space for the electronic states or chemical process of interest. This involves selecting which orbitals to include, verifying that the calculation describes the intended excited state, and deciding whether the results have converged. Here, we ask whether scientific agentic systems (harnesses) can make these decisions reliably. We use the El Agente Quntur multi-agent harness, with GPT-5.6 Sol and GPT-5.6 Terra as the underlying LLM components, to explore 32 diverse singlet excitations at fixed molecular geometries. The agents planned and executed calculations, choosing and refining active spaces under prompts that specified equal-weight two-singlet state-averaged CASSCF followed by strongly contracted NEVPT2 with def2-TZVPD. We evaluate excitation energies against published CASPT2 reference values at matching geometries and compare performance with six deterministic Active Space Finder protocols. Within the El Agente Quntur system, agents detect missing active orbitals, compare candidate excited states, and revise state assignments as calculations progress. Agentic execution reduced mean absolute error by 48% relative to the state-of-the-art Active Space Finder protocol. We note that computational traces vary substantially across runs in both active space selection strategies and cost, even for the same molecule. Our results underscore the advantage of agentic active space selection over fixed workflows, yet leave room to improve efficiency through runtime-aware decision-making, reduced inter-agent communication overhead and redundancy, more systematic checks of state identity, and clearer stopping criteria.
