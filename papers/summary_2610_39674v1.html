Formalizing the Intrinsic Dimension of Objective Landscapes by a Generalized Notion of Co-dimension

arXiv ID: 2610.39674
分类: stat.ML, cs.LG, math.MG
发布时间: 2026-10-29

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Ernesto Araya, Gitta Kutyniok, Weilin Li, Johannes Maly, Manjot Singh
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

In this work, we formalize the concept of intrinsic dimension of objective landscapes introduced in Li et al. (2018), which measures the structural complexity of learning problems by training along randomly generated subspaces. We define the intrinsic dimension of a set $Θ\subset\mathbb{R}^p$ as the minimal dimension $d_{\mathrm{int}}(Θ)$ such that a random plane of dimension $d_{\mathrm{int}}(Θ)$ in $\mathbb{R}^p$ intersects $Θ$ with probability one. We show that it is a generalized notion of co-dimension that depends on the global geometry of $Θ$. We study mathematical properties of the intrinsic dimension and develop techniques to estimate the intrinsic dimension of sets $Θ$ that arise in learning problems. More specifically, we derive upper and/or lower bounds where $Θ$ is the set of optimal parameters in student-teacher settings with shallow and deep ReLU networks, including finite and infinite sample cases, and with and without regularization. We discuss how our theory leads to novel types of generalization bounds, thereby showing that the intrinsic dimension is a measure of learning complexity. Our results connect techniques from seemingly different areas of machine learning research such as classification of symmetries of ReLU networks and stochastic geometry.
