Closing the Log Gap: Minimax-Optimal Regret for Episodic RL

arXiv ID: 2610.40165
分类: cs.LG, stat.ML
发布时间: 2026-10-29

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Guy Zamir, Victor Boone, Matthew Zurek, Yudong Chen
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

In this paper, we study the regret minimization problem in episodic tabular MDPs. Our algorithm achieves the first expected regret bound scaling as $\sqrt{H^3 S A K}$ for sufficiently large $K$, where $H$ is the episode length, $K$ is the number of episodes, and $S$ and $A$ are the numbers of states and actions, respectively. Our method is the first to match the minimax lower bound \emph{without extra polylog factors}. We introduce a variant of UCBVI which does not maintain strictly optimistic estimates, and uses MOSS-style exploration bonuses whose logarithmic terms adapt to both visit counts and estimated variances. A key challenge is controlling the propagation of errors caused by under-optimism, which we accomplish in an occupancy-weighted way, avoiding logarithmic overhead that would be incurred by uniform-over-rows concentration. Our analysis develops time-uniform and variance-aware concentration bounds that may be of independent interest. As a corollary we also develop an algorithm for $(\varepsilon, δ)$-PAC best-policy identification, which is the first to match the $Ω\big(H^3 SA\log(\frac{1}δ)\varepsilon^{-2}\big)$ minimax lower bound up to constants in some regimes.
