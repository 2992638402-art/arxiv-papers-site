Near-Optimal First-Order Regret for Bandits with Feedback Graphs

arXiv ID: 2610.40861
分类: cs.LG, stat.ML
发布时间: 2026-10-29

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Mengxiao Zhang, Yuheng Zhang, Haipeng Luo, Paul Mineiro
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

We study first-order regret guarantees for adversarial bandits with a fixed, known directed feedback graph. For strongly observable graphs, [ACBDK15] establishes a minimax regret of $\widetildeΘ(\sqrt{αT})$, where $α$ denotes the independence number of the graph and $T$ denotes the horizon. On the other hand, the best existing first-order bound scales as $\widetilde{O}(\sqrt{(κ+1) L^\star})$ [LLZ20], where $κ$ denotes the clique partition number, satisfying $κ+1\geqα$, and $L^\star$ denotes the cumulative loss of the best arm, leaving a gap between the graph dependence in worst-case and first-order guarantees. In this paper, we bridge this gap by obtaining an expected regret bound of $\widetilde{O}(\sqrt{αL^\star}+α)$. Our first result is an algorithm for the special case of self-aware graphs, in which every vertex has a self-loop. The algorithm combines the update of EXP3 [ACBFS02] with a sampling rule inspired by exploration by optimization [LS20]. More specifically, we construct a map whose fixed points yield sampling distributions that control the one-round regret remainder in terms of the learner's loss and the graph's independence number, which is crucial in deriving optimal first-order guarantees. We further show that a sufficiently accurate fixed point can be computed in polynomial time by a geometrically convergent iteration. Finally, we extend the algorithm to general strongly observable graphs and achieve the same $\widetilde{O}(\sqrt{αL^\star}+α)$ guarantee by adapting the two-level aggregation approach of [LLZ20].
