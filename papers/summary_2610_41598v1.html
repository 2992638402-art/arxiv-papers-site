Scout-Refine-Correct: Teaching Flow Models to Refine Their Own Samples

arXiv ID: 2610.41598
分类: cs.CV
发布时间: 2026-10-30

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Sergey Karpukhin, Aibek Alanov
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

The quality of image generation models trained with diffusion or flow matching is largely fixed by the training budget: at inference, the only available lever is the number of sampling steps, and additional steps improve quality only up to a point. We present a training recipe, Scout-Refine-Correct (SRC), that instead gives a flow-matching generator a genuine inference-time refinement capability. At each sampling step the model takes a scout rollout -- $K$ steps ahead along its own trajectory -- extrapolates a full-sample estimate, and feeds that estimate back to itself as a self-conditioning signal before taking the real step. This can be repeated for $R$ refinement rounds. Two correction losses, applied during training at states the model's own rollouts actually reach rather than only on the ground-truth path, teach the model to use this signal to reach new samples instead of collapsing to a suboptimal one. On ImageNet-256, SRC improves LightningDiT-B from FID14.5 to FID7.26 at NFE550, matching a privileged best-of-$N$ baseline that uses an oracle FID verifier to choose among samples. SRC uses no external verifier, no reward signal, and, after ablation, almost no extra parameters over the base architecture. Combining SRC with best-of-$N$ improves the result further, to FID3.81 at NFE~4400, showing that the two axes are complementary. We ablate the main design choices -- the architectural pathway for the scout signal, the scout horizon, each correction loss, and the training schedule to demonstrate key working components of training.
