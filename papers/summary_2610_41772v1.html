WorkIng: A Real-World and Synthetic Highway Work-Zone Benchmark Dataset for Autonomous Navigation

arXiv ID: 2610.41772
分类: cs.CV
发布时间: 2026-10-30

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Xujun Xie, Dominik Rößle, Adithya Mohan, Daniel Cremers, Torsten Schön
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Highway work zones are critical edge cases for autonomous vehicles (AVs), combining atypical geometries and jurisdiction-specific regulations that existing benchmarks fail to capture: real-world datasets lack 360° multi-modal, full-scene 3D work zone annotation, and no single recorded site can span the layouts and regulatory variants needed for generalizable models. We present WorkIng, a dual-domain benchmark and training resource. WorkIng-Highway provides a 360° multi-modal work zone benchmark with synchronized 128-channel LiDAR, six RGB cameras, and RTK-GPS across 32 km, yielding 12,000+ annotated frames and 227,399 tracked 3D bounding boxes. WorkIng-CARLA complements this with procedural simulation spanning 24 regulation-aware scenarios across six canonical topologies and six international traffic standards, populated by 100+ custom 3D assets. Benchmarking representative 3D detection and end-to-end planning architectures shows that real-world data remains the primary performance driver, and that scenario-stratified synthetic augmentation improves architectures across 2D recognition, 3D detection, and trajectory planning. Resources: https://github.com/cvims/WorkIng
