SpikeAD: Toward Multiplication-Free Spike-Controlled Neuromorphic Architecture for Energy-Efficient Small Object Detection

arXiv ID: 2611.00112
分类: cs.NE
发布时间: 2026-10-28

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Zhifan Song, Haralampos-G. Stratigopoulos
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Energy-constrained aerial platforms require accurate object detection under strict power and latency budgets, yet conventional inference remains bottlenecked by multiply-accumulate (MAC) operations. While spiking neural networks (SNNs) offer bio-inspired efficiency, many SNN detectors retain hybrid MACs in intermediate layers, or use spike-driven inference where multi-level spikes are interpreted as repeated binary events. We propose SpikeAD, a direct-trained spike-controlled neuromorphic aerial detector. In SpikeAD, sparse bounded integer spikes act strictly as multiplexer control signals for multiplier-free shift-add inference, preserving spike boundaries throughout a four-scale bidirectional feature pyramid. We introduce the Spike-Gated Multi-Scale (SGMS) block, performing receptive field expansion with activity-driven channel selection, implementable with adders and comparators. Evaluated on VisDrone and DOTA and across Nano, Small, and Medium variants, SpikeAD achieves up to 46.2% mAP@50 on VisDrone (+4.8% over SNN prior art), and generalizes to oriented bounding box (77.6% mAP@50 on DOTA) using the proposed SpikeOBB head. Under the Horowitz 45nm arithmetic energy model following standard methodology in SNN literature, SpikeAD achieves up to 18.9x estimated reduction relative to an equivalent MAC-based implementation. We propose the Spike Processing Core (SPC), an RTL compute primitive to validate hardware realizability for reconfigurable and scalable custom accelerator chip design on FPGA/ASIC, highlighting a promising path toward next-generation ultra-low-power neuromorphic processors for aerial object detection.
