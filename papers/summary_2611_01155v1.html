Lower Bounds on Inference Complexity for Polyak-Ruppert Averaged Stochastic Gradient Descent

arXiv ID: 2611.01155
分类: stat.ML, cs.LG, math.OC, math.ST
发布时间: 2026-10-31

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Denis Belomestny, Alexey Naumov, Sergey Samsonov
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

In this paper, we establish sharp lower bounds for Gaussian approximation of Polyak-Ruppert averaged stochastic gradient descent with polynomially decreasing step sizes. For every fixed exponent $γ\in(1/2,1)$, a single one-dimensional smooth, strongly convex example has approximation error with the limiting variance of order $n^{1/2-γ}+n^{γ-1}$ in Kolmogorov and convex distances. This matches the existing upper bound throughout the range of $γ$. The nonlinear bias contributes $n^{1/2-γ}$ even when the Gaussian reference uses the finite linearized variance. We also derive a lower bound of order $n^{-(1-γ)/2}$ for the relative mean error of estimating the limiting variance from one SGD trajectory, uniformly over a scalar quadratic family. With the limiting variance known, achieving approximation error $\varepsilon$ on this example takes order $\varepsilon^{-4}$ iterations for the best choice $γ=3/4$. Achieving both Gaussian approximation and relative mean variance-estimation errors at most $\varepsilon$ uniformly over the model class requires at least order $\varepsilon^{-6}$ iterations for every fixed $γ$.
