HuG: Learning Humanoid Whole-Body Affordance-Aware Grasping with Active Perception

arXiv ID: 2611.01659
分类: cs.RO
发布时间: 2026-11-01

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Xuanbin Peng, Lai Wei, Yuze Zhai, Qingyuan Yu, Keyi Wang, Jianglong Ye, Xiaolong Wang
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Acquiring objects in human environments is hard for humanoids: targets are partially occluded under egocentric viewpoints, and reaching them demands coordinated whole-body motion that keeps the target visible and reachable while the base stays stable. We present HuG, a framework for whole-body affordance-aware grasping with active perception that couples perception and action through a Perceive-Align-Grasp pipeline: belief-driven active perception resolves occlusion by treating viewpoint as an explicit decision variable; a point-cloud-conditioned affordance policy proposes whole-body grasps; and an affordance-aware alignment stage repositions the body to the most executable configuration. A key insight is that, on a floating base, kinematic validity is not enough: we curate training data with controller-in-the-loop dynamics simulation, rejecting 73% of kinematically valid grasps that would destabilize the deployment controller, so the policy learns kinodynamically feasible affordances. Trained entirely in simulation with zero real demonstrations, HuG attains 87.5% real-world success on a multi-level shelf task, generalizes to novel objects, and holds at least 73% success at every tested height. Beyond generalizing within its training distribution, the policy extrapolates spatially: at scale in simulation it succeeds at table heights well below any seen during training, indicating that kinodynamically filtered synthetic data grounds affordances in object geometry and whole-body feasibility rather than in memorized configurations.
