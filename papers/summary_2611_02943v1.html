Exact Fusion of Softmax Attention Heads Can Require Exponentially Many Atoms

arXiv ID: 2611.02943
分类: cs.LG, cs.NE, stat.ML
发布时间: 2026-11-02

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Hiroyasu Usami, Keisuke Hara, Ayato Tsuboi, Naohiko Matsuda
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Fusing independently normalized attention heads can increase the number of explicit key-value entries, or atoms, needed to preserve their output. We prove a sharp separation for $W_d(q)=\sum_{j=1}^dσ(q_j)$: its $d$ two-atom heads use $2d$ head-atom terms, whereas its minimum single-head representation has exactly $2^d$ atoms, even with arbitrary fixed replacement keys, scalar values, and positive masses. The same lower bound holds for every fixed uniform error below $1/2$ on $\mathbb R^d$, and the threshold is sharp. The proof uses exposed-key normal cones; exact equality on any nonempty open query domain inherits the bound. The resulting atom bound for a fixed head count is tight when that count divides $d$. A normalized family retains its exponential exact minimum while approaching a constant on fixed-radius query balls. For pretrained-model caches, a classical affine-rank bound and a certified instance exclude exact vector preservation at a specified atom count. Learned-cache comparisons on two disjoint banks of 64 contexts show budget-dependent reversals and calibration effects.
