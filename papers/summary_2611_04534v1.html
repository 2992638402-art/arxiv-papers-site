A Universal Raw-IQ Radar Encoder for Cross-Sensor Human Sensing

arXiv ID: 2611.04534
分类: cs.CV, cs.LG
发布时间: 2026-11-03

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Jonas Leo Mueller, Dario Zanca, Sebastian Hoefler, Jan Petermann, Thomas Altstidl, Naga Venkata Sai Jitin Jami, Emmanuelle Salin, Bjoern M. Eskofier
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Radar-based human sensing typically relies on end-to-end task-specific models trained on hand-chosen signal projections, which generalize poorly across unseen users, tasks, and hardware. We argue that the dominant signal processing choice, the micro-Doppler spectrogram, is a poor starting point for transfer because it keeps the Doppler axis, which carrier frequency rescales, and discards the angular axis, which carrier frequency leaves unchanged. We instead pretrain a convolutional beta-VAE without labels on clutter-removed raw radar measurements from four platforms spanning 60 and 77 GHz and 4 to 12 virtual antennas, aligned by a unified spectral interpolation grid. For every downstream benchmark the encoder stays frozen, and only a small supervised temporal classifier is trained on its embeddings. We compare against supervised models trained end to end on each benchmark and a pretrained radar foundation model across ten settings covering activity, gesture, sign-language, and gait recognition. When the test users and the radar are seen during training, our encoder is best on all five benchmarks. For users never seen in training, it leads on three of four benchmarks, by 4.8 to 23.2 points. On subject-disjoint gait recognition, it is best for each of the four held-out subjects and 8.3 points above the strongest baseline on average, even though its pretraining excluded every recording of this radar. Excluding the test radar from pretraining costs at most 5.3 points on any benchmark. Code is available at https://github.com/jonasmueler/UniversalRadarEncoder_WACV.
