FARR: Forecast-Aware Residual Refinement with Matched Endpoint Evidence for Long-Horizon Time-Series Forecasting

arXiv ID: 2611.07870
分类: cs.AI
发布时间: 2026-11-05

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Yiyang Zhang, Yuli Zheng, Bin Li, Meimei Zhang, Qiang Sun
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Large language models use auxiliary conditioning to incorporate additional information into time-series forecasts. However, auxiliary conditioning does not consistently improve accuracy and can increase forecasting error when its influence is poorly matched to the observed temporal context. We propose Forecast-Aware Residual Refinement (FARR), which learns to correct forecasts using endpoint evidence. FARR obtains a valid forecast under the standard condition and a null forecast under a predefined null condition. Their difference reveals how conditioning changes the prediction. A shared residual refiner combines this endpoint evidence with historical features to estimate the remaining error of the valid forecast. Horizon-wise gating then controls the contribution of the predicted residual to the final output. Experiments on four long-horizon forecasting benchmarks show that FARR achieves the best dataset-average MSE and MAE among the compared methods. Code will be released at https://github.com/blossomrichzyy-star/farr-forecast-aware-residual-refinement.
