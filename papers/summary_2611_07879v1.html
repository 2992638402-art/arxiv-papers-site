Network Effect Decomposition for Causal Effect Estimation under Network Interference

arXiv ID: 2611.07879
分类: stat.ML, cs.AI
发布时间: 2026-11-05

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Zhirong Huang, Wenzhen Zhang, Guangquan Lu, Boyan Chen, Debo Cheng, Shichao Zhang
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Estimating causal effects from observational network data requires accounting for both individual treatment and neighborhood exposure. Existing outcome models commonly incorporate these intervention variables into a joint predictor and estimate causal effects through prediction contrasts, leaving the corresponding response components implicit. We propose the $\underline{N}$etwork $\underline{E}$ffect $\underline{D}$ecomposition $\underline{Net}$work (NEDNet), which parameterizes the conditional outcome response as a query-invariant pre-treatment baseline plus separate treatment- and exposure-response pathways. Path-specific neighborhood contextualization supplies local pre-treatment information to the two pathways. We characterize the fitted response through a unique reference-normalized decomposition, which is equivalent to a zero mixed treatment--exposure contrast. Experiments on semi-synthetic benchmarks derived from BlogCatalog and Flickr show that NEDNet achieves the lowest PEHE among the compared methods for individual, peer, and total effects across homogeneous and covariate-heterogeneous response settings.
