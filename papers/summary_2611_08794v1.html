High-Dimensional Off-Policy Evaluation without Sample Splitting using an Onsager Correction

arXiv ID: 2611.08794
分类: stat.ML, cs.LG, math.ST
发布时间: 2026-11-05

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Saksham Jain, Guillaume Braun, Masaaki Imaizumi
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Reward models used for off-policy evaluation (OPE) are commonly selected using held-out data. When sample size and context dimension grow proportionally, the difference in test risk between models fitted on the full sample and on a fixed fraction of it need not vanish. Held-out evaluation can therefore consistently estimate the test risk of the model trained on the smaller sample, but because the relative ranking of candidate reward models can change after full-data refitting, held-out selection can be suboptimal. We develop an Onsager-corrected, target-policy-weighted criterion that estimates the full-data reward model's out-of-sample risk under the target policy using only its training observations. This enables early stopping along the full-data gradient-descent trajectory without sample splitting. For neural networks with fixed depth and hidden width under Gaussian contexts, single-index rewards, context-dependent multi-index target policies, and known context-independent logging, we prove uniform consistency of our criterion over a fixed finite set of gradient-descent iterates and a near-oracle guarantee for the selected reward model. The same correction yields a doubly robust estimator that consistently estimates the true policy value without sample splitting, even when reward-model prediction error does not vanish. Experiments show decreasing risk-estimation error as dimension grows at a fixed sample-to-dimension ratio, and improved reward-model selection relative to held-out baselines. Empirically, our correction also reduces the root mean squared error of doubly robust value estimates computed from the same data used to fit and select the reward models.
