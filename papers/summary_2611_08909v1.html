Geometry-Aware Score-Repellent Monte Carlo

arXiv ID: 2611.08909
分类: stat.ML, cs.LG
发布时间: 2026-11-06

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Jie Hu, Lingyun Chen, Do Young Eun
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

The recently proposed Score-Repellent Monte Carlo (SRMC) method improves MCMC sampling by tilting the target density $π$ in $\mathbb{R}^d$ with the factor $\exp(-α,θ_n^\top s(x))$, where the score function $s(x) = \nabla_x \log π(x)$, and $θ_n$ is the score average from past samples. However, a single scalar repellence strength $α\ge0$ cannot handle both the steep and the flat directions in anisotropic energy landscapes at once. We introduce \emph{Geometry-Aware SRMC} (GA-SRMC), which generalizes the Euclidean alignment to $θ^\top M s(x)$ for any symmetric positive-definite matrix $M$. A stochastic-approximation central limit theorem under a trace constraint on $M$ identifies the trace-normalized inverse Fisher $M_{\rm opt}\propto S^{-1}$ as the unique minimizer of the worst-direction covariance bound, elevating the Fisher information $S=\text{Cov}π(s,s)$ from a proposal-side preconditioner (e.g., FisherMALA, natural gradient) to the bound-optimal repulsion-side matrix, with a single online estimate $\widehat S_n$ serving both roles at no additional computational cost. The full-Fisher online metric $M_n$ converges almost surely to $M{\rm opt}$ and satisfies a CLT, providing a theoretical guarantee for the online implementation of the GA-SRMC framework. On an $8$-target simulation spanning the condition number $κ\in[1.3,100]$ and dimension $d\in[2,785]$, GA-SRMC dominates SRMC in ESS by $\boldsymbol{4.3\times}$ on anisotropic Gaussian ($κ=100$) and $\boldsymbol{6.8\times}$ on MNIST under the MALA baseline, by $\boldsymbol{16\text{--}32%}$ on three well-conditioned logistic-regression posteriors, and by up to $\boldsymbol{+48%}$ under FisherMALA; gains peak on the most anisotropic targets, and posterior-mean MSE is lower than with SRMC in all $16$ full-Fisher implementations.
