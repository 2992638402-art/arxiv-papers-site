Selective Cooperative Perception via Blind Spot Reasoning and Agentic Dialogue with Vision-Language Models

arXiv ID: 2611.10819
分类: cs.RO, cs.AI, cs.CV, cs.MA
发布时间: 2026-11-07

  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Real-time cooperative perception (CP) in multi-agent traffic environments is highly resource-intensive, which posing scalability challenges for large-scale deployment. Efficiency can be significantly improved by selectively performing CP only in safety-critical scenarios and for key occluding objects. Vision-Language Models (VLMs) are well-suited for complex reasoning in dynamic and uncertain environments, making them a promising tool for this selective CP task. This study proposes a VLM-driven agentic framework for blind spot reasoning and dialogue-based CP. A mixed-model architecture is designed that integrates conventional object detection for stable perception, vision-language reasoning for identifying impactful occlusions, and a physics-based geometric module to align reasoning with real-world visibility constraints. The framework also incorporates a Chain-of-Thought (CoT) prompting mechanism to enhance interpretability and intent-aware decision-making. We collect real-world data from connected vehicles operating in urban traffic and compare the driving-critical blind spot identification and dialogue performance of four state-of-the-art VLMs. Experimental results demonstrate that our method achieves over 80% accuracy in driving-critical blind spot detection and over 90% success in dialogue-driven perception sharing. This work provides a scalable, interpretable, and bandwidth-efficient alternative to conventional CP pipelines, and offers new insights into agentic communication design for future connected traffic systems.
