Communication-Efficient Federated Multi-View Clustering via View-Aware Quantization

arXiv ID: 2611.15270
分类: stat.ML, cs.LG
发布时间: 2026-11-11

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Khadija Ardif, Meryeme Ayache, Nidal Nasser, Ouiame Marnissi
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Federated multi-view clustering (FedMVC) has recently attracted increasing attention for its ability to exploit complementary clustering structures across multiple clients while preserving data privacy. However, existing FedMVC methods often suffer from high communication overhead due to the repeated transmission of high-dimensional representations, which limits their scalability in practical federated systems. Moreover, different views may contribute unequally to the clustering performance, calling for adaptive communication strategies. In this paper, we propose a view-aware quantization algorithm for communication-efficient FedMVC. By dynamically assigning quantization bits based on iteratively updated view importance scores, the proposed algorithm effectively balances clustering performance and communication efficiency under a global bit budget constraint. To validate its generality, we integrate our approach into four state-of-the-art FedMVC methods. Extensive experiments on multiple benchmark datasets demonstrate that view-aware quantization achieves up to 6.59x communication reduction while maintaining and in some cases surpassing baseline clustering performance, confirming the effectiveness and scalability of the proposed approach.
