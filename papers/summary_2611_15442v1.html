Sharp Gaussian approximation for Polyak--Ruppert averaged linear stochastic approximation

arXiv ID: 2611.15442
分类: math.PR, math.ST, stat.ML
发布时间: 2026-11-11

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Bogdan Butyrin, Eric Moulines, Alexey Naumov, Sergey Samsonov
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

We study how quickly the normalized Polyak--Ruppert average approaches its Gaussian limit in linear stochastic approximation with bounded i.i.d.\ coefficients. For step sizes decaying as $k^{-γ}$, with $1/2\leγ<1$, we prove an upper bound of order $n^{γ-1}$ on the approximation error over convex sets in fixed dimension. A matching lower bound holds for every fixed model. The fastest rate in this range is $n^{-1/2}$, attained at $γ=1/2$. We also obtain an $O(n^{-1/2})$ bound on the error of the recursive multiplier bootstrap procedure.
