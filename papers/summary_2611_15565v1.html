Generalized Inverse Kinematics via Conditional Flow

arXiv ID: 2611.15565
分类: cs.RO
发布时间: 2026-11-11

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Kaitlyn Zhang, Tomás Lozano-Pérez, Leslie Pack Kaelbling
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Inverse kinematics (IK) is a foundational primitive in robotics, mapping the task-space objectives expressed by higher-level planners to robot configurations that realize them. Traditional IK methods generally treat each query as an isolated pose-to-configuration problem, but planners often need a distribution over valid realizations that accounts for future reachability and preferences such as collision avoidance and stability. We introduce GenGenIK, a generative modeling approach that supports mobile bases, multiple end-effectors and targets, scene awareness, and preferences over robot behavior within a common interface. Experiments show that GenGenIK outperforms existing methods in both solve rate and runtime across tasks such as bimanual lift and flip-up, while allowing flexible control over the sampled distribution and remaining effective under partial scene observations.
