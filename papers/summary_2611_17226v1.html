Sheaf-Inspired Structured Scoring Extensions of LightGCN for Collaborative Filtering

arXiv ID: 2611.17226
分类: cs.IR, cs.CE, cs.CG, cs.LG
发布时间: 2026-11-12

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Ksenia Belkovskaya, Timur Lidzhiev, Maria Stebliy, Alexander Tarakanov
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Graph-based collaborative filtering learns user and item representations by propagating embeddings over a user--item interaction graph. We investigate a modular, sheaf-inspired scoring extension placed on top of a three-layer LightGCN backbone. The motivation comes from cellular sheaves, where local vector spaces are related by linear restriction maps; here, this idea motivates structured, pair-dependent transformations of user and item embeddings rather than a change to graph propagation. We compare the identity inner-product scorer, diagonal, rank-one low-rank, and full matrix residual scorers, and a parameter-matched residual MLP baseline (\texttt{mlp_full}) on Amazon-Book, Gowalla, MovieLens-1M, and Yelp2018. We report ranking quality and catalog coverage at cutoffs 10 and 20. The selected structured variants show modest, dataset-dependent changes in ranking quality and sometimes substantial changes in coverage. Our method is inspired by sheaf geometry but does not construct a cellular sheaf or implement sheaf-Laplacian diffusion; the experiments therefore do not establish a causal benefit from sheaf geometry itself.
