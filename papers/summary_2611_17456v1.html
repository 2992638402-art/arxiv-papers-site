Physical-Risk-Aware Decentralized Agents for Constrained Vehicle Communications

arXiv ID: 2611.17456
分类: eess.SP, cs.MA
发布时间: 2026-11-12

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Haoran Peng, William Weijia Zheng, Hang Liu, Ying-Jun Angela Zhang
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Decentralized V2X resource control is challenging because each vehicle has only local observations, while reliability depends on network-wide interactions. We develop a physical-risk-aware spatio-temporal graph attention (PA-STGAT) framework for agentic V2X control. Each vehicle perceives its local interaction graph, retains short-term temporal context, and independently selects its PC5 resource and power. Network outcomes are used during centralized training to refine the shared policy. Transmitter-specific credits map reliability failures to individual vehicles. A dual-priced graph term further converts network violations into coordination signals for discrete resource selection, with higher priority on short time-to-collision links. Experiments show higher raw-policy reliability than the tested graph-based baselines. Under filtered execution, PA-STGAT meets the nominal empirical communication requirements across all five seeds, with tradeoffs among utility, delivery reliability, resource occupancy, and filter-induced repair. Compared with GNN+MATD3, it requires fewer resource repairs but achieves lower utility and mean delivery reliability. Mechanism ablations show that removing dual-priced graph coordination causes resource-selection collapse. Density and mobility tests characterize the operating limits of empirical constraint satisfaction.
