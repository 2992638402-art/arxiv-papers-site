Collective Tree Exploration with Predictions

arXiv ID: 2611.17482
分类: cs.DS, cs.MA
发布时间: 2026-11-12

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Romain Cosson, Yichen Huang, Michael Mitzenmacher, Ali Vakilian
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

We study collective tree exploration with subtree-size predictions. In this problem, a team of $k$ mobile agents needs to explore an unknown rooted tree as fast as possible, and whenever a new vertex $u$ is discovered, the team receives a prediction $\hat n_u$ of the number of nodes $n_u$ in the subtree rooted at $u$. We provide algorithmic guarantees under two error measures: the maximum error $E=\max_u|\widehat n_u-n_u|$ and the multiplicative distortion $μ= (\max_u\widehat n_u/n_u)(\max_u n_u/\widehat n_u)$. For the maximum error, we present a deterministic algorithm that explores any tree with $n$ nodes and depth $D$ in $O(\text{OPT}+E)$ rounds, where $\text{OPT} = Θ(n/k+D)$ is the offline benchmark, and we show that there is no deterministic algorithm that admits an $O(\text{OPT})+o(E)$ guarantee. For the multiplicative distortion, we present several algorithms that induce a competitive ratio in $\widetilde O(\min{ \sqrtμ\log k, μ\log\log k+μ^{1.5} })$, where the $\widetilde O(\cdot)$ may hide logarithmic factors in $μ$.
