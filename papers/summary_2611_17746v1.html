Complex Schrödinger Bridges

arXiv ID: 2611.17746
分类: cs.LG, stat.ML
发布时间: 2026-11-13

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Dong-Sig Han, Tolga Birdal
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Diffusion bridges have emerged as versatile frameworks for facilitating transformations between arbitrary data distributions. However, these approaches are hindered by an immense optimization space and its underlying high-order structures. This paper explores a variant of diffusion bridges for solving real-world problems in complex vector spaces governed by scaling and rotation laws, such as Hamiltonian structures. To address this, we introduce conformal optimal transport (COT), a complex-valued transportation framework designed for physical systems with geometric and dissipative dynamics under a geometric lifting. Building on this, we propose a new class of dynamical diffusion bridges, called complex Schrödinger bridges (CSBs), whose derived algorithms can handle diffusion-based tasks using a conformal geometric prior. For scalability, we propose image-to-image CSB (I$^2$CSB), a tractable diffusion-bridge technique with an analytic spiral posterior. Finally, we implement a physics-informed CSB algorithm within simulations of quantum electrodynamics, mimicking the logical behavior of quantum logic gates. Our extensive results validate the underlying theory while highlighting advantages of complex-valued diffusion.
