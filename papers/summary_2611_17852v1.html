PADSNet: Physics-Aware Dual-Stream Network with Biomechanical Priors for Laryngeal Lesion Classification

arXiv ID: 2611.17852
分类: cs.CV
发布时间: 2026-11-13

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Zhenzhen You, Weifeng Yu, Zhenghao Shi, Shuangli Du, Minghua Zhao, Yingcheng Zhao, Xinhong Hei, Demin Kong, Xiaoyong Ren, Jin Hou
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Precise classification of laryngeal lesions is clinically crucial yet highly challenging, as inter-class visual differences are often subtle. While abnormalities in vocal fold vibratory dynamics provide complementary discriminative information, existing deep learning models predominantly rely on visual appearance and lack explicit mechanisms for modeling clinically meaningful vibratory dynamics. To address this, we propose PADSNet, a Physics-Aware Dual-Stream Network for laryngeal lesion classification that integrates visual representations with explicit biomechanical reasoning. Specifically, a Wavelet-Attention Enhancement Module (WAEM) is designed to bridge the spectral-semantic gap, enabling complementary learning of spatial texture and dynamic frequency representations. Meanwhile, a Knowledge Computing Module (KCM) extracts interpretable biomechanical indices from temporal glottal distance waveforms, including indicators of mobility, symmetry, glottal closure, and vibration stability. These descriptors are subsequently incorporated into a Physics-Aware Gating Module (PAGM) as biomechanical priors to adaptively recalibrate visual feature toward pathology-relevant vibratory patterns. Extensive experiments on a clinical dataset demonstrate that PADSNet achieves an accuracy of 0.8452, an F1-score of 0.8090, a sensitivity of 0.7953, and a specificity of 0.8866. These results indicate that explicitly integrating biomechanical priors with deep visual representations provides a more accurate, robust, and clinically interpretable framework for automated laryngeal lesion classification.
