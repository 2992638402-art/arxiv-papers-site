Causal AI-Driven Secure mmWave Beam Alignment with Defensive Variational Autoencoders

arXiv ID: 2611.18321
分类: cs.AI
发布时间: 2026-11-13

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Nasir Khan, Asmaa Abdallah, Abdulkadir Celik, Ahmed M. Eltawil, Sinem Coleri, Cicek Cavdar
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Efficient and secure beam alignment is essential for mmWave communication systems. However, existing deep learning (DL)-based beam alignment methods often neglect causal relationships between inputs and outputs, resulting in limited interpretability, poor generalization, and excessive beam sweeping. Moreover, these DL solutions are vulnerable to adversarial attacks, where small perturbations in received power measurements can mislead beam classifiers. To overcome these issues, we propose a causally-aware DL framework integrating causal discovery into beam alignment. A two-stage causal beam selection algorithm learns a Bayesian graph capturing dependencies between received signal strength indicators (RSSI) as inputs and the optimal beam index as the target variable, then uses it to select a minimal set of relevant input features for the DL classifier. To mitigate adversarial attacks, we introduce a defensive variational autoencoder (DVAE) that maps potentially perturbed inputs back to their clean versions. End-to-end fine-tuning of the downstream beam classifier on the DVAE-reconstructed RSSI representations further boosts prediction accuracy. Simulation results show that causal beam selection achieves accuracy comparable to conventional methods while providing an (\mathbf{829\times}) lower per-sample computational cost than SHAP (Shapley Additive Explanations), a widely used explainable AI (XAI)-based feature selection method. It further reduces beam-sweeping overhead by (\mathbf{59.4%}) and (\mathbf{89.8%}), compared to conventional DL-based beam selection and exhaustive search, respectively. The DVAE-based defense effectively denoises adversarial inputs, sustaining 85% accuracy across different perturbation bounds, retains baseline performance on unperturbed RSSI inputs and surpasses defensive distillation defense scheme by 15--20% on average under different attack scenarios.
