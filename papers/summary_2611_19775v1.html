Recovery-Guided Model-Based Diffusion for Safety-Critical Legged Locomotion

arXiv ID: 2611.19775
分类: cs.RO
发布时间: 2026-11-15

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Lei Zheng, Shivam Sood, Mithun Vanniasinghe, Guillaume Sartoretti, Armin Lederer
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Safety-critical high-speed legged locomotion requires motion planners to balance safety foresight with online computational efficiency. Although short prediction horizons reduce rollout costs, they provide limited safety foresight, and strict safety constraints severely restrict the feasible trajectory space. We propose a recovery-guided model-based diffusion (MBD) framework for safety-critical legged locomotion. It incorporates a learned terminal recovery critic into short-horizon trajectory optimization. Specifically, the critic approximates the worst-case future clearance margin along rollouts of a designated recovery policy. We introduce a feasibility-guided refinement strategy that combines within-horizon constraints and terminal recoverability at each sampling stage, while retaining feasible elites to mitigate sampling-support loss. Across 50 paired randomized Unitree Go2 obstacle-avoidance trials, our approach achieves zero collisions and 98% strict-clearance success, while requiring substantially less computation than long-horizon MBD baselines. A project page is available at https://zack4417.github.io/rg-mbd/.
