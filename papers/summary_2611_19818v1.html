Drone Navigation for Fast, Safe Emergency Response

arXiv ID: 2611.19818
分类: cs.RO
发布时间: 2026-11-15

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Pranav Nedunghat, Jeffrey Mao, Praveen Merumugumala
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

In 2025, there was a total of 358 weather-related disasters worldwide affecting millions of people. For unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) to autonomously aid these people, they need to be able to navigate around complex obstacles and reach their destinations to deliver supplies or quickly locate survivors in disaster-stricken areas. Our research addresses this need by providing a method to compute minimum-time trajectories in real time while providing guarantees of obstacle avoidance along the trajectory. Previous approaches treat the problem of computing time-optimal trajectories without considering obstacle avoidance. Our work not only includes obstacle avoidance constraints, but also improves overall mission time for navigation. We define mission time as the sum of planning time and traversal time. In time-sensitive emergencies, rapid trajectory planning is essential to ensure the prompt delivery of critical medical aid. As such, we consider the combined planning and traversal time as the key metric for how quickly the vehicle can reach a survivor.
