Sharp Gaussian Approximation for Q-Learning

arXiv ID: 2611.19850
分类: stat.ML, cs.LG, math.ST
发布时间: 2026-11-15

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Xingtu Liu
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Gaussian approximation provides a foundation for online statistical inference in reinforcement learning. We establish sharp non-asymptotic Gaussian approximation rates in Wasserstein distance for asynchronous Q-learning with Polyak-Ruppert averaging. We prove an upper bound of $\widetilde{O}(n^{-1/4})$, where $n$ is the number of iterations, together with a matching lower bound up to logarithmic factors. Q-learning is more challenging to analyze than linear stochastic approximation due to the nonlinearity of the Bellman operator. Asynchronous updates introduce further challenges due to Markovian noise. Our analysis separates the errors from Bellman nonlinearity, Markovian noise, and the finite-sample covariance discrepancy. In particular, we show that Markovian noise induces an unavoidable bias even when the Bellman operator is linear. The trade-off between this bias and the covariance discrepancy determines the convergence rate. We further extend our analysis to the synchronous setting and establish a sharp rate of $\widetilde{O}(n^{-1/2})$.
