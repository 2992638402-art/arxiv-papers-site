From Companion to Guardian: Designing an Emergency Support System Using a Quadruped Robot for Older Adults

arXiv ID: 2611.19932
分类: cs.RO, cs.HC
发布时间: 2026-11-15

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Chunyang Meng, Eduardo B. Sandoval, Josh Andres, Francisco Cruz, Mari Velonaki
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

As older adults increasingly live alone, emergency support becomes an important function for home companion robots. In Australia, demand for the care and support workforce is projected to nearly double between 2020-21 and 2049-50. Service robots such as robot dogs may support older adults by combining everyday companionship with assistance when human support is not immediately available. We present the design and implementation of a domestic companion-robot system on a Unitree Go1 quadruped platform that combines companionship with emergency awareness and response. The system uses a three-stage interaction model consisting of companion, danger-awareness, and emergency modes. A rule-based escalation policy combines signs detected from visual and speech input to determine the current risk level and adjust how long the robot waits for the user to confirm safety before entering emergency mode. Additional detected signs can shorten this waiting time. Users can confirm safety, request immediate help, stop robot motion, or reset the system. We verified the implemented decision rules and escalation timing through 105 automated trials and 25 staged physical trials. Additional visual evidence shortened the waiting time, and the system entered emergency mode when the waiting time ended without confirmation.
