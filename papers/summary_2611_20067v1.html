World-Model-Guided Plan Search for Cooperative Mixed-Traffic Control at Bottlenecks

arXiv ID: 2611.20067
分类: cs.MA
发布时间: 2026-11-15

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Jinghan Peng, Ning Liu, Xiangrui Kong, Sen Shen, Dongkun Han
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Cooperative speed control by connected and automated vehicles can reduce congestion at lane drop bottlenecks. However, effective coordination depends on vehicle roles and execution timing, while initial holding costs may be offset by later traffic benefits. This paper proposes an offline plan search framework that combines world model guidance with simulator verification. A traffic world model incorporating physical dynamics guides evolutionary search over vehicle roles, target speeds, holding durations, and start times. Paired SUMO simulations screen candidates for total delay reduction and limits on additional mean delay across vehicle groups. Accepted plans form a reusable library whose actions temporarily override a frozen MAPPO policy. Across 192 paired test scenarios at 60% penetration and a demand of 2,800 vehicles per hour, the searched library reduces mean total delay by 14.3% relative to MAPPO and by 3.8% relative to a handwritten plan library. The largest increase in group mean delay is 6.2 s, below the prescribed 10 s limit. In a separate screening experiment, the guided search achieves a 30.0% pass rate compared with 2.6% for random sampling. These results support the use of predictive search and independent verification for discovering cooperative plans under group delay constraints.
