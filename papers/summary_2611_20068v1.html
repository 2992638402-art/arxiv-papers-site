Kolmogorov-Arnold Lookup Table Demappers for Amplitude Phase-Shift Keying over Nonlinear Satellite Links

arXiv ID: 2611.20068
分类: eess.SP, cs.LG
发布时间: 2026-11-15

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Kürşat Tekbıyık, Güneş Karabulut Kurt, Antoine Lesage-Landry
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Next-generation high-throughput satellites increasingly demodulate and decode on board, so the per-symbol cost of the receiver constrains payload power and throughput. Soft demappers for nonlinear satellite links must therefore produce accurate bit log-likelihood ratios (LLRs) at low cost. We introduce a KAN-LUT demapper, a two-layer Kolmogorov--Arnold network (KAN) whose learned univariate edge functions are stored as 128-entry lookup tables (LUTs), so that inference needs only table reads, linear interpolation, and additions. We bound the LLR error introduced by the tables and evaluate the demapper on amplitude phase-shift keying (APSK) links with low-density parity-check (LDPC) coding and a nonlinear traveling-wave-tube amplifier. For 16-, 32-, and 64-APSK, KAN-LUT stays within 0.2 dB of a per-cluster Gaussian log-MAP (LogMAP-CG) demapper, while its cost relative to LogMAP-CG falls from 87% to 27% as the order grows. It matches or slightly exceeds multilayer perceptrons (MLPs) at about one fifth of their cost, remains competitive under operating-point mismatch, and needs only tens of kilobytes of tables. KAN-LUT is therefore a low-cost candidate demapper for regenerative payloads.
