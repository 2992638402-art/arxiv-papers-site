High-Throughput Serving of Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe with Regime-Aware Megakernels and Kernel Fusion

arXiv ID: 2611.20095
分类: cs.PF, cs.DC, cs.LG, eess.AS
发布时间: 2026-11-15

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Aditya Chatterjee, Sanjay Singh Chauhan, Mayank Jain, Arushi Dhingra, Sahin Chowdhury, Rahul Mittal
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe ranks first among open-weight models on the Hugging Face Open ASR leaderboards. Serving it efficiently takes more than fast operators: continuous batching changes the amount and mix of work at every iteration, and the best fusion granularity changes with it. We present a serving engine that, for every pass, chooses between persistent megakernels and chains of fused kernels at crossovers measured offline. On an H100 SXM GPU, throughput of our accuracy-preserving serving peaks at 4,345x real time, 1.39x the peak of tuned vLLM, and is up to 4.39x faster at low concurrency; on B200 it peaks at 6,367x, 1.64x vLLM at the highest concurrency we measured. Word error rate (WER) is 1.91 to 1.93% against 1.88 to 1.93% for vLLM. Within a 400 ms budget for p95 end-to-end completion time, it serves 8x as many concurrent requests. Choosing per pass beats either level alone: 23% higher throughput than always using megakernels at 128 concurrent requests, and 9% higher than always using fused kernels at 8. Opt-in switches, including greedy MoE layer skipping, add 13 to 16% throughput on B200 for offline jobs that can accept higher WER.
