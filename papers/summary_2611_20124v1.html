QA-CMIF:Accurate and Lightweight Cross-Modal Latent Imputation and Fusion for Fault-Tolerant sEMG-Inertial Hand Gesture Recognition

arXiv ID: 2611.20124
分类: cs.LG
发布时间: 2026-11-15

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Xinyu Tao, Shuang Huang, Yeyun Cai, Fang Deng
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Fusing surface electromyography (sEMG) with inertial measurements is widely used for myoelectric prosthesis control and wearable human-machine interfaces. In daily wear, however, socket micro-displacement, electrode lift-off, and wire failures often corrupt an unknown subset of sEMG channels through burst artifacts, lift-off shocks, or open-circuit dropouts. Existing fusion networks mix all channels in the first convolutional layer, so a few corrupted electrodes contaminate the entire latent representation, whereas gate-only attenuation of corrupted channels discards the co-activation cues needed to separate fine-grained gestures. We propose QA-CMIF, a quality-aware cross-modal latent imputation and fusion framework that encodes each sEMG channel independently, scores its reliability with a soft gate, and protects intact channels via a confidence-weighted residual bypass, while a cross-attention imputer repairs degraded channels in latent space using the inertial stream and remaining reliable sEMG channels under a bad-channel alignment loss. Across within-subject evaluations on 29 participants from Ninapro DB3, DB2, and DB5 spanning 17-52 gesture classes, QA-CMIF outperformed the gate-only baseline under 50% channel corruption by 1.29-2.65 percentage points, denoted pp, across all three 17-class cohorts with paired-t Holm-adjusted p<0.002, widened this margin to 2.59-4.15 pp on the 40- and 52-class tasks, and surpassed corruption-augmented conventional fusion by 4.61-19.85 pp. Requiring only 0.175-0.185M parameters and 2.45-2.63M multiply-accumulate operations across the 2000 Hz and 200 Hz setups, QA-CMIF saves 46%-55% in parameters and 72%-81% in compute over recent baselines with a 1.44 ms 99th-percentile single-thread CPU latency.
