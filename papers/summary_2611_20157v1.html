Kernel-Guided Learning of Lifted Sparse Polynomial Models of Nonlinear Dynamical Systems

arXiv ID: 2611.20157
分类: eess.SY, cs.LG
发布时间: 2026-11-15

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Mohammad Itani, Timm Faulwasser
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Polynomial models of dynamical systems enable constructive analysis via sum-of-squares programming. Yet, many physical systems involve non-polynomial nonlinearities which for certain classes of systems may be inferred from the underlying physics even when the exact dynamics are unknown. We show that fitting lifted polynomial models directly to lifted data is problematic, since the exact lifting constraints render the lifted derivatives linearly dependent. Instead, we learn the dynamics in the extended state, i.e., the state together with the known nonlinearities, and obtain the dynamics of the nonlinearities via the exact lifting relations. To avoid the combinatorially large libraries that sparse regression requires when the polynomial degrees are unknown, we propose KINDy, which selects the degree of each component by kernel ridge regression before applying sparse regression to the correspondingly reduced library. We prove that, for sufficiently small noise and regularization, provided the data satisfy a rank condition, the selected degree never underestimates the true degree. On the cart-pole system, KINDy recovers the true degrees at low noise levels and achieves speedups of up to 8.5 over sparse regression on the full library, while in almost all cases yielding models that are at least as sparse and have lower or equal test errors.
