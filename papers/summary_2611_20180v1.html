UrbanROI: Causal Modeling for Regional ROI Forecasting under POI Entry

arXiv ID: 2611.20180
分类: cs.LG
发布时间: 2026-11-15

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Zhaoqi Zhang, Miao Xie, Linyou Cai, Qianlong Xie, Xingxing Wang, Siqiang Luo, Gao Cong
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

As urban commercial environments continue to evolve rapidly, accurately modeling the economic impact of point of interest (POI) entry is essential for supporting data-driven advertising allocation, merchant onboarding, and regional business decision-making. Existing return on investment (ROI) prediction methods mainly focus on individual ads or merchants, overlooking the interaction effects among POIs and failing to capture the causal influence of structural changes in urban commercial ecosystems. In this paper, we introduce a novel research problem -- regional ROI forecasting under POI entry, which aims to predict the future ROI trajectory of a commercial region after the introduction of a new POI by modeling temporal ROI evolution and competition-driven interactions among surrounding POIs. To address these challenges, we propose UrbanROI, an intervention-aware causal forecasting framework for region-level economic outcome prediction. UrbanROI leverages an Interaction-aware Regional Graph Module to explicitly model competitive relationships among POIs based on user request contexts, spatial proximity, and request-level commercial signals, and constructs structurally aligned treatment and control graphs to simulate factual and counterfactual regional states. Extensive experiments on large-scale real-world POI and advertising datasets demonstrate that UrbanROI consistently outperforms strong baselines in post-entry regional ROI forecasting. The results demonstrate its effectiveness in temporal economic modeling, inter-POI interaction representation, and intervention-aware forecasting, providing support for regional economic analysis and business decision-making in dynamic urban commercial systems.
