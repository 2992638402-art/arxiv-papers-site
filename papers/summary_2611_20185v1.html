RoboKit: Accelerating Parallel Differentiable Optimization for Robot Learning

arXiv ID: 2611.20185
分类: cs.RO, cs.AI, cs.LG
发布时间: 2026-11-15

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Jianglong Ye, Xuanbin Peng, Conor Mc Gartoll, Changwei Jing, Lai Wei, Jiali Chen, Xueyan Zou, Xiaolong Wang
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

We present RoboKit, a high-performance, general-purpose toolkit for parallel differentiable optimization in robotics, built on three principles: (i) Performance and flexibility: GPU-parallel multi-seed solvers pair sampling with versatile optimizers, running 2-60$\times$ faster than task-specific baselines across seven tasks; (ii) Geometry, rendering and physics: hybrid and coarse-to-fine representations accelerate collision, differentiable rendering and simulation, optimizing 4096 grasps in 2.97 s; (iii) Integration with learning: costs are differentiable and simulation is parallel, so RoboKit both distills optimization into policies and trains RL; distilled policies run 9-240$\times$ faster at comparable accuracy, and RL reaches a GPU baseline's scores up to 2.1$\times$ sooner on the same simulator.
