Calibrated Functional Prediction from Overlapping Nonlinear Observations

arXiv ID: 2611.20567
分类: cs.LG, stat.ML
发布时间: 2026-11-16

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Peida Li
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

We study prediction of nonlinear audit functionals from scalar observations of a shared hidden table state. For Schur-rational operators, local Gaussian projections yield a common sparse kernel for overlapping tasks, repeated row identities, and cross-row dependence. Shared-state residual samples extend this kernel through a positive-semidefinite feature representation. Positive matrix integration and sample-value enclosures connect the original inputs to the actual saved hybrid prediction. A sorted calibration-role perturbation bound then certifies its complete finite-pool pinball risk. Independent complete-loss selection chooses the ridge, score scale, and residual weight, while task-rank calibration supplies an upper endpoint. Evaluation uses twenty new reference contexts per dataset at two task sizes, giving 400 conditions across ten benchmark populations. The mean dataset complete-loss ratios are 0.9977 to the degree-two kernel, 0.9961 to matched complete-MC shrinkage, and 0.9911 to the retrospective minimum over 174 public configurations. The last ratio has a paired reference-bootstrap interval of [0.9898,0.9927]. On 40 profiles at coefficient tolerance $10^{-7}$, Gamma quadrature has a geometric mean cold-time ratio of .0646 to strengthened Arb/Petras integration.
