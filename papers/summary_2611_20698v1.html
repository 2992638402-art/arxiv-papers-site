STARS: Stage-Adaptive Responsibility Separation for Shadow Removal in Remote Sensing Images

arXiv ID: 2611.20698
分类: cs.CV
发布时间: 2026-11-16

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Gaoliang Song, Chengjie Li, Pengju Liu, Chenchen Chang, Jinhao You, Hongzhi Zhang
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Shadows are a ubiquitous radiative attenuation phenomenon in remote sensing images and can severely affect high-resolution remote sensing interpretation tasks. In shadow removal, although introducing a mask prior provides explicit positional information for the restoration network, it also causes non-shadow regions to be modulated by the shadow prior, producing a systematic brightness shift; notably, this phenomenon is independent of whether the mask is accurate. In remote sensing images, non-shadow regions usually occupy a larger proportion, so a small shift over these regions incurs a much higher cost on whole-image metrics than an equal gain in shadow regions. Adopting a task-decoupling perspective, we therefore decompose shadow removal into two tasks, shadow-region restoration and non-shadow-region identity, within a cascaded architecture, and handle them sequentially. Based on this insight, we propose STARS, a cascaded shadow removal framework that separates regional responsibilities across stages. The method retains the advantages of the shadow mask while resolving the degradation of non-shadow regions, without requiring a complex parallel dual-branch structure. Meanwhile, to introduce large-model prior knowledge into remote sensing tasks, we further design a Feature Selection and Fusion Adapter (FSFA). We conduct extensive experiments on three benchmark datasets, UAV-SC+, ISTD+ and WSRD+ 2026, demonstrating the superiority of STARS. The framework shows that decoupling regional responsibilities can resolve the intrinsic contradiction of mask priors, providing a new direction for image restoration with region heterogeneity.
