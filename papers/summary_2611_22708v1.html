Cross-cultural Adaptation of Recipes for Low-Resource Languages: A Benchmark and Multilingual Culinary Lexicon for Hausa, Yorùbá, and Igbo

arXiv ID: 2611.22708
分类: cs.CL, cs.AI
发布时间: 2026-11-17

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Nuhu Ibrahim, Maryam Abdulazeez, Fatsuma Jauro, Salisu Aliyu, Robert Stevens, Riza Batista-Navarro
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Recent multilingual large language models (LLMs) have improved machine translation, yet culturally adaptive translation for low-resource languages (LRLs) remains underexplored. This is particularly challenging for recipes, where ingredients, cooking tools, measurements, and procedures may require adaptation to the target cultural context. In this work, we introduce ReciLoReL, a benchmark of 600 expert-translated and culturally adapted recipes across Yorùbá, Hausa, and Igbo (three of the most prominent languages in Nigeria), and ReciLex, a multilingual culinary lexicon containing approximately 15,000 translated entities across ten recipe entity categories, including ingredients, tools, chef actions, state of tools, and durations. We evaluate state-of-the-art multilingual LLMs for English-to-LRL recipe translation under zero-shot and few-shot settings and investigate whether augmenting LLMs with ReciLex improves culturally adaptive recipe translation without requiring large target-language recipe corpora for training or fine-tuning. Beyond conventional reference-based translation evaluation using BLEU, chrF, and TER, we introduce five criteria for evaluating cultural adaptation, adapted from established cross-cultural adaptation methods. Across the selected LLMs and languages, ReciLex improves average BLEU from 13.57 to 14.51 and chrF from 37.89 to 38.74, while reducing TER from 77.14 to 75.12. Human evaluation further shows improvements ranging from 0.11 to 0.36 points across the five cultural adaptation evaluation criteria on a five-point scale. Overall, our work provides new resources, evaluation criteria, and empirical evaluation for studying culturally adaptive recipe translation in low-resource settings.
