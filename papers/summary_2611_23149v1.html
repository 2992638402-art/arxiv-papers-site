Capability Composition in Heterogeneous Teams via Barrier-Guided Diffusion Sampling

arXiv ID: 2611.23149
分类: cs.RO, cs.LG, cs.MA, eess.SY
发布时间: 2026-11-17

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Diana Laura Morales, Ruoyu Lin, Magnus Egerstedt, Fabio Pasqualetti
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

The agents in a heterogeneous team can cooperate to accomplish tasks that exceed their individual capabilities. The team trajectories for a cooperative task can be generated by a generative model, provided that the model has been trained on demonstrations of the agents cooperating. Yet, demonstrations of cooperation are costly and may not exist for tasks that the team has never performed. The conditions under which the agents can combine their capabilities, however, are physical properties of the team and are known before any demonstration is collected. We encode the conditions as constraints on the team trajectories, and we enforce the constraints while sampling from generative models trained on demonstrations of each agent alone. We prove that the proposed method can generate every team trajectory that satisfies the constraints, although the demonstrations contain no cooperation. Hence the constraints determine which team trajectories can be generated, and the demonstrations determine how likely each team trajectory is. Numerical results on two cooperative tasks show the effectiveness of the method, which generates cooperative team trajectories that satisfy the constraints and requires no retraining when agents join or leave the team. Finally, we implement the method on a multi-robot testbed.
