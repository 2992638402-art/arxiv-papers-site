AdaS-ViT: Test-Time Adaptive Routing for Spiking Vision Transformers with Ultra-High Energy Efficiency

arXiv ID: 2611.23728
分类: cs.NE, cs.AI, cs.CV
发布时间: 2026-11-18

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Zecheng Hao, Zihan Huang, Shenghao Xie, Jiyuan Zhang, Zhaofei Yu, Tiejun Huang
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Spiking Neural Networks (SNNs) promise event-driven and energy-efficient vision, yet existing spiking vision transformers (S-ViTs) largely confine spiking tokens to fixed and uniform firing schemes along the temporal, spatial and channel dimensions, whereas suppressing the computational redundancy thus incurred calls for adaptive firing mechanisms that operate jointly across all three axes at test time. To this end, we propose the Adaptive Efficient Integrate-and-Fire (AdaE-IF) model, which consults the attention score of each spiking token and routes salient regions to fine-grained firing patterns while assigning energy-frugal coarse patterns to the remaining regions. Building on AdaE-IF, we further introduce Spiking Gated Self-Attention (S-GSA) and Spiking Local-Gated Unit (S-LGU), which enforce selective spike emission at token and channel granularity. We integrate the aforementioned mechanisms and modules into AdaS-ViT, a test-time adaptive-routing architecture trained with a progressive spatio-temporal curriculum that stabilizes the co-adaptation of firing precision and routing masks. Extensive experiments across benchmarks spanning diverse data scales and modalities demonstrate that AdaS-ViT consistently achieves highly competitive performance. Notably, on ImageNet-1k it consumes only 1.02 to 2.45 millijoules per image and attains an accuracy-per-energy ratio of 32$\times$ to 72$\times$, operating in an economical inference regime and thereby pushing the energy-efficiency boundary further.
