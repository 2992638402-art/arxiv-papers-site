OmniFish: A Bioinspired, Modular, Reconfigurable Soft Robotic Fish Enabling Multi-Modal Swimming

arXiv ID: 2611.24511
分类: cs.RO, physics.flu-dyn
发布时间: 2026-11-18

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Mathias Jensen, Saravana Prashanth Murali Babu
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

In this work, we introduce OmniFish, a bioinspired modular soft robotic fish designed to emulate the full spectrum of body and/or caudal fin (BCF) locomotion modes within a single robotic platform. The design combines morphology averaging across representative fish species with a tendon-driven compliant segmented spine whose effective stiffness can be reconfigured through progressive mechanical jamming. By selectively constraining body segments, OmniFish shifts deformation from whole-body undulation to posterior body bending and localized tail oscillation, enabling bioinspired Anguilliform, Subcarangiform, Carangiform, Thunniform, and Ostraciiform swimming using a single tendon-driven actuator. The design was guided by morphometric analysis and evaluated through caudal-fin thrust characterization, numerical body-drag simulations, kinematic analysis, and swimming performance experiments. Kinematic analysis confirmed the intended robot transition from distributed body undulation to localized tail oscillation. Swimming experiments demonstrated distinct mode-dependent performance, with the Ostraciiform configuration achieving the highest straight-line velocity of 8.14 cm/s and the lowest cost of transport of 2.42 at 1.5 tail beats/s. These proof-of-concept results show that a mechanically simple stiffness-reconfigurable architecture can generate distinct BCF swimming modes and provide a versatile strategy for developing multimodal soft robotic fish.
