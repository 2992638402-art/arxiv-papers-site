Objective-Specialized Residual Streams in Manifold-Constrained Hyper-Connections

arXiv ID: 2611.24567
分类: cs.CL
发布时间: 2026-11-18

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Shantanu Agarwal, Ruchita V. Maitri, Zhiqi Huang, Vivek Datla, Alfy Samuel
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Manifold-constrained Hyper-Connections (mHC) extend a Transformer's residual stream into multiple parallel streams with learned cross-stream connections. Prior work trains all streams toward a shared objective. We instead study whether assigning distinct learning objectives to different streams can improve task performance and induce objective-specific specialization. Using complementary lexical (sparse) and semantic (dense) retrieval objectives as a test case, we find that objective-specialized mHC consistently outperforms a non-specialized mHC model across the full sparse--dense fusion range, for both two- and four-stream models and at two model scales. We then examine how specialization changes the model internally using probes of stream representations, residual mixing, objective-specific gradient flow, and read/write gates. The specialized and non-specialized models behave similarly through most of the network but diverge in the final layers: the specialized model develops increasingly objective-specific gradient flow, distinct read/write routing patterns, and reduced cross-stream mixing, and streams assigned to different objectives become increasingly dissimilar while those trained toward the same objective remain highly similar. Fixing the residual mixing to the identity leaves the specialization benefit intact, indicating that the benefit stems from objective-specific read/write routing rather than learned cross-stream mixing. These results show that assigning distinct objectives to mHC streams can improve multi-objective retrieval while inducing structured, depth-dependent specialization in the residual computation.
