Conceptual full-vehicle modeling of a modular all-wheel-drive robotic ADS test platform as a foundation for a digital twin architecture

arXiv ID: 2611.24646
分类: cs.RO
发布时间: 2026-11-18

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Felix Florian Schwald, Martin Meiners, Michael Viehof, Erik Hultman, Edwin Kamau
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

This paper presents the development of an initial full-vehicle simulation model of the PixKit modular electric test platform in IPG CarMaker. The objective is to create a physics-based and extendable model structure that can serve as a low-level foundation for future digital twin applications. The reference vehicle is a skateboard chassis combining unconventional vehicle characteristics such as four independent wheel-hub motors and rear axle steering capable of high steering angles. Since complete measurement data of the vehicle is not yet available, the model parameterization is based on a combination of manufacturer documentation, derived values and engineering assumptions. The modeling approach focuses on the system-level integration of the main vehicle subsystems rather than on a fully validated representation. The resulting model was assessed through basic driving scenarios with different steering inputs and configurations. The simulations showed stable model behavior and physically plausible interaction between the subsystems. The all-wheel-drive configuration produced symmetric wheel responses in longitudinal driving, while counter-phase rear axle steering increased the yaw response and reduced the simulated turning circle diameter compared with front axle steering only. The circular driving scenario further indicated bounded roll and slip behavior. Overall, the implemented CarMaker model provides a traceable and functional simulation basis for the PixKit vehicle. It can support future model refinement, calibration with real vehicle data and development with the goal of creating a validated digital twin architecture.
