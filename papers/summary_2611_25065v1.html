Empirical Variational Bayes Factorization for Longitudinal Matrix Data

arXiv ID: 2611.25065
分类: stat.ML, cs.LG
发布时间: 2026-11-18

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Jiakun Li, Eric F. Lock
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Collecting high-dimensional data over time for multiple individuals is increasingly common in biomedical research, particularly in studies of disease progression or molecular time-course experiments. Often data are missing or the timepoints are irregularly spaced across individuals. There is a lack of flexible methodology to explore latent patterns of change over time and account for missing data. We propose an empirical variational Bayes (EVB) approach to factorize high-dimensional longitudinal data, in which the underlying factors have temporal correlation over time. The EVB approach is computationally efficient and accommodates flexible covariance structures over time with automatic determination of all hyper-parameters (including the underlying rank, i.e., number of factors). Simulations demonstrate that the approach captures the underlying signal more precisely than other strategies, and performs well with respect to missing data imputation under different scenarios. An application to a gene time-course data for multiple sclerosis treatment provides a factorization with interpretable components over time and improved imputation accuracy for missing data.
