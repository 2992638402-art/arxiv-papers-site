Statistical, Machine Learning and Deep Learning Methods for Missing Covariates: An Empirical Evaluation with Applications to Alzheimer's Disease Research

arXiv ID: 2611.25219
分类: stat.ML, cs.LG
发布时间: 2026-11-18

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Yiqing Pan, Sharon X. Xie, Shujie Ma, Angela L. Jefferson, Panpan Zhang
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Missing data are common in biomedical research, particularly when biomarkers are costly or require invasive procedures to obtain, such as cerebrospinal fluid measures in Alzheimer's disease. Although a wide range of missing data methods exists, from traditional statistical approaches to modern machine learning (ML) and deep learning (DL) algorithms, their comparative performance under different missingness mechanisms remains insufficiently understood. In this study, we provide an empirical evaluation of eight methods for handling missing covariates, including complete-case analysis, multiple imputation, ML-based techniques, and DL frameworks such as multilayer perceptrons, variational autoencoders, and generative adversarial networks. We summarize their conceptual foundations, practical implementation considerations, strengths, and limitations. Through extensive simulations, we assess estimation bias, efficiency, standard error accuracy, confidence interval coverage, and computational burden across different missingness mechanisms and proportions. An application to the Vanderbilt Memory and Aging Project further illustrates the implications of different methods for regression analysis in a real-world biomedical setting. Finally, we provide practical recommendations for method selection and highlight remaining challenges that warrant future investigation.
