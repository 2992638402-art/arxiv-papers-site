PUPPET: Physical-Unified Platform for Presence, Embodiment, and Teleoperation

arXiv ID: 2611.26350
分类: cs.RO
发布时间: 2026-11-19

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Mahika Vats, Milan Groshev, Carlos Gomez Cubero, Eduardo Castelló Ferrer
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Remote access to robotics hardware does not by itself guarantee usable experimental data. A teleoperated trial can complete the task and still produce an incomplete or inconsistent record. PUPPET aims to solve this problem by presenting a cyber-physical framework that opens a physical robotics laboratory through a continuously synchronized VR replica of the robot, stranding environment, and task objects. In addition, PUPPET integrates a trial validation pipeline built into the experimental workflow. We evaluated PUPPET in a within-subject study with 30 participants, who performed the same table-to-shelf object-transfer task in person, through 2D teleoperation, and through PUPPET. VR reduced post-learning completion time by 27.7% over 2D (68.7,s vs. 95.0,s), statistically indistinguishable from in-person execution (72.1,s). It also produced more validation-ready (85.6% vs. 66.0%) and intervention-free (89.7% vs. 67.2%) trials. With PUPPET we show that modality shapes the remote task performance and the reusability of the resulting experimental record.
