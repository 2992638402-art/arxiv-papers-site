SpecFlow: Flow Matching in the Acceleration Spectrum for Multi-Agent Trajectory Forecasting

arXiv ID: 2611.26390
分类: cs.RO
发布时间: 2026-11-19

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Ziyang Xu, Peng Han, Hai Zhou
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Multimodal trajectory forecasting must cover diverse future behaviors without sacrificing dynamical coherence. Most generative forecasters evolve frame-wise positions directly, leaving the relationships among position, velocity, and acceleration implicit. We present SpecFlow, a conditional flow-matching framework that treats the Discrete Cosine Transform (DCT) coefficients of future accelerations as its generative state. SpecFlow learns a transport from Gaussian noise to acceleration spectra and reconstructs velocity and position through fixed analytical integration initialized by the last observed state. A joint phase-space objective supervises all three kinematic levels. To represent destination uncertainty without future information, an endpoint predictor produces candidate destinations and confidence scores solely from observed context, and the highest-confidence candidate conditions the spectral denoiser. Alternating attention over trajectory hypotheses and agents captures multimodality and interaction. Under best-of-20 evaluation, SpecFlow achieves average minADE/minFDE of 0.15/0.22 on ETH/UCY, 0.69/0.86 at four seconds on NBA, and 7.38/11.57 on SDD. It establishes state-of-the-art results across all five ETH/UCY scenes and achieves the lowest four-second ADE on NBA with a tied-best FDE, while remaining competitive on SDD. Together, these results demonstrate that acceleration-spectrum flow matching provides an effective structured generative state across smooth pedestrian motion, coordinated multi-agent sports dynamics, and heterogeneous outdoor trajectories. Code will be made publicly available.
