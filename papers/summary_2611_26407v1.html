Flow Matching from Non-Gaussian Sources: A Theory of Efficient Sampling

arXiv ID: 2611.26407
分类: stat.ML, cs.AI, cs.IT, cs.LG
发布时间: 2026-11-19

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Yuchen Zhou, Lili Zheng, Yuxin Chen, Gen Li
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Flow matching is a versatile framework for generative modeling that transports a source distribution to a target data distribution. A key advantage is its flexibility in the choice of source distribution, such as heavy-tailed and multimodal sources. Yet finite-step sampling guarantees for non-Gaussian sources remain limited. We establish a nonasymptotic convergence theory for Euler discretization of flow matching with linear interpolation. Suppose the source distribution on $\mathbb{R}^d$ has an everywhere positive, continuously differentiable density, finite total variance $\mathsf{TotVar}{\mathsf{base}}$, and an $L$-Lipschitz score. Using the population velocity field and a suitable time grid, this sampler achieves total variation error at most $\varepsilon$ in $\widetilde{O}(Ld\mathsf{TotVar}{\mathsf{base}}/\varepsilon)$ iterations, provided the target distribution satisfies a mild second-moment condition. These assumptions cover, among many examples, source distributions such as multivariate Student's $t$ distributions with more than two degrees of freedom and finite Gaussian mixtures with shared covariance, while accommodating target distributions with neither a smooth density nor bounded support.
