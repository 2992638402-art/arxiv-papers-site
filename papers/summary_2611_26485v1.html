An Open-Source ROS 2 Framework for Shared Control in Surgical Teleoperation: Integrating the Haply Inverse3 with the da Vinci Research Kit

arXiv ID: 2611.26485
分类: cs.RO, eess.SY
发布时间: 2026-11-19

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Balint Varga, Sebastian Fritz, Gergely Kiss, Kristof Takacs, Peter Galambos
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Surgical teleoperation deprives surgeons of touch, forcing visual inference of tissue forces; the commercial platforms now restoring force feedback remain closed, preventing open research on haptic shared control. This paper provides a system contribution: it presents an open-source, ROS 2-native framework that integrates the compact, three-degree-of-freedom (3-DOF) Haply Inverse3 haptic device with the da~Vinci Research Kit (dVRK). The framework includes a novel mapping strategy that combines 3-DOF translational device input with separately tracked VerseGrip orientation into 6-DOF patient-side manipulator commands. Two virtual-fixture-inspired guidance modes serve as reference implementations to evaluate the framework in a within-subjects user study on a teleoperated hot-wire benchmark task in simulation, and the architecture is further demonstrated on physical dVRK hardware. Relative to unassisted manual control, linear guidance significantly reduced both the number of wire contacts and the task completion time, whereas the progressive-stiffness guidance produced consistent but statistically non-significant trends in the same direction. Subjective ratings of workload, agency, and satisfaction showed favorable descriptive trends for the guidance modes. The results demonstrate the feasibility of haptic shared control with a compact device coupled to the dVRK and provide an openly available, reproducible platform for future research. They further suggest that simpler linear feedback may be more robust for inexperienced operators than more complex profiles.
