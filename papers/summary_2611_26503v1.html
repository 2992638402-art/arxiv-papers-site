JARVIS: An Agent That Designs, Builds, and Tests Circuit Boards on Its Own

arXiv ID: 2611.26503
分类: cs.RO, eess.SY
发布时间: 2026-11-19

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Shibo Zhou, Tianqi Hong, Zheng Yan, He Yang
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Power electronics is central to the energy transition, but developing new hardware still relies on human-led design, fabrication, testing, and iteration. We present JARVIS, an embodied AI agent that autonomously closes this loop for circuit boards. JARVIS maintains machine-specific capabilities and constraints, scans its workspace using a robot-mounted 3D scanner, and constructs an executable digital twin in which it rehearses robot motions, fabrication steps, and circuit behavior before consuming materials. A single text instruction initiates the physical experiment. Using unmodified desktop equipment, the agent coordinates a robot arm, a PCB printer that deposits conductive silver ink and solder paste, and a pick-and-place machine. It verifies progress using cameras and sensors, then tests the fabricated circuit with an OPAL-RT real-time simulator and an oscilloscope. Based on the measurements, JARVIS diagnoses deviations from the design and generates a revised design in KiCad for subsequent rehearsal. In a demonstration involving a 60 Hz notch filter for grid-connected converter sensing, JARVIS completed a first-run experiment in 2 h 39 min without human intervention, achieving 50.6 dB attenuation at 60 Hz. Its digital-twin rehearsal identified five issues before fabrication, including a test criterion that would have incorrectly rejected a functioning board. During execution, the agent also resolved encountered faults autonomously. These results demonstrate an integrated agent-driven workflow for physical design, fabrication, measurement, and iterative improvement of power-electronic circuitry. To our knowledge, JARVIS is the first system in power electronics in which an agent closes the physical design-build-test-learn loop for circuit boards.
