On the loss landscape of the linear neural network: beyond critical points

arXiv ID: 2611.26671
分类: cs.LG, cs.AI, cs.NE, stat.ML
发布时间: 2026-11-19

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Antoine Lejay, Lionel Lenotre, Sa{ï}d Toubra
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

While the critical points of the loss landscape in linear neural networks have been extensively studied since the seminal work of Baldi &amp; Hornik in the late 1980s, a description of this landscape at an intermediate or global scale is still lacking. Such a characterization is crucial, particularly as a substantial body of literature indicates that gradient descent circumvents critical points. The present article introduces an intermediate structure, termed the critical thalweg, which encompasses all critical points and exhibits key geometric properties. Beyond providing a geometric proof of Baldi &amp; Hornik's results using simpler assumptions than the existing literature, this framework offers a global view of the loss landscape that can be easily visualized in a two-dimensional plane. Empirical observations suggest that gradient descent is attracted toward the critical thalweg, along which the loss continues to decrease to its global minimum.
