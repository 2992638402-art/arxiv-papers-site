MG-EDA: Maturity-Gated Skew-Normal Estimation of Distribution Algorithms

arXiv ID: 2611.26938
分类: stat.ME, cs.AI, cs.NE
发布时间: 2026-11-20

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Jun Zhang, Tong Qian, Hua Ding, Weijie Yu, Zhi-Hui Zhan
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Gaussian estimation of distribution algorithms shrink their variance on slopes and stall far from the optimum. This paper equips the corrections that act along the direction of movement -- a mean shift, an elongation of the covariance along the path and a skewness -- with the rule they lack: a calibrated gate, driven by a maturity statistic of the population, decides whether, how strongly and for how long each correction acts, and when the gate is closed the algorithm is exactly the estimation of multivariate normal algorithm (EMNA). The statistic is a cumulative path of whitened mean shifts with an inverse-Wishart correction, whose expectation is one under a null model without systematic movement and whose quantiles give thresholds with known false-alarm rate. It gates the three corrections as three rank-one terms of one matrix sampling equation, the skewness being carried by a skew-normal sampler that preserves the estimated mean and covariance; all vanish at maturity. We call the resulting algorithm the maturity-gated EDA (MG-EDA). A full factorial study on a training set of black-box optimization benchmarking (BBOB) functions separates the parameters: variance scaling is the prerequisite, the mean shift needs a feedback correction removing the injected shift from the statistic, the elongation turns the variance loss on a slope into a gain, and the skewness substitutes for the mean shift rather than complementing it. On held-out instances MG-EDA with the first- and second-order corrections is significantly better than the skewness-only predecessor and than EMNA with variance scaling, ties with EMNA with anticipated mean shift, and trails the adapted maximum-likelihood Gaussian model (AMaLGaM) in 20 dimensions.
