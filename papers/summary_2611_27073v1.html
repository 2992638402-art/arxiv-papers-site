Seabird-Aware Multi-Objective Offshore Wind Capacity Allocation: A Gippsland Case Study

arXiv ID: 2611.27073
分类: cs.NE, eess.SY
发布时间: 2026-11-20

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Leilah Taouk, Canchen Jiang, Markus Wagner, Yuqing Xiao, Yidan Zhang
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Offshore wind planning requires balancing capital cost, wind-resource potential, and seabird exposure. We formulate a spatial tri-objective mixed-integer linear program for offshore wind capacity allocation, minimizing capital cost and modeled seabird exposure while maximizing wind-weighted capacity. Applied to Australia's Gippsland offshore wind area, the model identifies exposure reductions of 23-44% at capital-cost premiums of 0.8-6.1% across 15 wind-weighted capacity requirements. We benchmark exposure-aware optimization against 19 percentile-based exclusion thresholds. At a fixed wind-weighted capacity requirement, the 5th-percentile threshold reduces exposure by 30.1% at a 37.6% cost premium, versus at least 30.1% at a 1.4% premium with optimization. Under a fixed capital budget, exclusion screening reduces exposure by 48.2% with a 26.9% capacity loss, compared with 48.5% and 7.6%, respectively, under optimization. These results highlight the value of explicitly optimizing graded seabird exposure.
