Spiking Neural Networks Incrementally Track Firing-Rate Networks

arXiv ID: 2611.27322
分类: cs.NE, cs.LG
发布时间: 2026-11-20

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Hyunseok Oh, Youngki Lee
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Understanding how spiking neural networks (SNNs) efficiently process data streams is key to (i) studying how biological brains conserve energy for lifelong predictions and (ii) enabling efficient continuous AI inference. In this work, we propose a theory on how an SNN incrementally tracks its corresponding firing-rate network, a continuous-valued neural network (ANN). We prove that the output of a discrete SNN continuously tracks the instantaneous ANN output implicitly determined by the SNN input. Based on this principle, we develop LIF-Stream, a conversion framework for efficient SNN inference over unbounded data streams. It enables the converted SNN to reuse its prior activation state and compute only the activation updates between frames, thereby reducing the average number of time steps needed per frame. Specifically, LIF-Stream controls the SNN to continuously track the reference ANN output, which gives a high-accuracy prediction for every frame. Evaluation on three large-scale streaming inference benchmarks with distinct modalities shows that LIF-Stream achieves the best efficiency-accuracy trade-off compared to high-accuracy SNN techniques.
