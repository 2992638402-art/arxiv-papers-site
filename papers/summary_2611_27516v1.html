PhiJEPA: Denoising Latent Predictions for Visual Representation Learning

arXiv ID: 2611.27516
分类: cs.CV, cs.AI, stat.ML
发布时间: 2026-11-20

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Makoto Yamada, Yao-Hung Hubert Tsai
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Can a predictor trained to infer hidden image regions still help a world model plan when part of an object disappears from view and the visible context is blurred? We introduce PhiJEPA, a joint-embedding predictive architecture that adapts PhiNet's two-stage prediction to masked Transformers. From sparse, photometrically corrupted context, a Transformer block predicts hidden tokens of an independently corrupted teacher view. A token-wise MLP then maps these predictions to clean teacher tokens. Controlled ablations show that the combined objective improves dense features over the tested one-stage alternatives. Pre-trained on ImageNet-1K with $5.6\times10^{19}$ training FLOPs, PhiJEPA achieves 69.3 mIoU on linear PASCAL VOC segmentation, exceeding the evaluated I-JEPA and DSeq-JEPA checkpoints in segmentation accuracy, with 25% fewer training FLOPs than I-JEPA. It also improves hidden-region segmentation over Simple I-JEPA (49.1 vs.\ 44.3 mIoU). As an application, we use the frozen encoder in DINO-WM on PushT, and reuse the pre-training predictor, which JEPAs usually discard, to complete occluded tokens without fine-tuning. In open-loop planning with simultaneous object occlusion and Gaussian blur ($σ=2$ pixels), predictor completion raises success from 33% to 65%, compared with 25% for Simple I-JEPA with its predictor and 40% for iBOT with pixel inpainting. Together, these results support two-stage latent prediction as an efficient approach to learning visual representations that remain useful under photometric degradation and support completion under partial observation.
