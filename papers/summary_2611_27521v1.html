PaMuk-Bench: A Parallel Multilingual Multitask Benchmark for Vision-Language Tasks

arXiv ID: 2611.27521
分类: cs.CV, cs.AI, cs.CL, cs.LG
发布时间: 2026-11-20

  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

While vision-language models (VLMs) currently obtain notable results for highly-studied languages, e.g. English, they exhibit significant performance drops in certain low-resource languages, especially due to the lack of annotated datasets. To this end, we propose PaMuk-Bench, a novel parallel multilingual multitask benchmark for vision-language tasks. Our benchmark includes a set of over 10K curated English prompts that are translated into six languages: Arabic, Danish, French, Hindi, Italian, Romanian. Parallel translations, which are obtained via a semi-automatic process, along with accompanying generated and retrieved images enable the comparative development and evaluation of VLMs across a wide range of tasks: text-to-image generation, image captioning, content-based image retrieval, image generation difficulty estimation, image retrieval difficulty estimation. We empirically show that zero-shot English and multilingual pre-trained VLMs compare consistently worse than their fine-tuned counterparts, confirming that PaMuk-Bench is a valuable resource that can drastically reduce the language gap across a variety of VLM architectures and tasks. PaMuk-Bench is available at https://github.com/ghesil/pamuk_bench.
