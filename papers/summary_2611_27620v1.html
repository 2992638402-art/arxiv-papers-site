Sketch2TikZ: Multi-Reward Reinforcement Learning for Converting Hand-Drawn Sketches to Executable TikZ

arXiv ID: 2611.27620
分类: cs.CV
发布时间: 2026-11-20

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Shubham Paliwal, Arushi Jain, Monika Sharma, Manasi Patwardhan
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Automatic digitization of hand-drawn sketches into editable formats is crucial for modern document workflows and LaTeX integration. Converting sketches into executable TikZ code provides a structured representation of diagrams, but remains challenging due to noisy and ambiguous inputs, which hinder reliable interpretation and limit the effectiveness of token-level supervision for ensuring global diagram validity. To address these challenges, we propose Sketch2TikZ, a reinforcement learning framework that optimizes sketch-to-code generation via a sequential multi-reward strategy, combining Code-Structure ($\mathcal{R}{cs}$), OCR ($\mathcal{R}{ocr}$), CleanDIFT ($\mathcal{R}{cd}$), and Self-Similarity ($\mathcal{R}{ss}$) rewards to enforce textual, visual and structural correctness. We evaluate Sketch2TikZ on the SkeTikZ and FlowChart-3B benchmarks using metrics including compilation success rate (CSR), image similarity, code similarity, and character similarity. Our method achieves near-perfect compilation success and improves visual and textual alignment over strong open-source baselines, while remaining competitive with proprietary GPT-based vision-language models. Extensive ablations demonstrate that curriculum-based reward scheduling is critical for reducing reward interference, stabilizing training and mitigating proxy over-optimization. Overall, our results highlight the effectiveness of reference-aware, multi-objective reinforcement learning for structured diagram generation from noisy sketches.
