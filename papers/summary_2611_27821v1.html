Sampling non-abelian lattice gauge theories with diffusion models

arXiv ID: 2611.27821
分类: hep-lat, cs.LG
发布时间: 2026-11-20

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Gert Aarts, Diaa E. Habibi, Andreas Ipp, David I. Müller, Thomas R. Ranner, Lingxiao Wang, Wei Wang, Qianteng Zhu
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

We present gauge-equivariant diffusion models as samplers for lattice gauge theories (LGTs). Using lattice gauge-equivariant convolutional neural networks (L-CNNs) and the Metropolis-adjusted annealed Langevin algorithm (MAALA), we are able to accurately sample two-dimensional U(2) field configurations and show first promising results for four-dimensional SU(3). Our two-dimensional models extrapolate remarkably well to larger inverse couplings than originally trained on. We confirm this by comparing observables to analytically known values and to hybrid Monte Carlo results.
