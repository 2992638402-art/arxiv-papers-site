Topology Hub Optimal Transport: Eliminating Long Context Dilution at Flash Speed

arXiv ID: 2611.27951
分类: cs.LG
发布时间: 2026-11-20

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Dylan Forde
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Long-context models use sparse or structured attention to avoid quadratic cost, yet retrieving a small set of relevant facts embedded among hundreds of thousands of distractors degrades as normalization mass disperses with length. Hub-and-spoke optimal transport (OT) replaces the dense $L \times L$ matrix with a linear-size graph over local windows and $K$ shared hubs balanced by Sinkhorn scaling. Building on block-wise tail refinement and fixed-support transport topology (arXiv:2605.08123, arXiv:2609.07954), we introduce Topology Hub Optimal Transport (THOT): (1) bounded-support capacity gating via simplex sparsemax ($O(L\log L)$) or sorting-free token-local thresholded ReLU ($O(L)$), driving excess distractor mass to zero; (2) a three-sweep $1+2$ annealed row-closed Sinkhorn schedule that damps bipartite residuals; (3) an exact rank-$(D+2R+1)$ One-Reference-Tile adjoint in batched split-factor Triton kernels (BF16 Tensor Cores, FP32 potentials); and (4) a chunked exclusive-prefix causal variant with verified zero future leakage. On NVIDIA L4, sparsemax THOT trained at $L=128$ reaches 97.40% zero-shot recall of fixed bindings at $L=262{,}144$ under pure task loss ($α=0$) and 98.83% at $α=0.05$ (matched by $O(L)$ thresholded ReLU at $α=0.05$, vs. 80.60% at $α=0$), while softmax-gated hub OT drops to 3.52%. In bidirectional operator microbenchmarks, our kernel runs forward+VJP in 100.1 ms at $H=8, D=64, L=262\mathrm{K}$ (2.2x faster with 4.0x smaller compiler temporaries than latent softmax) and 1.34 ms vs. 4.37 ms for cuDNN FlashAttention at $L=4{,}096$; multi-layer LM runs use the unfused path. Zero-shot WikiText-103 multi-needle retrieval hits 100.00% (128/128 queries at $L=65\mathrm{K}$) vs. 0.78% for short-trained RoPE FlashAttention, and compact 4-layer WikiText-4K runs show modest causal and masked LM gains.
