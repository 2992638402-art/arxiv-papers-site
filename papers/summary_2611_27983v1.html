Optimal Linear Regression Without a Variance

arXiv ID: 2611.27983
分类: stat.ML, cs.AI, cs.IT, cs.LG
发布时间: 2026-11-20

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Artin Tajdini, Jasper C. H. Lee, Jonathan Scarlett, Kevin Jamieson
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

We study high-probability linear regression in the infinite variance regime, wherecthe regression noise has only a finite conditional $α$-moment $ν$ for some unknown $α\in(1,2)$, and the covariates may only have finite moments up to a given order (e.g., 4th moment). We give a black-box reduction that converts finite-variance regression procedures into weak-moment procedures. The reduction is based on empirical residual pruning and is agnostic to the moment exponent, the noise scale, the covariance, and the design moment. Under a finite fourth-moment condition on the covariates, any finite-variance subroutine attaining the sub-Gaussian rate yields the following when the number of samples $n$ is above a suitably defined threshold: Without knowing $α$ or $ν$, we have with probability at least $1-δ$ that $ |\widehatθ-θ^*|_Σ \lesssim_α ν^{1/α} \big(\frac{d+\log(1/δ)}{n}\big)^{\frac{α-1}α}, $ where $d$ is the dimension, $Σ$ is the covariate covariance matrix, and $|v|_Σ:=(v^\topΣv)^{1/2}$. We further prove a matching minimax lower bound that holds under the same conditions. The reduction is computationally efficient whenever the finite-variance subroutine is. We also obtain a distribution-free prediction guarantee under a bounded raw $α$-moment assumption on the response, and give an analogous $α$-agnostic reduction for mean estimation.
