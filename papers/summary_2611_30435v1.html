Tensor Recovery with Heterogeneous Mode Structures

arXiv ID: 2611.30435
分类: stat.ML, stat.ME
发布时间: 2026-11-23

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Zhixin Huang, Ziyuan Chen, Fang Yao, Ying Yang
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Tensor recovery methods often impose a common structural assumption across modes, even when different modes may exhibit distinct forms of regularity. We propose a unified convex framework that treats each mode according to its structure. Smoothly varying modes form an indexing domain and are handled by local kernel weighting, whereas retained modes may receive low-rank, group-sparse, graph-Laplacian, or autoregressive regularization in distinct and potentially overlapping combinations. The framework also covers ordinary tensor recovery without local smoothing. A consensus HM-ADMM algorithm separates the active penalties into tractable proximal and linear-system updates. For the population-quadratic estimator, we derive deterministic oracle inequalities and high-probability bounds under independent trace regression. The bounds disentangle stochastic error, rank and support complexity, smoothing bias, and quadratic mismatch. They also identify when quadratic penalties sharpen the Frobenius-error bound. For regular smooth domains, the theory characterizes the bias-variance trade-off and yields an optimal bandwidth order, with a specialization to uniform tensor completion. Simulations and two applications illustrate the gains from matching regularization to mode-specific structure, especially under severe undersampling.
