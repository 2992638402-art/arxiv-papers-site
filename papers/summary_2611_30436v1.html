MF-EDA: Joining Membership Functions to Adapt the Number of Distributions in Gaussian Estimation-of-Distribution Algorithms

arXiv ID: 2611.30436
分类: cs.NE, cs.AI
发布时间: 2026-11-23

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Jun Zhang, Tong Qian, Hua Ding, Wei-Jie Yu, Xiao-Fang Liu, Jian-Yu Li
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

An estimation-of-distribution algorithm (EDA) usually samples from one Gaussian, whose peak sits where most selected individuals are. On a landscape with two funnels that is not where the best solutions are: the wider funnel supplies most of the selected individuals, and the deeper funnel, which holds the global optimum, is lost early. This paper builds the search distribution as a neuro-fuzzy system builds a model: from Gaussian membership functions joined by membership degrees. In every generation the proposed MF-EDA searches the principal directions of the selected individuals for the direction along which they are bimodal, describes them on that direction by one or two membership functions according to the Bayesian information criterion and a separation test, joins each membership function to a local Gaussian in the other directions, and samples from the normalised union. A membership function has height one whatever the number of individuals under it, so a detected funnel receives samples according to its width. A two-region selection model shows that sampling shares proportional to the head count, as in a Gaussian mixture, compound the early disadvantage of the deeper funnel, and that the union does not. With one membership function the algorithm is its single-Gaussian baseline; the steps cost no fitness evaluations but make a generation 8 to 17 times slower to compute. On 36 double-funnel settings the component raises the successful runs of a rank-weighted Gaussian EDA from 805 to 1712 of 1800. On the Lunacek double funnel in 30 dimensions it raises that EDA from 4 to 49 and AMaLGaM from 0 to 47 successes in 50 runs, while results on single-funnel functions and on ten multimodal BBOB functions do not change. Restarted CMA-ES remains cheaper in evaluations on the standard double funnel, and the component stops helping when the global funnel is much narrower than the other.
