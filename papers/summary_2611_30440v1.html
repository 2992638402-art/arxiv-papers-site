Spectral Regularization Beyond Ridge Regression

arXiv ID: 2611.30440
分类: stat.ME, stat.ML
发布时间: 2026-11-23

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Kathryn Vasilaky, Walter Vasilaky
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

We study Inclusive Ridge (IR), a two-parameter spectral regularization family that contains ridge regression as well as iterated Tikhonov regularization. We derive its exact finite-sample coefficient risk and establish necessary and sufficient conditions for increasing or decreasing the additional filter-shape parameter around the ridge baseline to locally lower risk. We show that oracle-tuned ridge is generically suboptimal within the IR family. Under joint divergence of the penalty and filter order, we show that a nondegenerate spectral limit exists precisely when their ratio converges to a positive finite constant, yielding a continuous exponential filter. Along the fixed effective-scale path, we also obtain an exact risk-difference comparison and explicit sufficient conditions under which both higher-order estimators and the exponential limiting filter strictly outperform oracle-tuned ridge. In this regime, IR becomes progressively less sensitive to perturbations in the absolute penalty parameter while retaining sensitivity to its effective spectral scale, and a plug-in coefficient-risk first-order condition coincides with a degrees-of-freedom-adjusted discrepancy equation. Numerical examples based on the Fredholm inverse problem, tomographic reconstruction, a regression design, and synthetic difference-in-differences donor-weight estimation illustrate both the potential gains and the limitations of feasible tuning, distinguishing oracle improvements from gains attainable with estimated tuning parameters.
