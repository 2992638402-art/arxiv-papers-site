What Quality Engineers Need to Know About Generative AI: Structured Data Extraction

  • PDF: 下载

📝 摘要

In this expository paper, we explain how quality engineers can use large language models (LLMs) and vision-language models (VLMs) for structured data extraction. We introduce the relevant concepts, discuss structured extraction from a measurement-system perspective, and demonstrate its implementation in R and Python. We then present two freely available web-based applications (apps): (a) extracting information from automotive recall notices and (b) monitoring quality engineering research on arXiv. To ensure the accuracy of our AI-driven structured extraction, human evaluators validated the results. In the first app, four reviewers assessed information extracted from 30 recall notices using two models, with 98.6% of the 720 field-level ratings judged fully correct. In the second app, 21 authors evaluated information extracted from 45 of their arXiv papers. All paper summaries were rated mostly correct or correct, although the evaluations identified problems with some classification fields and mathematical expressions. These results demonstrate the practical potential of structured data extraction while emphasizing the need to validate the extracted information. We make both apps and their source code publicly available so that readers can adapt the approach to their own applications.
