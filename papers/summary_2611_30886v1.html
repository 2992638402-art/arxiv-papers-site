Vitrify Exact Learning: exact differential equations, changes of variable and closed forms from numerical data

arXiv ID: 2611.30886
分类: cs.SC, cs.LG
发布时间: 2026-11-23

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Benedict Irwin, Alexander Mathiasen, Tom Etminan
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Symbolic regression searches a space of expression trees for a formula that fits data. Many functions of the sciences have no finite expression over the usual operations and still satisfy a linear differential equation with polynomial coefficients. Vitrify Exact Learning takes nu- merical values of a function and proceeds in three steps. It assumes that the function, in its own variable or after a change of variable or a pushforward of a density, is annihilated by an operator with polynomial coefficients. It finds that operator as the null vector of a matrix of weak-form measurements, in which the data are integrated against test functions and never differentiated, makes the coefficients exact, and tests the exact operator on data that the fit did not read against a noise level propagated from the data. It then solves the exact operator symbolically and keeps the closed form that matches the data. Each step has precedent; the combination takes values on an interval, from a quadrature, a float64 table or a sample, to an exact equation and a closed form. On noise-free benchmarks it recovers 45 of 53 classic targets, 108 of the 120 AI Feynman equations and 74.1% of the Feynman problems of SR- Bench, against 33, 69 and 55.8% for the best other programs, and 50 of 132 special-function targets generated after its code was frozen, against 23. From 40-digit values it returns Meijer G densities, hypergeometric forms for the deflection of light by a black hole, and Appell and Lauricella functions of two variables. With noise in several variables, with sparse data and for equations above fourth order it mostly returns no model.
