Predicting Rare-Mode Collapse in Recursive Generative Training from Single-Generation Calibration

arXiv ID: 2611.30897
分类: cs.LG, cs.AI
发布时间: 2026-11-23

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Jingyao Zhang, Mingyu Du, Lu Han
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Recursive generative training can preserve sample counts in a rare region while silently eroding its characteristic core. Mapping this finite-horizon collapse across real-data quotas, rare prevalences, and feedback perturbations via exhaustive multi-generation chains is computationally prohibitive. We develop a decoupled framework that forecasts multi-generation collapse probabilities and locates operating risk boundaries from independent single-generation conditional fits alone. Pairing an exact three-source multinomial count law with single-generation response banks propagates count marginals in closed form, backed by explicit calibration-error and boundary-resolution bounds. For additive Gaussian perturbations, we further prove that equal-variance all-row and sparse policies reverse their boundary-crossing order at a unique hyperplane distance. Across a 2,178-setting count benchmark, exact propagation cuts risk mean absolute error (MAE) from 1.85 to 0.49 percentage points (pp). Across ten two-dimensional learners, calibration achieves 2.60--5.69pp six-round core MAE on nine stationary learners (65--81% below an unperturbed baseline), diagnoses non-stationary outlier drift in standard DDIM (16.38pp) via a stability probe, and resolves the predicted six-round geometric reversal. On Fashion-MNIST latent flow-matching chains, calibration outperforms direct-chain Gaussian-process interpolation at lower fit budgets and tracks the 20% latent-perturbation boundary shift (3.14 versus 10.78~pp MAE for proportion copying) driven by opposing inflow to the rare region and outflow from its core.
