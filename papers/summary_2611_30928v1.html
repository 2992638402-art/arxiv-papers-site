Personalization of a Commercial Powered Knee Prosthesis Under Clinically Realistic Conditions via Reinforcement Learning: A Feasibility Study

arXiv ID: 2611.30928
分类: cs.RO
发布时间: 2026-11-23

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Xinyi Liu, Atli Örn Sverrisson, David Langlois, Ming Liu, Varun Nalam, He Huang
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Personalization of powered prosthesis control to individual user needs remains a significant challenge. To address this challenge, policy iteration with constraint embedded (PICE), a reinforcement learning (RL) tuning approach, has been developed and validated. This feasibility study investigated whether the developed approach can personalize a commercial powered knee prosthesis under clinically realistic conditions. To reduce the time for personalization, tuning was initialized with a policy learned offline from one non-disabled participant walking on a treadmill and was then applied to three other non-disabled participants walking with the knee through a bypass adapter on a 5-meter walkway. All participants reached the goal within 8-18 tuning steps (1.7-4.2 minutes of walking), reducing the knee-kinematic feature error by 64-90% on average. These results support the feasibility of implementing the RL-based tuning approach in clinically realistic conditions with space and equipment constraints.
