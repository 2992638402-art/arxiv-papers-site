ExitLUT: Boolean Early Exits for Efficient FPGA-Based Neural Network Inference

arXiv ID: 2611.31540
分类: cs.AR, cs.LG, stat.ML
发布时间: 2026-11-23

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Oliver Cassidy, Marta Andronic, George A. Constantinides
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Early exit neural networks reduce average inference cost by terminating once sufficient confidence is reached. On FPGAs, conventional confidence-based exits add arithmetic control paths poorly matched to LUT fabrics, consuming resources that could otherwise support the main datapath. We present ExitLUT, which replaces softmax-style exit decisions with learned Boolean functions mapped directly into FPGA LUTs. In our CNN evaluation, fpgaConvNet is the static spatial-dataflow baseline, while ATHEENA is its softmax-based early exit extension. ExitLUT reduces ATHEENA's exit-decision LUT usage by 2.58$\times$ over the softmax path and eliminates decision-path DSP usage, while preserving accuracy and exit behavior. Rerunning design-space exploration reinvests these savings into the datapath, improving throughput by up to 4.08$\times$ over fpgaConvNet and 1.82$\times$ over ATHEENA. For LUT-based NNs, embedded ExitLUT exits achieve 1.41-1.89$\times$ average time-to-decision speedup over static NeuraLUT-Assemble designs with under 1% LUT overhead.
