A Kernel Framework for Linear Operator Equations

arXiv ID: 2611.31626
分类: math.NA, q-fin.CP, stat.ML
发布时间: 2026-11-23

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Damir Filipovic, Valentin Tissot-Daguette
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Solving linear operator equations with nonlocal terms or heterogeneous constraints often requires specific numerical schemes. We develop a unified approach based on embedding such equations into a reproducing kernel Hilbert space (RKHS). Differential, boundary, and integral operators are represented by their Riesz representers in the RKHS. The equations are solved in the regularized least-squares sense at finitely many collocation points. The solution is a finite linear combination of operator-induced representers and is obtained from a single linear system. Its sensitivities, such as financial Greeks, follow by direct differentiation. To reduce the computational cost for large collocation sets, we introduce a Nyström approximation based on subsampling the operator-induced representers. It yields the best approximation of this solution within the reduced trial space, in a regularized empirical norm. Numerical examples cover ordinary, partial, and partial integro-differential equations, including multi-asset and jump-diffusion option pricing.
