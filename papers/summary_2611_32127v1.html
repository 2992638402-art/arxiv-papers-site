EnSurv: Deep Generative Survival Modeling with Application to Kidney Graft Outcomes

arXiv ID: 2611.32127
分类: stat.ML, cs.LG
发布时间: 2026-11-24

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Rajdeep Pathak, Tamalika Koley, Shreyashi Basak, Dipankar Bandyopadhyay, Tanujit Chakraborty
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Kidney transplantation is the optimal treatment for life-threatening end-stage renal disease, providing significant survival advantages over dialysis. Given the severe shortage of donor organs, accurately predicting the time to premature graft failure is an essential clinical task for optimizing organ allocation and tailoring individualized immunosuppressive therapies. This study is motivated by a comprehensive United Network of Organ Sharing (UNOS) dataset containing 243,443 kidney transplant records with complex multi-region structures and high right-censoring rates. We propose EnSurv (survival engression), a deep generative survival framework designed to capture complex, non-linear clinical covariate interactions for modeling time to graft failure. Within a pre-additive noise model (pre-ANM) family, EnSurv optimizes an energy score augmented with pooled Kaplan-Meier tail completion to handle right-censoring. We identify the pooled-completion target of the objective, establish consistency for its best approximation within the pre-ANM family, and give conditions and error bounds for its relation to true conditional graft survival. EnSurv also provides global explainability through summaries of fitted feature weights and incorporates domain constraints through established clinical directions using a soft gradient penalty. Extensive simulation experiments were conducted to evaluate the performance of the proposed method in comparison with existing methods. Application to the UNOS cohort demonstrates that EnSurv effectively models individual and region-level effects, consistently outperforming baselines across evaluation metrics encompassing discrimination, calibration, and accuracy.
