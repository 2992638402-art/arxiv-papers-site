Support2Sim: Visually Faithful Real2Sim with Stable Object Supports

arXiv ID: 2611.32727
分类: cs.RO, cs.CV
发布时间: 2026-11-24

📄 论文信息

  • 作者: Samarth Chopra, Ankit Prabhu, Akshayan Nagarajah, Sumer Murthy, Pratik Chaudhari, Vijay Kumar
  • PDF: 下载
  • arXiv 链接: 查看

📝 摘要

Reconstructing a scene for physics simulation requires recovering its spatial layout and the support relationships that govern its behavior. We present Support2Sim, a Real2Sim system that reconstructs simulation-ready scenes from a single RGB image. Our design objective is to maximize source fidelity, support realization, and stability within a practical time budget for scene generation. Support2Sim infers a support graph, which a contact-guided pose initializer uses to propose poses for objects with multiple supports. The initializer generates and ranks candidates by geometric fit and source-image agreement. A coarse-to-fine refiner allocates a fixed search budget across supported objects and evaluates pose updates with physics rollouts. After an accepted update, it searches from the new pose at a finer translation and rotation scale. We benchmark our method on 50 scenes spanning four datasets. Compared with the strongest baseline, Support2Sim improves source-mask IoU after 10 seconds by 21.4 pp, realizes 87.1% versus 73.2% of intended supports, and reduces mean object drift from 79.6 to 45.3 mm. See our project page at https://support2sim.github.io for more details.
