Field Life: Particle Life as Interacting Chemical Fields

分类: nlin.CG, cs.AI, cs.NE
发布时间: 2026-11-24

  • 作者: Iliya Zhechev
  • PDF: 下载

📝 摘要

Field Life is a cellular automaton inspired by the interaction dynamics of Particle Life. In Particle Life, each particle has a colour, and an interaction matrix sets how strongly each colour attracts or repels each other colour. In Field Life, each colour is a channel of the automaton, and each cell holds a concentration of every colour. One step of the rule has three parts. Each colour is convolved with a ring-shaped kernel, which measures how much of that colour surrounds a cell. The interaction matrix combines these measurements into an attraction for each colour. The mass of each colour then moves toward higher attraction, in a way that conserves mass. A random matrix and a random initial state are usually enough to produce moving structure: bodies that glide, bodies that shed diffuse clouds, and gliders that break up into smaller gliders. We sample rules at random, keep the rules that move and form structure, and describe the patterns that appear: colours that sort into regions, colours that form stripes, and gliders, compact bodies that travel across the grid. The simulation and 120 saved rules, which we call worlds, are released.
